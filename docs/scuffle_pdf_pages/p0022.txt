Scuffle — Arcade Game g Design Document (a Stick Fight game)
• Sticks: a yelp per hit (pitched per player color), a grunt per punch, a scream per fall, a
squeak when frozen, a sizzle when burning, a gurgle underwater, a pop when a head
comes off. Taunts have a voice each.
• Weapons: every weapon has a distinct fire, hit, pickup, and empty sound; the minigun's
spin-up, the black hole's pull (the mix bends into it), the bubble gun's giggle, the snake
gun's hiss, the confetti cannon's party.
• Stages: the engine room's pistons on a beat, the cave's drips and the slipstream's rush,
the reef's surge channel on a count, the plaza's tuna whoosh, the Void's silence and the
worm's rumble, the Salon's crowd and jukebox. Hazards have tells you can hear a half
second before they fire.
• The wall: each world's wall has a rising sound (water, grinding rock, the tide, the city's
groan, the abyss's tone, the bouncer's whistle).
• Events: al s tell each (a splash, a tentacle's slap, a fuse, a thunderclap, a bark).
• Rounds: a countdown tick, a round-win sting, the replay's slow-motion low-pass, the
match-win fanfare.
• Music: a fast brass-and-drum loop per world that drops out during the wall and comes
back as a riff on the win; Boss Arena has the boss's theme from the expeditions; the
Gauntlet has a ticking clock under its loop.
