Overview
Scuffle is Depth's Stick Fight: a 2D physics brawler for 2-8 players in which stick figures
fight on small, dangerous stages with whatever falls from the sky, and the last one
standing wins the round. It keeps everything that makes Stick Fight work (one-screen
stages, ragdoll bodies, weapons that drop in crates, rounds that last thirty seconds, levels
that try to kill you) and adds the things a group that plays it for a year wants: more players,
more worlds, a level editor with sharing, trinkets that make each stick a little different,
mutators, mid-round events, objective modes, co-op boss arenas, and a gauntlet.
What it keeps from Stick Fight
• Stick figures with full ragdoll physics: every death is a tumble, every hit is a flail, every
bullet moves a body.
One-screen stages that are the real opponent: lava, saws, spikes, snakes, collapsing
floors, black holes.
• Weapon crates from the sky at a timer; everyone starts with fists.
• Fists that punch, block bullets with a timed punch, and knock people into hazards.
• Rounds of about thirty seconds; first to a score wins the match; the stage changes
every round.
• Chaos as the design: the balance is that nothing is balanced for long.
What Scuffle adds
1. Eight players on bigger stages, with camera zoom that follows the pack.
2. Six worlds from Depth (the Nautilus, the Cave, the Reef, Atlantis, the Void, the Salon)
with their own hazards, and a stage generator alongside hand-built stages.
3. Trinkets: one small perk each stick picks per match (a double jump, a thick skull, a
lucky crate).
4. Mutators: lobby rules that change the physics or the arsenal for a match (low gravity,
bullets bounce, everything's a snake).
5. Events: mid-round surprises (the floor floods, the kraken reaches up, the lights go out).
