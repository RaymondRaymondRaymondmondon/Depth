Tokens: IO per match, 1 per 50 score, 20 per match won, 25 per first story. The leaderboard
shows round wins; the end-of-match screen shows the kill feed's best moments replayed
(a 10 s ragdoll replay of the round's most physics).
Pace
A 10-round match with 6 players runs 12-15 minutes with the wall; an 8-player match has
more draws and more laughter. The design target is a round every 40 s on average.
Weapons
Forty-eight weapons in crates, all physics objects, all with a wrong use. They're Depth's
arsenal with triggers: gas pistols and harpoons from Red Tide, the Nautilus's brass, the
Flats dealer's bottles, and a few things nobody asked for. Weights drop by world so every
match's arsenal leans a different way.
