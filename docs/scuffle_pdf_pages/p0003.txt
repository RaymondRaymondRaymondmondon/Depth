There is no double jump by default (a trinket gives it). Knockback is real: a shotgun blast is
6 m of flight; a punch is 1.5 m; falling 10 m stuns for 0.5 s.
Fists
• Punch (fire with no weapon): a jab with the lead arm, 10 damage, 1.5 m knockback, 0.25
s. Three in a row is a haymaker (20, 3 m).
• Block: a punch thrown at a bullet in the 0.15 s before it lands deflects it (it flies off at
the punch's angle). Blocking a rocket deflects the rocket. Blocking is the skill ceiling
and the reason unarmed is never hopeless.
• Grab (hold fire while touching): hold a stick or a crate; throw it with a direction. A
thrown stick is a projectile (15 damage to both). Grabbing a weapon's owner can pull
the weapon loose.
• Kick (fire while diving): 15, 4 m knockback, the thing you do to someone standing on a
ledge.
Health
100 HP; most bullets do 25-40; a melee weapon 30-50; hazards kill outright. No
regeneration during a round. Low health shows as a wobble and a red tint on the stick;
dead sticks are ragdolls that stay on the stage as obstacles and can be thrown.
Weapons in hand
• You hold one weapon; picking up a second drops the first. Weapons have ammo and are
dropped empty (they're still a thrown object: 10 damage).
• Aim is a free 360-degree arm; recoil moves the body (a minigun pushes you backward;
a sniper rocks you).
• Weapons are bodies too: a dropped shotgun falls, slides, and can be shot off a ledge.
