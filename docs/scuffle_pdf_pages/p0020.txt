Presentation, physics, technology, and netcode
Look
• Depth's ink in 2D: stick figures are three-pixel black lines with round joints on
parchment-toned stages; the worlds are drawn in the expedition backgrounds' style
(inked, hatched, a little painterly) with the stage's physics pieces outlined heavier so
what's solid is obvious.
• Deaths are ink: a stick that dies goes limp and grey; blood is an ink splash; fire is ink
that curls; ice is a white hatch.
• Weapons are drawn as Depth's relics: the harpoon rifle from Red Tide, the frying pan
from the Gull, the Tesla gaff.
The camera frames all living sticks with a minimum and maximum zoom; replays use a
free camera.
