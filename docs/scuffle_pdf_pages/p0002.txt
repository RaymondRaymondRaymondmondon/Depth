Design pillars
1. Thirty seconds of panic, then laughter. Rounds are short; the stage is the joke; death
is a punchline, never a penalty.
2. Physics over stats. Everything is a body with mass. A punch moves you; a shotgun
moves you more; a black hole moves everyone.
3. Easy to join, impossible to finish learning. Two buttons and a stick play it; the ninetieth
round still surprises.
4. Depth's world, Depth's ink. Stick figures drawn in the ink style, stages from the game's
locations, weapons that are Depth's relics with a trigger.
Session. A match is first to 10 rounds (lobby: 5 / 10 / 20), 10-20 minutes; 2-8 players, bots
fill. Score pays arcade tokens; tokens buy skins and hats or spin the crate.
Tone. Fast, loud, inked. The stick figures have one face (two dots and a line) and it conveys
everything.
The feel
Everything in Scuffle is a physics body, including you. The stick figure is an active ragdoll: a
torso, a head, two arms, two legs, driven by motors toward a target pose while you're alive
and limp the moment you aren't. The movement code's job is to make a body that's always
slightly out of control feel exactly controllable.
