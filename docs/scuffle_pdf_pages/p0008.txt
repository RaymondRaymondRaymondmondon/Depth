Crates fall every 5 s at random crate zones, on parachutes (shoot the parachute and
the crate drops faster; it crushes a stick it lands on for 30).
• Weights by world (the Reef drops more chum cannons and snakes; the Void drops
more black holes and gravity guns; the Salon drops more frying pans and fish). A
match's arsenal can also be chosen in the lobby: Classic (guns), Melee only, Chaos (the
strange ones), Snakes (it's all snakes), Random.
One crate in ten is gear (next section) and one in thirty is a trap (a snake, a bee hive, a
flashbang that goes off on open).
• A crate can be punched across the stage, shot (it opens where it lands), or grabbed
and thrown as a weapon (20).
