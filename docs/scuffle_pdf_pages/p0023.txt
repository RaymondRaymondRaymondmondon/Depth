All targets met or logged
--scuffle-sim <players> <rounds> <arsenal> runs bots and reports: round length,
kills by weapon, deaths by hazard, draws, and the wall's share of deaths.
--scuffle-arsenal fights every weapon against every other on three stage shapes
(open, corridor, vertical) and asserts every weapon has a shape it wins on.
--scuffle-verify <stage code> runs the reachability check; --scuffle-verify-
all on the 240 built-ins.
--scuffle-determinism <seed> runs the same inputs twice and asserts identical
physics.
Balance targets
• Round length 25-45 s with four players; 30-60 with eight; draws under 8%.
• Deaths: weapons 55%, hazards 30%, fists and throws 10%, the wall 5%.
• No weapon over 12% of kills across a 100-round sample on Random arsenal.
