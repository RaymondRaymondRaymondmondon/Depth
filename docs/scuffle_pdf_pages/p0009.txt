Weapon balance
The rule is that every weapon wins a one-on-one in some situation and loses in another:
the sniper owns long sightlines and loses in a corridor; the scatter gun is the reverse; the
minigun wins if it starts firing first and is useless if punched; the black hole wins
everything once and then there's a black hole. Weights, damage, and ammo are in
scuffle _ weapons . json and are the first thing a balance pass touches.
Levels
A stage is one screen (two for eight players, with the camera zooming to fit the living
sticks), built from tiles that are physics bodies: platforms, ropes, moving pieces, breakable
pieces, and hazards. Six worlds, 40 hand-built stages each, a generator for endless ones,
and the editor for everyone else's.
