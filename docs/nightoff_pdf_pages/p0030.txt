Balance targets
• A careful player walks home with 300-500 and two kidneys about half the time.
• A reckless player loses a kidney about one night in five and gets arrested one in six.
• The average night has 2.4 events, 3 fights, 1.5 go-homes, and one headline nobody
expected.

The second bar: the Brass Monkey
The lobby offers two bars. The Sodden Gull is the dockside pub; the Brass Monkey is the
uptown place across the harbor: a former gentlemen's club gone to seed, with a card room
that takes itself seriously, a rooftop instead of a yard, and a crowd that dresses better and
fights dirtier. Most patrons belong to one bar; a few move between them, and what they
remember travels with them.
