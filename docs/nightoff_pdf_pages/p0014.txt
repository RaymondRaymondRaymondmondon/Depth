For the generator's extras the odds are the same with the kidney at 3%. The twist: the
kidney thieves are the best flirts in the bar. Dottie Finch and Jasper Coil succeed on offers
more than anyone, and their lines are the funniest. The sober player sees the glance at the
window; the drunk player sees the funniest person in the room.
Avoiding the bathtub
• Ask the bartender: for a drink he'll tell you who's "trouble" tonight (one true name, one
false).
• The fortune teller's reading names "a smile with a cooler" if you're about to leave with
one.
• Go home with a teammate watching: a player who follows within 30 s can interrupt a
bad night at the door (a short brawl in the alley; the thieves run).
• Carry the dog. Nobody steals a kidney in front of a dog.
Group outcomes
Two players can leave with the Twins (one shared morning line, both score). A player who
leaves with someone another player was flirting with starts a feud with that player (the
morning headline mentions it, and the next night's bartender does too).
