A Night Off — Arcade Game 6 Design Document
Session. One night, 25-40 minutes real time, on a clock from 7 p.m. to 3 a.m. (or until every
player is asleep, arrested, hospitalized, or gone home). Players can leave early, which ends
their night and starts their morning.
Relationship to the other arcade games. It reuses Red Tide's 3D renderer, inked low-poly
style, rig and Chain animation, and the shared networking layer, plus Flats' card and
physics code for the poker and bullshit tables and Scuttle's bot seats. It does not share
any design with them: it's a social sandbox, not a shooter.
The night
A night runs on a clock from 7 p.m. to 3 a.m. at about four real minutes per game hour. The
clock drives the crowd, the events, the prices, and the patrons' moods, so the same bar is
a different place at 8, at midnight, and at 2.
