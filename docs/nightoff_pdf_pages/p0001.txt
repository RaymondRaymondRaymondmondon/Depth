Overview
A Night Off is a 1-6 player third-person 3D comedy sandbox set in a dockside bar called
the Sodden Gull. The crew of the Nautilus have one night ashore. They drink, flirt, play
darts and pool and poker, lie to each other at the bullshit table, get their fortunes told,
start fights or avoid them, and try to make it to the morning with their money, their dignity,
and both kidneys. Nothing in it is serious; everything in it has consequences, and the
consequences are the jokes.
Design pillars
1. A night is a story you tell afterward. The game's goal is the story at breakfast: who
went home with the fortune teller, who lost a kidney, who won the bar in a poker hand,
who got arrested. The morning-after screen is the real scoreboard.
2. Drinking is the dial. Every drink trades charisma for toughness. Sober, you talk your
way through the night; drunk, you fight your way through it; too drunk, you lose your
night to a blackout and someone else narrates it.
3. The bar is alive and it changes. Patrons come and go, the crowd swells and thins,
events walk in the door, and the room itself gets messier, louder, and stranger as the
hours pass.
4. Everyone has a personality and a memory. Thirty regulars with traits, moods, and
grudges that last the night and, for some, across nights.
5. Silly with friends. Mechanics are simple to pick up, deep enough to scheme with, and
built for six people shouting at each other.
Tone. Brass-and-gaslight dockside, the same inked style as Depth, played for laughs: a
drunk Mechanic trying to line up a dart, a Siren reading tarot in the corner, a biker gang
that turns out to be a book club. Violence is cartoon bar-brawl: black eyes, broken bottles,
people thrown over tables and waking up in the alley. Romance is flirting, a walk out the
door, and a fade to black with a morning-after line. The kidney thing is a comedy
consequence, not horror: you wake in a bathtub of ice with a note and a stitch, and for the
rest of the game you get drunk twice as fast.
