A Night Off — Arcade Game 6 Design Document
Mood
Mood runs from Hostile to Delighted and moves with: drinks bought (+), being ignored (-,
for Talkers), being flirted with (depends on type and your charisma), insults (
being
beaten at a game (-, bad losers --), their drink being spilled (-), a round bought (+), the
band (+ for most, - for Brooders), and the hour (everyone's mood loosens after 10 p.m.
and sours after 1 a.m.). Mood decides what they'll do: Delighted patrons pay for your
drinks, tell secrets, and take your side in fights; Hostile ones start them.
Conversation
Talking is a short mini-game: three to five exchanges with four options each (ask, agree,
joke, challenge), each rolled against charisma and the patron's traits. Captions show what
you said; at high drunk levels, what you said is not what you picked. Conversations give
information (who's the thief, which hustler cheats, where the robbery's safe is), buffs (a
laugh is +5 charisma for 5 minutes), items (a lucky coin, a key), and sometimes a fight.
