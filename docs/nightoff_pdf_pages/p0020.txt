A Night Off — Arcade Game 6 Design Document
The bartender, the Gull
The bartender is the bar's referee: honest, SUSPiCiOUS, and in love with Big Ruth. He has a
mood like everyone else, and it is the most important mood in the room.
• Delighted: cheaper drinks, warnings about trouble (who's a thief, when the police are
coming), and he looks away when you take the shotgun.
• Neutral: the menu at list price.
• Annoyed: prices up 20%, cuts you off at 60 drunk, and tells the police which of you
started it.
• Hostile: bars you. One more incident and you're on the pavement.
What moves him: paying as you go (+), rounds (+), breaking things (-), fighting (-), going
behind the counter (--), flirting with Big Ruth (---), buying Big Ruth a drink for him to
give her (+++), and the hour (he's tired by 1 a.m.).
He also sells: the cigar cabinet's key (a cigar is +5 charisma with Gamblers), a cab home
(50, the only guaranteed safe exit after 1 a.m.), and, if Delighted, the name of the one slot
machine that isn't rigged.
Debt
A player at zero can borrow 100 from Harrow at the poker table. Harrow borrows it from
the cartel, which means that at the cartel event the player is on their list. Debt at the end
of the night is -5 score per 10 unpaid and, with a profile, a visit next night.
Events and the crowd
Events walk in the front door. Two or three happen per night from the schedule below,
rolled against the hour and the crowd, and each one changes what the bar is for an hour.
They are the night's set pieces, and they're all survivable, profitable, and funny if you read
them right.
