A Night Off — Arcade Game 6 Design Document
Patrons who travel
Five patrons move between the bars (Boxer Mags, Captain Vane, Sweet Wren,
Bartholomew Crane, and Dottie Finch, who thieves wherever the coats are better). A
traveler who's at the Monkey tonight isn't at the Gull, and their memory of a player is the
same in both rooms. The ferry between the bars runs every game hour; a player can
change bars mid-night (15 minutes of game time, the ferryman charges IO, and anything
left on a tab follows you).
Which bar
The lobby picks the bar per night; the arcade profile tracks both bartenders' memories.
The Gull is the louder, cheaper, fightier night; the Monkey is the richer, sneakier one,
where the card room and the kidney thief are the dangers and the library is a refuge.
Seasonal nights
Some nights one of the two bars is hosting something. The lobby shows what's on where;
players choose the bar with the event or the bar without it, so a group that wants a quiet
poker night can take the other ferry. Seasonal nights change the crowd, the prices, the
events that can roll, and the morning headline's flavor. They rotate on a real calendar (one
is always available; which one depends on the date) and the host can force any of them.
