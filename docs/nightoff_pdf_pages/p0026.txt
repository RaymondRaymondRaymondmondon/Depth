Dialogue
All dialogue is text with formant-synth gibberish voices (each patron a pitch and rhythm),
captioned in the ink style. The conversation and flirt mini-games are a four-option wheel;
drunk captions substitute the spoken line after the choice. Dialogue lines are data
( nightoff_dialogue . j son ): about 40 lines per regular, 10 per generic patron, grouped by
situation and trait.
Simulation
The bar is a small agent simulation: each patron has a schedule (arrive, drink, play, talk,
leave), a mood, a target (a game, a person, a stool), and a memory of each player. About 40
agents at full rate; events script their arrivals and goals on top. The host runs it; clients
receive agent positions, animation states, and mood faces.
Networking
Host-authoritative through the shared layer: player movement predicted locally;
conversations, games, bets, and fights resolved on the host; dice rolls are host-side and
logged for the morning screen's story generator. Bandwidth is small (a few dozen agents,
no projectiles beyond darts and balls).
Save and profile
The arcade profile stores: nights played, headlines earned, kidneys lost and won, the
bartender's memory of you, feuds and friends among the regulars, and cosmetics.
Nothing in it changes a night's numbers except the hangover and the bartender's prices.
Sound design
The Gull is heard before it's seen: the clink of glasses, thirty gibberish voices at once, the
jukebox, the rain. Everything is synthesized through Depth's cue registry. The sound's job
is to make the room feel crowded or empty, to make drunkenness audible, and to give
every event a tell you hear from the door.
