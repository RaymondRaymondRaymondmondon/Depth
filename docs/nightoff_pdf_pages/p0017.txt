A Night Off — Arcade Game 6 Design Document
After a fight
The loser is on the floor; the winner's charisma is +10 for 5 minutes with violent and loud
patrons and -20 with everyone else; the damage goes on the tab of whoever started it
(the bartender's judgment, which favors whoever he likes). A fight during a police
inspection is an arrest. A fight you win while sober is the hardest story in the game.
Bar games
Eight games, all playable against players or patrons, all for money, all affected by the drunk
meter in their own way. Each has a hustler who is better than you and a tell that gives
them away.
