A Night Off — Arcade Game 6 Design Document
• Patrons react to events by trait: cowardly ones leave when the police or the robbers
arrive; violent ones join the brawl; superstitious ones do what the fortune teller last
said; generous ones buy a round when the band plays.
• Leaving: at their leave hour, or when Hostile and nobody's fighting them, or when
they've gone home with someone, or in pairs with whoever they flirted with
successfully (patrons flirt with each other too; the room pairs off after midnight).
The fortune deck (22 cards)
The Diver, the Helm, the Periscope, the Lantern, the Kraken, the Drowned, the Siren, the
Island, the Cave, the Weeds, the Sunken City, the Abyss, the Bottle, the Coin, the Knife,
the Key, the Dog, the Goat, the Gull, the Stitch, the Bathtub, the Morning. A reading is
three cards: who you'll meet, what you'll do, how it ends. The reading is generated from
the night's actual seeded schedule (which events will fire, which thief is in, which patron
will flirt with you), so it's true: the Stitch and the Bathtub together mean a thief will make
an offer; the Key means the safe is open tonight; the Dog means feed it; the Morning
drawn third means you'll walk home. The fortune teller reads a player once per night; a
second reading is a lie she tells for fun.
The headline and story generator
The morning screen builds from a log of tagged moments (every go-home, kidney, fight,
win, event, and cartel purchase, with who and when). Headlines are templates ranked by
rarity: the rarest moment of the night leads (a sober shotgun on the cartel beats a lost
kidney beats a hole-in-one beats a go-home). Templates are in nightoff_scoring.json ,
about 80 of them, with slots for names, numbers, and the goat. The morning line per player
is chosen the same way from that player's log. Two players who did something together
share a line.
The dog
The alley dog has a loyalty meter per player (fed once: follows for 5 minutes; twice: follows
all night; three times: fights for you and sleeps outside your door, which stops the kidney
thieves). It can be fed chips, stew, or a hustler's winnings (it eats money). Only one player
can own it at a time; feeding it more than the current owner steals it, which is a feud. The
dog's name is whatever the first player to feed it types.
