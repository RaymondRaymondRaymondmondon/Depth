A Night Off — Arcade Game 6 Design Document
Fighting and weapons
Fights are cartoon brawls: punches, grabs, throws, and whatever's in reach. Nobody dies
from a punch. Weapons escalate what a fight costs, and the bar, the police, and the cartel
all notice.
The brawl system
• HP 100, scaled by toughness (so a wrecked player has 200 and takes half damage, and
a sober one hits for less and feels all of it).
• Moves: jab (quick, 10), haymaker (slow, 25, a windup you can see), grab (hold 1.5 s, then
throw into a table or over the bar), shove, block (halves the next hit), dodge (at low
drunk only: above 60, dodging is falling over).
• Drunk aiming. Above 40, your swings drift by the same curve as darts; a haymaker at
80 drunk might hit the wrong person, which starts a second fight.
• Environment. Tables flip (cover and a thrown-through surface), chairs are thrown, the
bar top is a stage, stairs are a fall. A patron thrown through a window is out for the
night and the bartender adds the window to your tab.
• Knockout. At O HP you're on the floor for 30 s; a teammate can drag you to the toilets.
Three knockouts in a night and the bartender bars you.
• Crowds. A fight pulls in anyone within 3 m with a violent trait and anyone Delighted
with either side. On a packed night a one-on-one becomes a room in under a minute.
Sister Ash ends fights by walking into them.
Who fights
Brooders antagonized past tolerance; Sailors of a rival crew at midnight; jealous partners;
anyone whose drink you spilled twice; the biker gang if the piano is touched; the
bartender if you go behind the counter; the cook if you enter the kitchen without buying
food; a hustler you caught cheating (they swing first); and any patron a teammate has
made Hostile and pointed at you.
