A Night Off — Arcade Game 6 Design Document
Seasonal nights never remove the base game: the games, the patrons, and the morning
after are all there. They add one layer and change the odds.
The cartel's wares
The cartel sells as well as collects. From the moment they arrive (and at the Monkey's
cellar any time after 11), the quiet man's men will sell a player anything on the list for
money or a favor. Every substance has an effect that is funny, useful, or disastrous, and
most are two of those. They stack with drink.
