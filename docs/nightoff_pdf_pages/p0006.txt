A Night Off — Arcade Game 6 Design Document
• Mess. Every drink leaves a glass; every fight leaves broken furniture; byl a.m. a busy
night is a floor of glass and overturned chairs the cook sweeps grudgingly. Broken
bottles on the floor are weapons for anyone who picks them up.
Light and sound. The lamps dim as the night goes on; the jukebox gets louder; after
midnight someone always puts on the same sad song, and the bar sings it.
• Damage. Smashed windows stay smashed; a broken pool cue means one less cue; if
the piano is thrown through the window during the biker gang event, there's no piano.
The bartender's mood tracks the damage and his prices follow it.
• Weather. Rain starts at a random hour; the yard clears, the golf balls float, and the
smokers come inside.
• The dog. The alley dog can be fed, befriended, brought inside, and will fight for
whoever feeds it most. It is the single most effective ally in the game.
• Persistence across nights (optional profile). The bartender remembers a player who
paid their tab, a player who didn't, and a player who shot his window. The regulars
remember who fought them. It's a short memory (three nights) and always reset by
buying a round.
Drinking
Every player has a drunk meter from O to 100. Drinks raise it, time and food lower it, and
every number in the game reads it. The trade is always the same: charisma down,
toughness up.
