Flirting and going home
Flirting is a conversation with stakes: three rounds, a charisma roll against the patron's
interest, and an offer at the end that you can take or walk away from. What happens after
the door closes depends on who they were, and you usually don't know until the morning.
The flirt mini-game
1. Open. Choose a line (compliment, joke, buy a drink, dance invitation). Roll charisma +
the line's fit against the patron's traits. A romantic patron wants a compliment; a flirty
one wants a joke; a SUSPiCiOUS one wants a drink first; a loud one wants to dance.
2. Build. Two more exchanges with four options each. Each SUCCess raises interest by
one; each failure drops it. Three successes make the offer; two failures end it. Drunk
captions apply: at 60+ drunk, what you say is a surprise.
3. The offer. "Walk me home?" or "My place is upstairs." Take it and your night ends in a
fade; decline and you keep a friend (+mood, they'll back you in a fight).
4. Signals. Each type has a tell that a sober player can read: a kidney thief glances at the
toilets' window; a married patron turns a ring; a sincere one laughs at your worst joke.
Absinthe or a fortune shows trait icons outright.
Interest is modified by the room: a packed bar after IO p.m. lowers every patron's
threshold by 1; a round bought raises it; a fight you won within sight raises it for violent
and loud patrons and ruins it for the gentle ones.
