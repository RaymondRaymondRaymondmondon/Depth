Traits (three each)
Friendly, SUSPiCiOUS, lonely, loud, generous, broke, jealous, cowardly, violent, honest, liar,
drunkard, teetotal, superstitious, romantic, grieving, hungry, flirty, married, in love with
the bartender, afraid of the cook, hates sailors, loves sailors, owes money, carries a knife,
carries a kidney cooler (hidden), has a tell, bad loser, good loser, cheat.
Traits set the dialogue options, the roll thresholds, the reaction to drinks bought, and the
fight stats. A liar's stories don't match the fortune teller's reading; a superstitious patron
will do anything a fortune says; a jealous patron fights whoever flirts with their partner.
