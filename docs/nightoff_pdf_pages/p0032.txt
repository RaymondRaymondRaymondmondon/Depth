The Monkey's drinks
Celeste serves cocktails: the Harbor Light (+18, +10% charisma for 3 minutes), the Sidecar
(+20), the Marlin (+30, a snooker buff: cue wobble halved for one frame), the Widow's
Kiss (+40, honest captions and a tell: you see every patron's trait icons for 2 minutes), and
Champagne by the bottle (+25 and a round for the room, 60). Beer is available and Celeste
says something about it.
