Systems in depth
The numbers and rules Claude Code needs to build each system without guessing.
Conversation math
• A conversation is 3-5 exchanges. Each exchange presents four options (ask, agree,
joke, challenge). Each option has a base difficulty (ask 40, agree 30, joke 50, challenge
60) adjusted by the patron's traits (a friendly patron: all -10; suspicious: ask +15;
honest: agree -10, challenge -10; loud: joke -15; grieving: joke +30; liar: ask +10).
• Roll = dlOO + charisma modifier (charisma% - 100, so Merry is +10 and Wrecked is -80)
+ mood modifier (Delighted +20, Annoyed -15, Hostile -40) + noise modifier (-10 at
peak noise unless within 1 m).
• Success if roll difficulty. Three successes end the conversation well (information, a
buff, an item, +mood); two failures end it badly (-mood, a fight if the patron is violent
and the last option was a challenge).
• At drunk 60+, each option has a 25% chance to be replaced at the moment of speaking
by a random option; at 80+, 50%, and the replacement is always the most insulting one
available. The caption shows what came out.
• Listening (Talkers): a passive option that always succeeds and costs time; every 30 s of
listening is +2 drunk (you keep sipping) and +1 patron mood.
Flirt math
• Interest starts at O; the offer comes at 3; the flirt ends at -2. Each exchange: roll as
above against the patron's threshold (romantic 45, flirty 35, lonely 40, SUSPiCiOUS 60,
married 70, teetotal 65, in-love-with-someone-else 80), adjusted by the line's fit (the
right opener -15, the wrong one +15) and the room (packed after 10 p.m. -5, a round
bought -10, you won a fight in sight: violent/loud -15, others +20, you lost one:
everyone +10).
