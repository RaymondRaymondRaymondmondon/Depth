A Night Off — Arcade Game 6 Design Document
Fights
Cartoon punches (a soft thud with a slide whistle at Wrecked), the bottle smash, the cue
crack, a chair through a table, a window, the shotgun's single enormous boom and the
silence after it.
Events (each heard before seen)
The bachelor party's cheer from the street; the bachelorette's whistles; the bikers'
engines in the yard (and, a minute later, a polite discussion of chapter nine); the police's
knock and the inspector's whistle; the robbery's kitchen door and a shout; the cartel's
quiet (the murmur drops when they walk in); the rival crew's shanty; the lock-in's bolt; the
wake's organ; the band tuning; the goat.
Music
The jukebox is the music. When it's silent, a low piano from the stage noodles by itself (the
ghost of the Widow Tusk's husband, if you ask her). The morning screen plays a hungover
waltz.
