What's different from the Gull
YOU can be refused entry for
being Wrecked or barred; a bribe
(30) or a good coat (a skin) gets
you in
Cocktails are expensive and
precise (see Drinks below);
Celeste remembers everything
and forgives nothing
Cheating is harder (the croupier
watches); the high-stakes table is
where the cartel's banker sits
The quiet room: conversations
only, no games, no fights
(Celeste's rule: a fight in the
library is an instant bar)
Snooker instead of 8-ball: longer
games, bigger money, a hustler
who's a retired champion

longer; the police come up the fire
stairs
The robbery event's target here;
