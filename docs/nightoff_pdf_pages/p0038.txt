and 20 for a first on each seasonal night. A good night is 25-40 tokens.

One token, one spin, from a brass wheel in the cloakroom. A duplicate is a duplicate (the
wheel laughs; nothing is refunded). Odds: Common 60% (15 skins, 4% each), Rare 25% (IO
skins, 2.5% each), Super Rare 12% (IO skins, 1.2% each), Special 3% (5 skins, 0.6% each).
Expected cost to own all 40 is about 290 spins, which is a long way to a Special and the
reason to keep playing.
