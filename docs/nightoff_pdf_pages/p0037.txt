Rules: one dose of each per night; nothing is sold to a player above 80 drunk (the quiet
man has standards); a player seen buying by Sergeant Mallory or during a police
inspection is searched; a dose a player didn't buy can be slipped into their drink by
another player (the Siren and the Cocktail are the USUal choices, and it's the game's
dirtiest trick). The cartel never sells to the kidney thieves. They're competitors.
Skins and the token spin
A Night Off's arcade tokens buy skins, and nothing else. There are 50 skins: 10 that any
player can save up for, and 40 that come only from the spin. Skins are cosmetic and visible
to everyone, which in this game is the whole point.
