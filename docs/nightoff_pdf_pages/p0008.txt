Time lowers the meter by 1 per game minute (about 15 per real minute). Prices rise 20% at
10 p.m. and double at last call. Rounds (buying for everyone within 5 m) cost the sum but
add +10 charisma with each recipient for 10 minutes.
Hangovers and the next night
If the profile persists, a night that ended above 60 drunk gives the next night a hangover:
start at -10% charisma for the first game hour and a sensitivity to the jukebox.
The kidney
If a player wakes without a kidney (see Flirting, the Cartel, the Robbery), every drink's
drunk value is doubled for the rest of the night and, with a persistent profile, for two
nights after. The morning screen shows the stitch. There is exactly one way to get a kidney
back: win one in the poker game's high-stakes pot, which the cartel sometimes puts up.
Doing so is worth 200 score and a headline.
