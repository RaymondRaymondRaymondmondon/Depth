A Night Off — Arcade Game 6 Design Document
The band and the jukebox
Not games, but the dance floor has a rhythm mini-game (hit the beats) that gives +15
charisma for 5 minutes to anyone who finishes a song. Drunk players dance badly and are
loved for it (+5 regardless).
Money and the bartender
Every player walks in with 200 in wages. Money buys drinks, games, bribes, and favors; it's
also what the night takes from you. The scoreboard counts what you walk out with over
what you walked in with, so a player who spends nothing and leaves at 8 p.m. scores zero
on money and zero on everything else.

Drinks go on a tab, not paid per drink. The bartender closes tabs at last call and at the
door. A player who leaves without paying is remembered (profile) and the bouncer bars
them next night until they pay double. A tab can be paid by a friend, a Delighted patron, or
by winning it back from the bartender at darts (he plays one game a night and is very
good).
