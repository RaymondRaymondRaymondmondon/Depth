Every game can be cheated (a marked card, a weighted dart, nudging the slot machine,
moving your ball) by holding the cheat key during the action. Cheating succeeds on a
charisma-plus-sobriety roll; a failure is caught by whoever's watching, which means a fight
with a bad loser, the bartender's judgment, or the whole table turning on you. A caught
cheat at poker is a story and a feud.
Side bets
Any player or patron can bet on any game in progress by walking up and talking to a player
at the table. Side bets are the way a player with no money left stays in the night.
