Any island with a nest site can be colonized: the Founder (or a Pathfinder) builds a nest, a
mate is called, two chicks are moved, and the island gets its own caches. An island is held
by whoever has the most nests on it; two players can share one until they don't.
Dangerous islands give their prize to whoever holds them and their danger to everyone.
Weather on islands
Storms ground birds for a game hour, raise the sea (the atoll floods; the stack doesn't),
and empty the sky of SCOUtS; fog hides raids; the dry season halves tropical twigs; the
kraken surfaces in storms.
The living sea
The ocean is Red Tide's ecosystem engine running under an RTS: species with diets, blood
and scent, sound, territory, and population, simulated by the host. Birds are members of
the web: they eat fish, and some things eat them. The sea is why fishing is never free and
why a war's blood matters.
