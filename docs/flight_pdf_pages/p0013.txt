The Flight — Arcade Game 7 Design Document
Flocks
Warriors are commanded in flocks of 2-12 with a leader (the Founder or a Flockmaster). A
flock has a formation (see War), a target, an altitude order, and a stance (raid, hold,
escort, retreat-when-half). Birds outside a flock fight as individuals and lose.
Upkeep
Every bird is a mouth. Warriors eat a fisher. A colony of 15 fishers can feed about 8
warriors at rest and 5 on campaign (flying burns feed). A standing army starves a colony;
armies are raised for a war and demobilized to fishing after it, which is the rhythm the
game teaches.
Islands
Every island is a different economy and a different defense. Starting islands are safe and
fair; the dangerous ones are the map's center and its best ground, if a player can hold
them.
