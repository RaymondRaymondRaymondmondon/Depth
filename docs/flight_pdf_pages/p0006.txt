The Flight — Arcade Game 7 Design Document
Starvation
When feed runs out, chicks die first, then the oldest adults, one per hour each, while the
colony panel flashes. A starving colony's warriors desert (they go fishing for themselves)
and its fervour collapses. A siege works by making this happen.
Second colonies
The Founder (or a Pathfinder bird, late) lands on another island and builds a nest; one
mate and two chicks moved there become a colony with its own caches and panel.
Colonies share research and the player's orders; they do not share feed unless a feed line
(Feeders assigned between islands) carries it, which is a supply line and a target.
Resources and the feed economy
Five resources, all carried by birds, all stored in caches on the island. Fish is the one that
matters; the others are what a colony is built from and what it trades with.
