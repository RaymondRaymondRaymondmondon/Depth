Eggs and chicks
• An egg hatches after one game day if warmed (a mate or any adult sitting on it) and not
stolen or eaten. Unwarmed eggs chill and fail after two days.
• A chick eats for two game days, then fledges into an adult of a role the player assigns
at fledging (fisher, builder, feeder, SCOUt, warrior type, priest, trader). Changing a role
later costs a day of retraining.
Chicks in a nest are a target: CUCkOOS, raiders, rats on fishing towns, and skull island's
beasts all take them. A nest can be guarded by a Watcher.
