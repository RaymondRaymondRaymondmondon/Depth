The loot crate (42 Founder costumes)
One token, one crate, from a nest of eggs in the lobby: tap an egg, it hatches a costume.
Duplicates hatch a feather and nothing else. Odds: Common 60% (15 costumes, 4% each),
Rare (10, 2.5%), super Rare (10, 1.2%), Special 3% (5, 0.6%).
