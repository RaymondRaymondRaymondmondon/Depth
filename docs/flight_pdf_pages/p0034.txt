--flight-sim <island> <days> runs a bot colony and reports birds, feed
per day, days of food, deaths by cause, research reached.
--flight-war <scenari0> runs scripted fights (altitude, wind, formation, stimulants)
and asserts the five rules.
--flight-fair <seed> asserts the map's fairness rules.
--eco-sim flight <minutes> (Red Tide's tool) for the sea.
Balance targets
• A careful solo colony reaches 40 birds by day 6 and 100 by day IO on a tropical island;
30 and 70 on a sea stack.
A fisher dies to predators once per 15 fishing-days on a safe ground, once per 4 on the
cove.
• The twelve founders within ±3% overall with distinct windows (the Fathoms phase
model).
