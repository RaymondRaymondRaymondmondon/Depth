3. Stamina. Flapping fights last 20-30 s before a flock is exhausted (half speed, no climb).
Diving and gliding cost nothing. The flock that makes the other one flap loses nothing
and wins the second engagement.
4. Morale. Every flock has morale (0-100): leadership (the Founder +20, a Flockmaster
+10), fervour, Screamers, recent wins, and feed set it. At 30 a flock retreats home on its
own; at O it scatters and is eaten. Killing the leader is -30 at once.
5. Blood. Birds that fall into the sea call sharks; a fight over water for more than a minute
is a fight over a shark pit, and fishers on both sides will die for days after. Fight over
land to keep your sea clean; fight over the enemy's grounds to poison theirs.
