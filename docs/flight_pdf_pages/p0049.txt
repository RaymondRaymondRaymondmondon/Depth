Structures beyond nests: caches, the Roost, the shrine, the Works, towers, hedges,
perches (a Watcher's post; +sight), smokehouses, the Lookout (a tall perch that doubles a
SCOUt'S report range), the Rookery (a nursery: chicks there are warmed by any adult and
fledge together), the Beacon (a signal fire: calls every flock home at once), and the
Monument (a late, expensive pile of shells that is score and nothing else, which is the kind
of thing people build at the end of a long match).
