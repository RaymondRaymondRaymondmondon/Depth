Relics and legendary birds
Relics are single items found on the Maelstrom's rocks, the Ghost Ship, the Whale's back,
Iron Island's magazine, and the Grey Wings' crag: a colony holds at most three, at the
shrine. Each is one rule: the Ship's Bell (morale +10 everywhere), the Lamp Lens (scouting
range the Kraken's Beak (your flocks ignore the kraken), the Golden Egg (one mate's
clutch doubles), the Fort's Flag (every town's rates +20%), the Eagle's Feather (Strikers
+20%), the Siren's Shell (mates arrive on call), the Keeper's Logbook (every ground's yield
known). Relics can be stolen by raids (the shrine is a target) and traded.
Legendary birds are neutral heroes: one per map, found by the Visitor event or at the end
of a quest line (a town's old pelican sets it). Recruit one and it joins your colony as a
named unit with a rule: the Albatross of the South (your flocks never tire over water), the
Fisher King (a kingfisher; every ground it fishes doubles for a day), the Old Owl (every
Watcher sees everything), the Phoenix Chick (a chick that fledges into your founder
species' best warrior), the Dodo (does nothing, worth 200 score if alive at the end, and
everyone wants to eat it).
Diplomacy, lightly
Six players need a way to talk that isn't a treaty screen. Diplomacy is four things, each one
click, each public:
• Truce (a day, renewable): neither side's warriors attack the other's birds or nests;
fishing the other's grounds is allowed; breaking it costs the breaker 20 fervour and a
town reputation hit, and everyone sees the broken flag.
• Feed pact: a feed line between two colonies; both panels show it; cutting it is an act of
war against both.
