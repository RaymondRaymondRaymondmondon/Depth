Overview
The Flight is a 2-6 player 3D real-time strategy game in which each player is a bird. You
start alone on an island with wings, a hunger, and an empty nest. You fish to feed yourself,
fish to attract a mate, fish to feed the chicks, and the chicks grow into the colony that
fishes, builds, scouts, trades, prays, and fights for you. The sea you fish is Depth's living
food web, and the things in it eat birds. Other players' colonies grow on other islands;
whoever feeds, grows, and fights best is the flock that owns the sky when the match
ends.
What makes it different from Fathoms (Depth's other RTS): you are in the game as a unit.
The camera is on your back. You fish with your own talons, carry with your own wings, lead
attacks with your own beak, and if a shark takes you, your colony loses its leader for a
while and you respawn from your nest as a chick-leader with half your stats until you feed
yourself back up. The colony is commanded with RTS orders; the leader is flown.
Design pillars
1. Feed or die. Every bird eats. A colony that grows faster than its fishing starves back
down. The core tension is birds versus fish, all match, and the best players feel it every
minute.
2. The sea is alive and it is not yours. Fish move, breed, flee, and are eaten; sharks follow
the blood your fishers make; the kraken cove and skull island are ecosystems, not
spawn points. The food-web engine from Red Tide runs the ocean.
3. Your bird matters. Twelve founder species with boosts that bend the whole strategy,
plus the fact that the leader is a flyer with a body: altitude, wind, stamina, and a shark's
open mouth are things that happen to you.
4. War is elaborate and learnable. Scouts report, flocks form, altitude and wind decide
fights, raids steal eggs, sieges starve islands, bombers and chemists change the rules
late. A new player can win a skirmish; a good player can win a siege.
