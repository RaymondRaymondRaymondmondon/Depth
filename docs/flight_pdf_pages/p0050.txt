What a player learns over repeated runs, in the order they tend to learn it; it's also the
order the game's hints appear:
1. Feed before you grow (days of food).
2. Where the fish are by season, and which technique each ground wants.
3. Which founder fits which island and which friends.
4. Altitude, wind, and stamina in a fight; get above them.
5. Decrees as a daily plan, not a daily gift.
6. Raids are theft: eggs, fish, chicks; defend caches and nests before warriors.
7. The dangerous islands' rules (the kraken's offering, the ape's tuna, the fort's flocks).
8. The pirates, the fleet, and the Grey Wings as tools.
