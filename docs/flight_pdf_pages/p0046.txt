Great events
Beyond the seasons' events, the map rolls one great event per match from this list,
announced a day ahead by the Augur (or not at all without one):
• The Red Tide: a bloom kills a ground's fish and poisons feed for a day; caches with
Keepers are safe.
• The Kraken's Walk: the kraken leaves its cove and crosses the map, eating islands'
nests below 20 m; the cove is free for a day.
• The Great Storm: two days of gales; every drifting island moves; ships wreck (new
wrecks to salvage); the lighthouse goes out.
• The Eclipse: a day of night in Summer; owls and Shadows own it.
