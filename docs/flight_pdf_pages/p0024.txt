• Cache raid: Skirmishers take fish from an enemy cache and fly home; a Watcher on the
cache nets one; the rest escape. Every fish stolen is a fish you didn't catch and they

Egg raid: with Trade and parasitism tier 3 (Cuckoos from the start), a Skirmisher carries
an egg home; it hatches yours. The most hated raid in the game and the one that starts
most wars.
• Fisher harassment: a Skirmisher pair over an enemy ground makes fishers drop their
catch and flee; the dropped fish bleed and the sharks come, and the ground is spoiled
for a day.
• Chick snatch: Strikers take chicks from unguarded nests (skull island's lizards do this
to everyone); a chick taken is a mouth you don't feed and a mate they wasted.
Sieges
A siege starves an island:
1. Blockade (War tier 4): a flock holds the enemy's fishing grounds; their fishers can't
work them without a fight.
2. Cut the feed lines: Feeders between islands are unarmed; a Skirmisher pair kills the
line, and the second island starves.
3. Spoil the water: fight over their grounds; the blood brings sharks that fish for you.
4. Hold altitude over the island with a Wall; their Scouts can't leave and their Traders
can't come.
5. Wait. Chicks die first, then adults, then the warriors desert to fish. A siege takes 2-3
game days and costs the besieger feed too (a flying army eats so a siege is a race
between two hungers, and the colony with more fish in caches wins it.
Assaults
Taking an island means landing on it and destroying nests until you hold the most.
Defenders have hedges (walls Skirmishers can't pass; Strikers go over; Bombers burn),
towers (Watchers with nets and double sight), a shrine's fervour (defenders don't rout),
the island's shape (a stack is a fortress; a beach is a landing), and the island's own
monsters (skull island's ape throws rocks at the attackers too). A good assault: Scouts
first, a Hammer to break the Watchers, Bombers on the hedges, Tanks to hold the beach,
Strikers on the nests, Skirmishers on the caches, and a Priest to claim the shrine.
