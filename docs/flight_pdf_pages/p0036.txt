The lobby picks match length by seasons: Spring and Summer only (30 min), three
seasons (45), all four (60). Scores at the end of a four-season match weigh Winter's
holdings double, so late colonies matter.
Decrees
At dawn every game day the player picks one decree from three offered (drawn from a
deck of 24, no repeats in a match). A decree lasts the day. It's one click, it shapes the day,
and the deck is where a match's variety lives.
