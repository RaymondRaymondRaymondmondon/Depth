Rule
As designed
Teams of 2-3 share research and feed lines; a
team's score is summed
No dangerous islands; a longer limit; the pure
economy race
The kraken is awake from the start and roams;
whoever kills it wins outright
Every player starts with 10 birds and no Founder
respawn: one life for the leader
The map's wind is a constant gale that rotates each
day; everyone's islands are on the move (the atolls
drift)
One player, bots at three difficulties, and the sea
Pacing of a 30-minute match (about 8 game days)
Day 1: fish, nest, mate. Day 2: eggs, Roost, the first SCOUt. Day 3: fledglings, first fishers,
War 1. Day 4: raids begin; the center's grounds are contested. Day 5: second islands; sieges
start. Day 6-7: bombers, stimulants, the dangerous islands change hands. Day 8: the
deciding assault or the score.
Costumes
Arcade tokens buy costumes for the Founder (the player's own bird) and, separately,
colony liveries (a color and a hat for every bird in the colony, so an army is recognizable
from across the map). There are 50 Founder costumes: 10 in the token shop and 40 in the
loot crate. Costumes change nothing but the silhouette, and the silhouette is visible to
everyone, which in a game about scouting is a choice.
