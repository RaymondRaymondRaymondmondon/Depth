The rules birds live by
• Blood. A fish that fights on the talons bleeds; a bird that falls bleeds more; a war over
water is a feast for sharks, who then hunt the fishers of both sides. Chemists'
stimulants make birds bleed less; the Beaked founder's Tear makes enemies bleed
more. The sea is a weapon if you can aim it.
Splash and noise. Every dive is a splash the web hears; a ground fished by ten fishers
at once draws predators in minutes; spreading fishers across grounds is safer and
slower.
• Yield. A ground's yield is its standing population; it recovers over days if rested; a
kraken's cove recovers overnight because the kraken keeps everything else out.
• Predator pressure per ground is shown on the map as a shark icon that darkens with
risk; Scouts' reports update it.
