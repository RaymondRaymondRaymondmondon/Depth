The Flight — Arcade Game 7 Design Document
Mates with traits
Every mate that arrives has one visible trait that its chicks inherit as a small bonus: Big
Eggs (+10 HP to chicks' adult form), Quick (+5% speed), Hardy (+10% HP), Keen
(+scouting), Fierce (+5% attack), Fertile (clutch +1). Courtship can be picky: a nest's bowl
filled with a specific fish (the mate's favorite is shown) attracts a mate with the trait you
want. Breeding a colony for traits over a long match is the Lyrebird's game and anyone's
hobby.
