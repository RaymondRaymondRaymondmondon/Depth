The Flight — Arcade Game 7 Design Document
The Tycoon's boom and bust
The Tycoon's Boom is a button: 2 game days of +60% to feed, build speed, and research,
then 1 day of -40% to all three. It's on a 3-day cooldown, so a Tycoon can boom every
other day of a long match. Everyone on the map sees the Tycoon's banner change color
when the boom starts (and when the bust does), so the bust day is when neighbors
attack. The Tycoon's play is to boom into a war or an expansion and have the fish cached
before the bust; the counter-play is to scout the banner.
Match structure and victory
Lobby
2-6 players; founder species; starting island (or random); map arrangement; teams or
free-for-all; match length (20, 30, 45 minutes); dangerous islands on or off; the kraken's
mood (sleepy, normal, hungry); starting season.
Victory
The match ends at the time limit or when one player (or team) holds two-thirds of all nest
sites in use on the map. At the limit, the highest colony score wins:
