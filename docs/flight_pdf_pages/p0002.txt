The Flight — Arcade Game 7 Design Document
5. Islands are characters. A vertical rock, a tropical island, a fishing town, a volcano, a
cove with a kraken, an island with giant beasts: each is a different economy, a different
defense, and a different risk.
Session. 25-45 minutes, 2-6 players, free-for-all or teams, hosted through the Deep
Arcade's shared layer. Score pays arcade tokens; tokens buy bird costumes or spin the
loot crate.
Tone. Depth's inked low-poly style, bright and windy: a tropical sea under a brass sky,
birds with character, a kraken that is a joke until it isn't. Bird deaths are a feather burst
and a splash; chicks starve with a sad peep and a tombstone of shells. Silly names, real
strategy.
A note on the founder birds. The twelve are built from the boosts you listed; four are
renamed so the game owns its characters: the charisma bird is the Lyrebird, the divinity
bird is the Ibis, the egg-thief is the Cuckoo (which is what real cuckoos do), the boom-
and-bust bird is the Tycoon, and the bombing-and-chemistry bird is the Alchemist. The
mechanics are exactly as described.
You are a bird
The player's bird is the Founder: the first bird on the island, the colony's leader, and a unit
the player flies directly in third person. Everything the colony can do, the Founder can do
better, and the Founder is the only unit the player steers with their hands rather than with
orders.
Flight
Speed and stamina. Cruise at 12 m/s; sprint at 20 for as long as stamina lasts (8 s base);
stamina refills while gliding. Flapping costs stamina; gliding on an updraft or a tailwind
refills it. Carrying a load slows you by its weight.
• Altitude. Three bands matter: low (under 10 m: fishing, landing, and the band sharks
can reach when they breach), mid (10-60 m: travel, fights), high (60-200 m: scouting
range, bombing runs, and the band where wind is strongest). Climbing costs stamina;
diving converts altitude into speed (a dive from high reaches 35 m/s and is the fastest
thing in the game).
• Wind. The map has a wind field that shifts every few minutes and is shown by cloud
drift and flag directions on islands. Flying with it is +30% speed; against it, -30%.
Thermals rise off tropical islands and volcanoes in the afternoon: free altitude.
