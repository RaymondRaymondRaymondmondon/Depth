The Flight — Arcade Game 7 Design Document
Watchers and counter-scouting
Watchers see SCOUtS at mid and low altitude and mark them; a marked SCOUt'S report is
known to the enemy (they know you looked). Owls (Watchers) at night see everything but
the Shadow founder's birds. Fog and storms blind both sides.
Wild information
Towns sell gossip (what the fleet saw) for fish. The fortune-teller-equivalent is the old
pelican at every town who, for a pearl, tells you one true thing about the enemy's island.
Frigatebirds you drive off drop the fish they stole and a feather that shows who they
robbed.
Research: the nine trees
Research happens at the Roost (the colony's first structure after the nest) and costs
pearls, shells, and days. One research at a time per Roost; a second Roost on a second
island runs a second line. Each tree has four tiers; tier 4 is the match-changer. The
