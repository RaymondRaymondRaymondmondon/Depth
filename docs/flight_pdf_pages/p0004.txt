The Flight — Arcade Game 7 Design Document
What the Founder does that the colony can't
Fly the camera, scout in person, pick the mate (by delivering the courtship fish), start a
new colony (by landing on another island and building the first nest), lead a flock (a flock
led by the Founder gets +10% speed and morale), carry a bomb, make a trade in person at
a fishing town, and pray at the shrine for a fervour burst. The best players are flying
between these jobs all match.
The colony loop
A colony is birds, and birds come from eggs, and eggs come from a mate, and all of it
comes from fish. The loop is: fish -5 feed yourself -Y feed a courtship -Y a mate -Y eggs -Y
chicks (fed) -Y adults (fed) more fish, more nests, more mates.
A game day
The match runs on a day cycle of about 4 real minutes: dawn (fish rise, the best fishing),
day (building, war), dusk (fish rise again), night (predators hunt, scouting is hard, birds
sleep unless fervour keeps them up). Chicks age by days.
Hunger and feeding
• Every bird has a hunger bar that empties over one game day. A bird at O for half a day
starves (dies). Chicks empty twice as fast.
• Feed comes from caches. Feeders (a role) carry fish from caches to nests; adults fetch
their own if no Feeder is assigned. The colony panel shows feed per day against
mouths per day; the gap is the whole economy.
• A fish's feed value is its size (1-6, from the food web): a sardine feeds one chick for a
day; a tuna feeds a nest.
• Overfeeding does nothing except make chicks grow faster (+25% at full), so surplus is
for growth, trade, or courtship.
Courtship and mates
1. Build a nest (twigs and a site). A nest holds one pair and up to four eggs.
2. Attract a mate: fill the nest's courtship bowl with fish (3 fish of feed value 2+ for the
first mate; more for each after). A mate arrives from the sea's wild-bird population
within a game day, faster with a Lyrebird founder, with a shrine, or on a tropical island.
