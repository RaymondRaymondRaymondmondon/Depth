Fishing
1. Spot fish from altitude (shadows and ripples; schools are dark patches; a Scout's
report marks them on the map).
2. Dive. A dive aimed at the water enters a strike: a slow-motion 0.5 s in which you steer
the talons onto a fish. Hit chance depends on the fish's size, your talon stat, the dive
speed, and the water's clarity.
3. Grab. A fish up to your carry stat comes up with you; a heavier one pulls you under for
2 s (vulnerable) and escapes. A Strongbird carries more; a Taloned bird grabs easier.
4. Fly it home. A carried fish is weight (speed down) and scent (sharks and frigatebirds
follow). Drop it in the nest or at a cache (a storage pile) and it becomes feed.
5. Eat. Your own hunger is a bar that drains over a game day; eating a fish from a cache
refills it. A starving Founder loses stats, then faints at O and is a shark's dinner if it
faints over water.
Carrying and building
The Founder carries one thing: a fish, a twig, a shell, a chick (to move it), an egg (yours or
someone else's), or, late, a bomb. Nests and structures are built by dropping materials on a
site; the Founder can build alone at first and directs Builders later.
Dying
The Founder can be eaten by a predator, killed in a fight, drowned by a load, or caught in a
volcano's ash. On death:
• The colony keeps running on its last orders for 60 s, then goes leaderless: no new
orders, fishers keep fishing, warriors go home, fervour drops.
• The player respawns at the nest as a chick-leader after 30 s: half speed, half carry, no
fighting, and hungry. Feeding yourself three fish restores the adult Founder. Ten more
seconds at full stats and the colony takes orders again.
• With no nest (the island was taken), the player respawns as a chick on a drifting log
and must be found by their own colony or land on neutral ground. Three Founder
deaths in a match is -10% to everything for the rest of it (the colony's confidence).
