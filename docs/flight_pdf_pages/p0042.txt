Veterans and names
A warrior that survives three fights becomes a veteran: it gets a name (from a list, or
typed), +15% to its stats, a feather marker, and a trait (Fearless: never routs; Lucky:
survives one killing blow; Keen: sees stealth; Greedy: steals a fish from every kill; Loyal:
follows the Founder anywhere). Veterans are what a player cares about by Autumn, and
losing one is the game's grief.
