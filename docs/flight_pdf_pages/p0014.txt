Arrangements (the lobby's map option)
• Archipelago: starting islands 300-500 m apart with small neutral islets between;
dangerous islands in the middle. Fast games, early contact.
• Safe Distance: starting islands 900-1,200 m apart with open sea between; the
dangerous islands in the center are the only reason to cross. Slow games, big colonies,
long wars.
• Ring: starting islands around a central kraken cove; every expansion is toward the
same water.
• Chain: islands in a line; your neighbors are your only neighbors.
Maps are generated with rotational fairness (Fathoms' rules): equal distance to the
nearest dangerous island, the nearest town, and the nearest neighbor.
