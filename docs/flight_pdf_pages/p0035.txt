Expansion: the long match
Everything below extends the design above for a 30-60 minute match with more to
choose and more to master, while keeping each system small enough to explain in a
sentence. The rule for every addition: it is one choice with a clear trade-off, it is visible to
other players, and it can be ignored by a new player without losing on day one.
The seasons
A long match runs 12-16 game days through four seasons of 3-4 days each. Seasons
change the sea, the wind, and what pays, so the match has acts without a tech ladder.
