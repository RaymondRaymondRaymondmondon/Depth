Founder stats (the player's own bird) follow the species: the Taloned has the best strike,
the Strongbird the best carry, the Swift the best speed, the Beaked the best attack, the
Albatross the best stamina, and so on. The colony inherits the boost, not the Founder's
body: a Swift's fishers are faster, not smaller.
Balance intent. Each founder has a window: the Taloned, Strongbird, and Lyrebird are
strongest early; the Beaked, Swift, Shadow, and Cuckoo in the mid-game; the Sigma, Ibis,
Tycoon, Alchemist, and Albatross late. The phase model from Fathoms is reused to hold
them within ±3% overall.
Colony birds
Every colony bird is one of the player's species, assigned a role at fledging. Roles are the
unit types. Stats are for a size-3 adult at base; founder boosts and research modify them.
All birds have hunger, can be eaten, and sleep at night unless fervour keeps them working.
