Presentation, technology, and sound
Look
• Depth's inked low-poly in daylight: a bright sea, white cliffs, green islands, brass-and-
sail fishing towns, a sky that goes gold at dusk and ink at night. The ink outline is
thinner than Red Tide's (birds are small and far); silhouettes do the work.
• Birds are rigged from one body plan with species parameters (wingspan, beak, tail,
crest, leg length, a three-tone palette per founder species, a livery color per player).
Wings are two-bone rigs with feather quads that spread and fold; Chains for tail
feathers, crests, and carried fish. Twelve founder species plus the wild birds and the
warrior silhouettes (Tanks are rounder, Strikers are hawks, Watchers are owls, Bombers
are pelicans).
Flight animation is physical: flaps on climb, glides on level flight, tucked wings on a
dive, a flare on landing, buffeting in wind. A tired bird's flaps are visibly heavier.
• Fish, sharks, the kraken, and skull island's beasts reuse Red Tide's CreatureBui1der
body plans and species records.
• Islands are generated from a type's rules (the sea stack is a lathed column with ledges;
the tropical island is a noise heightmap with trees; the town is a kit of docks, roofs, a
tower; the volcano a cone with a lake) and decorated by habitat rules. The map is 2-3
km across.
• Weather is a wind field (shown by clouds, flags, and spray), thermals (visible as
shimmer), storms (a front that crosses the map), and fog.
Camera and control
Third person behind the Founder for flight; a free RTS camera (the C key) for orders; the
map (M) for the chart. Orders: box-select birds, right-click a ground, nest, cache, or target;
flock commands on a bar (formation, altitude, stance); role assignment at fledging on the
nest panel. Controller supported (the Founder flies well on sticks).
