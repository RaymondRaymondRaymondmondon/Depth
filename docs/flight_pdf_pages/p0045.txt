Island
The
Maelstrom
The Ghost
Ship
Bird Island
What's there
A whirlpool a
hundred
meters wide
with rocks at
its center
A drifting
wreck crewed
by the
Drowned at
night
A wild mega-
colony of ten
thousand gulls,
boobies, and
terns
The danger
Anything below
30 m over it is
pulled in; the
rocks at the
center are
reachable only
on a dead-calm
day
The Drowned
take eggs; the
ship rams islands
it drifts into
The wild birds
mob anything
that lands and
eat anything
smaller than a
tern
The prize
Everything the
sea swallows
ends up on the
rocks: pearls,
wreckage, salt
fish, relics
Relics in the
captain's cabin
(below); the
ship's cannon
can be fired at a
neighbor once a
season
Mates without
limit; conversion
at IOX for an Ibis;
guano enough
for every
chemist on the
map
How to hold it
Nest on the
center rocks after
reaching them;
you're
untouchable by
raids and you
can't leave in a
storm
It isn't held; it's
boarded by day
and fled by night
Priests at the
edges convert it a
flock at a time; an
Ibis founder can
take the whole
island by Autumn;
everyone else
trades at its edge
Neutral factions
Three neutral powers live on every map and deal with every player by reputation, like
Fathoms' pirates.
