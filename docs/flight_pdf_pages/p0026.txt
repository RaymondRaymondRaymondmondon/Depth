Trade and barter
• Towns. Each fishing town has a dock market with prices for fish, twigs, shells, and
pearls that move with supply and events (a storm doubles fish prices; a festival
doubles twigs). Traders carry goods there and back. Towns also sell gossip, the old
pelican's truth, and, if the colony is liked (a reputation from trade volume and no stolen
fish), a fleet contract: the town's boats fish a ground for you for a day.
• Players. The barter screen: offer any goods, a research (sharing it costs a pearl), a
truce for N days, a feed line's passage, or a mate (a bird transferred); the other side
counters or accepts. Traders carry the goods; a trade with a player you're at war with is
possible and is a story.
• Reputation. Each town tracks each player: trade raises it, stealing from the fleet or
killing a Trader lowers it; at -50 the town's humans shoo your birds off its roofs; at +50
the town defends your Traders with its own gulls.
The Sigma's cornering
Once per match the Sigma buys a town's whole fish stock at the morning price and sells it
back at evening (prices rise through the day); the profit is pearls, which is research, which
is why the Sigma wins late.
