Tier 1: 2 pearls, 10 shells, half a day. Tier 2: 5 pearls, 20 shells, a day. Tier 3: 10 pearls, 40
shells, 2 days. Tier 4: 20 pearls, 80 shells, 3 days, and the colony must be 25 birds or more.
Pearls are the research bottleneck by design: the oyster beds, the cove, and the trade
screen are where research comes from, so a colony with no deep divers and no traders is
a colony with no late game.
Order of work the game expects
Nesting 1 and Fishing 1 by day 2; War 1 and 2 by day 4 (or you'll be raided); Faith or Trade by
day 5 by temperament; Bombing and Chemistry from day 7; tier 4s from day 10. A 30-
minute match is about 8 game days, so most matches see one or two tier 4s, which is the
point of them.
War
War in The Flight is fought in three dimensions by birds that get tired, get hungry, and get
eaten. The rules below are the strategy system; a player who learns them beats a player
with more birds.
The five things that decide an air fight
1. Altitude. Height is energy. A flock above its enemy dives for +50% damage on the first
strike and can disengage upward; a flock below can only climb (slowly) or run. Strikers
win from above and lose from below. The first order in any fight is "get above them".
2. Wind. A flock flying downwind is faster and arrives first; a fight against the wind costs
stamina every second. Wind shifts every few minutes and is on the map; a flock that
attacks into a headwind turning to a tailwind halfway is the classic mistake.
