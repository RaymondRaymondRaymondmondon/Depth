A cache is a pile with a capacity (20 fish at first, upgraded by research). Fish in a cache
spoil after two game days; a smokehouse (research) keeps them for six. Caches are
targets: raiders take from them, and a cache near the shore is a gull's buffet.
Feed per day and the colony panel
The panel shows: birds (by role), mouths per day, feed per day (from fishers' average
catch), fish in caches (days of food), twigs, shells, pearls, fervour, and the research in
progress. The one number to watch is days of food: under 1 is an emergency, over 3 is
growth.
Fishing grounds
Each ground is a region of sea with a species mix and a yield. Yield = the food web's
standing population there. Overfishing a ground drops its yield (and the predators it
supports leave for a while, which is sometimes the point); resting it recovers. Grounds
near islands are shallow and safe-ish; open-sea grounds are rich and full of things that eat
birds; the kraken's cove is the richest water on the map.
Trade
Fish, twigs, shells, and pearls can be traded at fishing towns (an NPC market) and
between players (a barter screen: offer, counter, accept). Prices move with supply; a town
with a storm coming pays double for fish. Traders carry goods and make the trip; the
Sigma bird's colony gets better rates everywhere.
