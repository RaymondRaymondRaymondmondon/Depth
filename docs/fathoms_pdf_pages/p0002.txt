What Fathoms borrows
Terrain modifiers, morale and supply away from
home, stack efficiency, upkeep, diplomacy stances,
Al takeover of inactive players
Short matches, compact tech, score victory at the
time limit, small readable maps
Housing caps and base needs, gods that bless or
punish
Factions, bosses, relics, and status effects (stun,
bleed, poison, weaken, strengthen)
Match setup and lobby
The host picks every match setting in the lobby; each player picks a faction, a color, and a
team, then pulls their ready lever.
