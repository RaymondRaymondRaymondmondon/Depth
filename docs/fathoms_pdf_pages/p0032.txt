Values are economy x army strength relative to a faction with no bonuses (1.00). Every
faction has a weak era and a strong era, and the early aggressors (Islanders, Merfolk) are
balanced against the late-game factions (Nautilus, Atlanteans) by the length of each era.
Other balance rules
• Heroes and ultimates have identical costs, HP, and damage budgets for every faction;
ultimates with strong crowd control (Neptune's stun wave, Cthulhu's gaze) deal about
25% less damage to compensate.
• Era timing. An economy model puts a steady player in the Steam Era at about minute 9
and the Leviathan Era at about minute 18, matching the pacing targets.
• Neutral rewards pay back their cost in 4-6 minutes, so taking them is worthwhile but
never automatic.
