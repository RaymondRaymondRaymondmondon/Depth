Islanders
The Dethroned Island Queen's people. They win by making the map's tribes fight for them
and raiding with fast canoes.
• Kinship. A Shaman (Barracks, 60 F, 40 B) can perform a 45 s ritual at a tribal town with a
gift of 150 Food and 100 Doubloons. The town becomes an ally: its island counts as
Islander territory and it produces 1 free Tribal Warrior (60 HP, 7 melee attack) every 60
s, up to 6 per town. Warriors don't use population. If the Shaman is killed during the
ritual, the gift is lost.
• Totems (80 Brass, one per island): friendly units within 8 tiles get +15% attack.
• Economy bonus. Fishing and fruit gathering +8%.
• Weakness. Ironclads have 15% less HP.

• Hero: The Coconut Queen. Aura: nearby units heal 1 HP/s. Active "Coconut Barrage"
(45 s cooldown): 40 blast damage in a 3-tile circle, stuns for 2 s.
• Faction techs. Blood Oath (Steam, 200 F, 150 B): Tribal Warriors +25% HP. Sun Drums
(Leviathan, 250 F, 200 B): Totem radius +4, Totems also give +1 armor.
• Ultimate: The Tribal Demigod. A giant warrior that leaps between targets and knocks
units back.
• Wonder: Temple of the Sun. All allied tribal towns produce warriors twice as fast;
volcano Sun Gods ignore your units.
Crustacean Brood
The undersea cave's creatures: slow and armored, getting stronger with every fight they
survive.
• Molting. Units earn experience equal to damage dealt. At 60 experience a unit molts: it
is cocooned for 5 s (takes +50% damage), then gains +25% HP and +15% attack and a
bigger model. Units molt once; Lobster Knights molt twice.
