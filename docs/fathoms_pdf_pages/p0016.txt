Morale and supply (Call of War's rule)
• Every unit has morale from O to 100. Inside your territory (any Lighthouse, Harbor, or
Colony Hall radius) it recovers 5 per 10 s.
• Outside your territory it drops 2 per 10 s; enemy territory doubles that.
• Below 50 morale: -10% damage. Below 25: -25% damage and -10% speed.
• Medics, heroes, and the Supply Caches tech slow the loss. An army can push about 4
minutes into enemy land before it weakens.
• Ships beyond coaling range (30 tiles from a friendly Harbor, Dock, or Coaling Station)
move 25% slower.
Landings. A Transport unloads 1 unit per second on a beach. Units that landed in the last
10 s take +25% damage (Amphibious Drills removes this). Landing directly under a
Watchtower or Coastal Battery is meant to hurt.
Status effects (from Depth's roguelike, used by Unique units and heroes): Stun (cannot
act), Bleed and Poison (damage over time; Medics cure them), Weaken (-20% attack or
speed), Strengthen (+20% attack or speed). Durations are 3-8 s; the same effect does not
stack, it refreshes.
