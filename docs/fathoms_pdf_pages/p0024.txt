• Counter-bids. The target of a raid gets a warning 20 s before the pirates arrive and can
pay 125% of the contract to call them off, or 150% to send them back at whoever hired
them. The hirer can top that bid by another 25%. Each raid contract can be bid on at
most twice, so bidding wars end quickly.
• Limits. One raid contract per target at a time; each cove takes a new contract every 90
s. You cannot hire pirates against an ally.
• Reputation. Hiring +5, trading +1 per 100 resources, sinking pirate ships -30, attacking
a cove -100. Every 25 reputation shifts prices by 5%. Below -50, that cove refuses you
and its raiders target you when unhired.
• Unhired pirates raid the most valuable trade route on the map every 4 minutes (a
Trade Ship route's owner gets a 20 s warning).
• Storming the cove. Destroy the fortress and defeat Blackbeard (1,500 HP; pistol
volleys, calls 4 pirates every 30 s). The cove becomes yours: your deals cost 50% less,
other players can still trade there at +25% and you receive that fee. It is a Pirate Ship
nod from the platformer.
• Ghost Pirate Ship (minute 15, Medium and Large maps): a spectral ship roams the map,
moving and attacking faster than normal ships (1,200 HP). Sinking it pays 400
Doubloons and 1 relic.
