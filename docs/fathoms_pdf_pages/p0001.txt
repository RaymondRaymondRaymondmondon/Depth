Overview
Fathoms is a 2-6 player steampunk island RTS in Depth's Deep Arcade, built for 20-30
minute matches over LAN or the internet. Players start on a home island, claim new
islands with Lighthouses, fight over Coal, Ichor, and relics, and bargain with or battle
pirates, tribes, and volcano gods.
Design pillars
• Islands are the map. Every expansion and every attack crosses water, so the navy
decides where you can go and the army decides what you keep.
• Six factions that play differently. Each has a signature mechanic that changes how
you build, fight, or expand — not just a stat bonus.
• Neutral powers make every match a story. Pirates can be hired against rivals, tribes
raid or ally, volcanoes reward the brave and punish the careless.
• Short and readable. Five resources, three eras, about IO shared unit types per faction,
and a hard 30-minute cap.
• Rooted in Depth. Factions, bosses, and relics come from the roguelike's locations and
characters, so the arcade feels like part of the same world.
