• Changing stance. Offering a better stance needs the other player to accept.
Downgrading is one-sided but announced to everyone, and breaking Allied or Peace
into War gives the betrayer's units -15 morale and pauses their Trade Ships for 60 s, so
a backstab is possible but costly.
• Tribute. Send any resource or Doubloons to a player at Peace or better; 10% is lost as a
fee (0% for allies in Locked teams).
• Trade routes. A Trade Ship sails between your Exchange and another player's
Exchange (Peace or better) or a pirate cove. Each round trip pays both ends Doubloons
equal to 0.15 x the distance in tiles (a 100-tile route pays 15 per trip; a round trip takes
about 2 minutes, so 6 ships earn about 45 Doubloons per minute). Longer, more
exposed routes pay more.
• Communication. Team and all chat, map pings (attack here, defend here, danger), and a
diplomacy panel listing each player's stance, score, and islands, in the spirit of Call of
War's diplomacy screen.
• Team victory. Allied players win together. In Open diplomacy, alliances must be at least
5 minutes old at the moment of victory to share it, so no one allies at the last second to
claim a win.
Victory conditions and scoring
The first player or alliance to meet any enabled victory wins; if the time cap arrives first,
the highest score wins. Every countdown below is shown to all players so everyone can
react.
