Tribal Towns
Tribal towns guard fertile islands. A small town has a Longhouse (3,000 HP), 8 warriors,
and 4 spear-throwers; a large town adds a mini-boss: the Tribal Chieftain (1,200 HP, war
cry buffs warriors +20%) or the Witch Doctor (900 HP, poisons and heals tribesmen).

• Raids. From minute 6, each town sends 4 warriors (Sail), 6 (Steam), or 8 plus a

Volcano Islands
The highest-risk, highest-reward land on the map.
• Rewards. Two volcanic Ichor vents (3,000 Ichor each, gathered 60% faster than ruin
vents) and the summit altar, which gives its owner +1 Ichor per second and is the
objective of Volcano Ascension victory.
• The Sun God guards the altar: 4,000 HP, a fire-beam that deals 60 blast damage in a
line, and a Strengthen aura on 6 fire imps. He reforms 8 minutes after defeat if nobody
holds the altar.
• Eruptions. The volcano sleeps until minute IO, then erupts every 4 minutes. A 30 s
tremor warning shows glowing cracks along 3 lava paths. Lava flows for 40 s at 40
damage per second and destroys buildings on its paths. An ash cloud follows for 60 s
