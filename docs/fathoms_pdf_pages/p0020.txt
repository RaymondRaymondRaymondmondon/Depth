• Seafloor walking. Land units cross shallows at full speed and can walk between islands
joined by shallows without boats. Every map guarantees each home island a shallow
path to at least one neighboring island, and the same path length for every player.

• Hero: The Lobster. 700 HP instead of 500, the slowest hero. Active "Crushing Claw" (40

• Faction techs. Chitin Plating (Steam, 200 F, 150 B): all units +1/+1 armor. Rapid Molting

Merfolk of the Weeds
Mermen, mermaids, and octopi. They own the sea between islands and strike from the
kelp.
• Swimming army. Merfolk infantry and riders swim through any water at 1.2 speed, so
they need no transports and have no landing penalty. On land they move 20% slower,
and more than 6 tiles from the shore they deal 10% less damage.
• Kelp Spread. Kelp beds you own grow 1 tile every 15 s, up to 10 tiles out. On your kelp,
Merfolk units are +20% faster and hidden unless an enemy is within 3 tiles; enemy
ships on it are 20% slower.
• Economy. Kelp gathering +40%. Buildings can be placed on reefs and shallows.
