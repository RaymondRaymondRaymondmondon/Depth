• Hero: The Eel King. Aura: swimming units +15% speed. Active "Riptide" (50 s
cooldown): pulls enemy ships in a 5-tile circle toward the center and slows them 50%
for 5 s.
• Faction techs. Octopus Ink (Steam, 150 F, 150 B): units leaving kelp stay hidden 5s
longer. Tidecallers (Leviathan, 250 F, 200 B, 50 1): Kelp Spread twice as fast and 14 tiles
out.
Ultimate: Neptune. A sea giant whose trident sends a wave that stuns every ship in a
line.
• Wonder: The Coral Throne. Your kelp heals your units 2 HP/s.
Atlantean Lost Ones
The people trapped in the sunken city, worshipping something old. Weak for ten minutes,
frightening after twenty.
• Devotion. A meter from O to 1,000. Gain +1 per second per relic held, +0.5 per second
per ruin isle owned, and by offering units at a Chapel (half their cost in Devotion).
Spend it on conversions and on the ultimate.
• Awakening economy. Gathering is 10% slower in the Sail Era, normal in Steam, and 10%
faster in the Leviathan Era.
• Weakness. The slowest start in the game; rushes hurt.
