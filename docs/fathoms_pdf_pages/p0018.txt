Nautilus Crew
Captain Nemo's crew: a steampunk navy that can adapt to any plan. The easiest faction to
learn.
• Nemo's Logbook. On reaching each era (including the start), draw 3 cards from the
Logbook and keep 1. Cards include Galvanic Batteries (ships +10% damage), Deep
Survey (reveal every ruin and wreck), Pressure Hulls (Nautilus Submarines +25% HP),
Diving Bells (Ichor gathering +30%), Steam Turbines (ships +15% speed), Marine Drill
(Diving Marines +2 armor), Surveyor's Instinct (Lighthouses cost 30% less), and
Quartermaster (Coal upkeep -25%). No card may be drawn twice.
• Weakness. All units cost 10% more.

• Hero: Captain Nemo. Aura: nearby units +10% attack. Active "Full Ahead" (60 s
cooldown): ships within 8 tiles +40% speed for IO s.
• Faction techs. Ballast Tanks (Steam, 200 B, 100 C): Submarines can fire once every 20 s
without surfacing. Electric Harpoons (Leviathan, 250 F, 200 B, 50 1): Harpooners stun
targets for 1 s on hit, once every 8 s.
• Ultimate: The Nautilus. The legendary submarine surfaces for 120 s: 2,500 HP, torpedo
volleys that deal splash damage, can submerge for IO s once.
• Wonder: Nautilus Drydock. Your ships regenerate 2 HP/s inside your territory.
