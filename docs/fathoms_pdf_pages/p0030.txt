How balance was checked
1. Resource weights. Food and Brass count as 1, Coal as 1.5 (fewer seams, and it is also
upkeep), Ichor as 4 (few vents, needed to advance). A unit's cost value is the weighted
sum of its cost.
2. Cost-efficiency. Efficiency = V(HP x damage per second) + cost value. Under
Lanchester's square law this is what decides who wins a straight fight between equal
budgets, so generalist units must sit close together.
3. Equal-budget fights. Two armies of the same resource value (1,200 land, 1,500 naval)
fight in the simulator with armor, bonuses, range, closing speed, and melee crowding
(only about 70% of a melee blob can reach the enemy at once). The target for a
counter is 55-65% of its army surviving: a clear win, not a wipeout.
4. Abilities modeled. Chain lightning was modeled as damage and the Siren's song as
+25% effective HP; abilities that can't be simulated (invisibility, swimming, conversion)
were paid for by lowering raw efficiency.
5. Faction phase model. Each mechanic was turned into an economy and army multiplier
for each era, then weighted by era length (8, IO, and 12 minutes). Factions were tuned
until overall strength matched within ±3% while their strongest era differs.
