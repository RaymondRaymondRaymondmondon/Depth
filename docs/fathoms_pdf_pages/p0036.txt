Fathoms — RTS Design Document
1. Economy manager. Keeps workers training, balances gatherers against the targets in
the Resources section, builds Cottages before hitting the pop cap.
2. Expansion manager. Scores nearby islands (resources, distance, danger) and sends
Lighthouse builders.
3. Military manager. Tracks enemy unit types it has seen and builds the counter from the
counter table.
4. Neutral manager. Decides whether to conquer, pay tribute to, or ally each tribe; trades
with and hires pirates; avoids lava paths.
5. Diplomacy manager (Open diplomacy only). Offers peace to strong neighbors, allies
against the score leader, and responds to human offers.
6. Faction behaviors. Each faction adds one rule set: Islanders prioritize Kinship, Merfolk
fight on kelp, Crustaceans keep molted units alive, Atlanteans gather relics, Clockwork
guards its Coal, Nautilus picks Logbook cards by what it's facing.
Build roadmap for Claude Code
Build Fathoms in eight stages, each ending in something playable, and only start a stage
once the previous one's check passes.
1. Foundations. Balance data file, tile map, camera, selection, commands, and a fixed 20-
tick simulation that takes commands as input (so networking plugs in later). Check:
move workers around a hand-made island.
2. Economy. Gathering, drop-offs, Harbor, Cottages, farms, the Exchange, population.
Check: reach the worker targets from the Resources section alone.
3. Combat. Shared land units, damage formula, counters, morale, the three eras, the
Workshop techs. Check: equal-cost fights in-game match the Balance tables within
about 10%.
4. Islands and navy. Archipelago generator with the fairness rules, ships, transports,
landings, Lighthouses, coaling range. Check: a full IVI against a basic Al on a generated
map.
5. LAN multiplayer. Shared arcade networking layer, lobby, host-authoritative updates,
fog filtering, reconnects. Check: two computers on one network finish a match; then
friends far away play it through ZeroTier or Tailscale.
6. Factions, part 1. Nautilus Crew, Islanders, and Clockwork Foundry, with heroes. Check:
all three pairings played several times each.
