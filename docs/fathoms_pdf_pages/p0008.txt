Starting position (Standard). Harbor, 4 workers, 1 fishing boat, 1 scout skiff, 200 Food, 200
Brass, 50 Coal, O Ichor, 50 Doubloons. High doubles these; Very High gives 1,000 of each
gathered resource and 200 Doubloons.
Population. The Harbor supports 10 population. Each Cottage adds 5 (cost 30 Brass). The
cap is 100 in 2-player games, 80 at 3-4 players, and 60 at 5-6 players, so six-player games
stay readable and network-light. Workers and infantry take 1 population, riders and heavy
units 2, ships 2-4, airships 4.
Upkeep. Steam-powered units burn Coal every minute (listed in each unit's stats). If your
Coal stockpile hits O, all Coal-burning units move 40% slower and deal 25% less damage
until it recovers. Sail-era ships and ordinary soldiers cost no upkeep. This is Call of War's
oil rule, and it makes Coal isles worth fighting for.
The Exchange. Trade Food, Brass, and Coal for each other. Every trade starts at 100 for 70
and moves that resource's price 3% up (buying) or down (selling); prices drift back 1%
every 20 s. Ichor cannot be bought here, only from pirates. Any resource can be sold for
Doubloons at 100 for 15.
