Every tech costs the same for every faction, so no faction can out-tech another by
default. Faction differences live in their mechanics and unique content.
Shared units and combat rules
Every faction has this roster (reskinned to fit it) plus its own unique units. Stats below
were tuned in a combat simulator so each counter wins an equal-cost fight with about 55-
65% of its army left (see Balance).
Damage formula. Damage = attack + bonus against the target's tags - the target's armor
of the matching type, minimum 1. There are three damage types: melee (reduced by melee
armor), pierce (reduced by pierce armor), and blast (ignores armor, and deals 50% splash
within 1.5 tiles). Speeds are in tiles per second; ranges in tiles.
