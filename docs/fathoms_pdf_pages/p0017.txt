The six factions
Each faction has one signature mechanic, two unique units, one hero, two faction techs,
one ultimate, and a wonder. They are balanced to equal overall strength but peak at
different times, so each has a window where it should attack and a window where it
should defend.

Rules every faction shares. A hero costs 200 Food, 200 Brass, 50 Coal, is trained at the
Harbor in the Steam Era, has 500 HP, and only one can exist; if it dies it can be revived at
the Harbor after 60 s for half its cost. An ultimate is summoned in the Leviathan Era for
300 Ichor, stays for 120 s, has 2,500 HP and roughly 40 damage per second, and has a 6-
minute cooldown after it leaves. Unique units follow the same damage rules as shared
units.
