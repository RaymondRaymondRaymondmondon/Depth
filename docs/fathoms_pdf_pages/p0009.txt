RTS Design Document
Economic targets for a steady player (used to tune costs and to build the Al): about 20
workers at minute 6, 32 at minute 12, 45 at minute 18. At 32 workers plus 4 fishing boats,
income is roughly 300 Food, 170 Brass, and 90 Coal per minute after walking time (about
70% of the base rate).
Buildings
Every faction shares these buildings; factions rename and reskin them and swap in their
own where noted in the faction sections. Workers build them; build time halves with each
extra worker, down to a minimum of one third.
