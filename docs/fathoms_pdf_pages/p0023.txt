Neutral powers
Neutral sites are the map's third force: every one can be fought, and most can also be
bargained with. Rewards are sized so that taking a site pays back its cost in about 4-6
minutes, and the riskiest sites pay the most.
Pirate Coves
Each cove is a fortress (4,000 HP) with 8 cutlass pirates, 4 shore cannons, and a harbor of
pirate ships. Every player has a reputation with the pirates from -100 to +100, starting at
