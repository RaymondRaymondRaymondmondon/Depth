Weather rolls every 3 minutes on Medium and Large maps: calm (60%), fog bank (20%,
vision -50% in its area), storm (15%, ships in it take 2 damage per second and move at
60%), whirlpool (5%, pulls ships toward its center for 45 s). A storm or whirlpool never
spawns within 20 tiles of a home island.
Fairness rules the generator must follow
1. Home islands sit on a circle around the center, evenly spaced by angle.
2. Each player's nearest Coal isle, Mining isle, and Fertile isle are the same distance (±2
tiles) from their Harbor.
3. Each player has one tribal town and one pirate cove at the same distance, or shares an
equidistant one with a neighbor.
4. Volcanoes and the richest ruins sit in the center, equally far from everyone.
