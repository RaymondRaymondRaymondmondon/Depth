Clockwork Foundry
The steampunk robots. An industrial army that never loses its nerve, as long as the
furnaces burn.
•
•
Coal-powered army. Military units replace their Food cost with Coal at 0.7x (a 40 Food,
30 Brass Rifleman costs 28 Coal, 30 Brass). Workers still cost Food.
Unbreakable. Clockwork units ignore morale.
Salvage. When any unit dies within 8 tiles of a Clockwork unit or building, the Foundry
gains 12% of that unit's cost as Brass.
No Medics. Mechanics (same stats as Medics) repair units instead.
Weakness. If Coal hits O, the whole army moves 40% slower and deals 25% less
damage, not just ships. Storms deal double damage to Clockwork units.
