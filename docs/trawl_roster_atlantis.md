# The Trawl: Atlantis Waters (transcribed from doc v2, pages 38-41, 46-50, 58, 61; for step 8)

## The ground (page 38)
Open ocean above the drowned city. The water drops from the terraces at 30 m to the Trench's edge at 400 m. The Pale
Eye watches from the deep; the richest fishing in the game and the best relics sit under it.
- Catch. Surface: marlin, giant bluefin, flying squid. Mid: swordfish, opah, oarfish. Deep-drop: Atlantean
  coelacanth, gulper eels, the drowned-gold grouper. Everything is large; a single marlin can cover a night's quota share.
- Flora: sunken gardens on the terraces (shelter the grouper and coelacanth), deep coral (the crabs' habitat), the
  Eye's bioluminescent veils (drifting lights that rise through the night and draw lantern fish, and so everything above).
- Signature, the Pale Eye: a great pale light far below. While any crewman looks at it (the searchlight pointed down, a
  diver facing it, or anyone on the sonar with a ping open over it), Wake rises twice as fast. It blinks when something
  big is coming.
- Threats: everything from the other grounds plus the Kraken, the Ghost Ship, the Deep Choir (a Siren chorus that sings
  the whole crew), and Cult longboats that chum the water around the Gannet on purpose.

Web notes: the longest chain in the game: lantern fish rise to the veils, squid rise to the lantern fish, tuna and
swordfish rise to the squid, marlin hunt the tuna, and the Kraken takes whatever is largest. The sperm whale is a tell,
not a catch: its clicks on the sonar mean a giant squid is below and the whale is hunting it, which briefly makes the
deep safer for divers. Jumbo squid packs strip hooked fish and bite divers.

## Marks, landings, bosses, variants
- Skiff marks (page 58): the Terrace Rim (coelacanth, gold grouper, deep-drop), the Veil Drift (squid and swordfish;
  Broadbill boss water), the Whale Road (the sperm whale; Old Red boss water).
- Landings (page 61): the Drowned Stair (the eternal brazier: cooks twice as fast, +2 Wake a fish; the Keeper of the
  Stair, a drowned priest, harmless while he is paid in offerings; offering bowls 400-1200 and relics), the Watchtower
  stump (a signal fire: lighting it shows every skiff mark on the sonar and draws everything on the ground; no trader;
  the tower cache 300-800), the Cult Landing (the cult bonfire: fast, and the longboats come back; the cult
  quartermaster, dark goods for dark money; the cult's hoard 500-1200, guarded).
- Mini-bosses (page 48): Broadbill (a giant swordfish, 400 kg, spearer: lunges every 4 s, 2000, an inscribed bill:
  spearers can't hurt the wearer), Old Red (a giant squid, 250 kg, grabber: takes two crewmen at once, 2500, a beak
  pendant: grabbers can't hold the wearer). Boss lures 400.
- Variants (page 41): the Eye wide open 8% (Wake builds all night; relics on the terraces glow and show on the sonar),
  Swordfish night 8% (swordfish at the surface from dusk; the sperm whale and a giant squid are both present),
  Procession 6% (cult longboats from the start of the night, chumming a ring round a sunken altar full of relics).
- Threat thresholds (page 50): the big three come on Wake, not a timer: 40 the White, 65 the Ghost Ship, 90 the Kraken
  (Atlantis only). The Ghost Ship: a bell answering yours; comes alongside and boards with drowned crew, steals set gear;
  flee at full steam, or fight: a beaten Ghost Ship leaves a relic chest. The Kraken: the Glass (dead calm), the sonar
  whites out, the fish vanish; arms take crew and gear off the deck, crush hull sections, can take the boat; cut the net,
  kill the lantern, run at full steam, harpoon an arm to make it let go. Cult longboat: chanting from the dark, torches;
  circles and chums the water round the Gannet; outrun it, rifle the rowers, stop looking at the Eye. Drowned sailors:
  gaffs and rifles, keep the deck lit, cast them back over. Isopods: raise the anchor, boiling water from the boiler
  bleed, clean deck.
