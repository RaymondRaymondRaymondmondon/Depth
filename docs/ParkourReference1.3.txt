
 Core Mechanics & Universal Rules
 In this high-velocity survival sandbox, the player has no weapons, no inventory, and no ability to
 hold or interact with objects beyond physical contact. You function on a
 one-hit-death
 system?any direct contact with a predator's attack or lethal hazard results in an immediate
 game over.
 The Traversal & Interactivity Rules
 ?
 Trajectory as an Instrument:
 You interact with the
 world purely through velocity, body
 collision, proximity herding, and slipstreaming.
 ?
 ?
 Proximity Herding:
 Sprinting or hydro-dashing close
 to small fauna frightens them into
 motion, steering their movement vectors into predator lines of sight.
 ?
 ?
 Body-Impact Flora Triggers:
 Plants activate their
 chemical, kinetic, or acoustic effects
 when your body physically collides with or grazes them at high speed.
 ?
 ?
 Persistent Injury Architecture:
 Beasts take real-time
 damage from environmental
 hazards or other predators. Injuries lower top speeds, widen turn radiuses, alter FABRIK
 joint rigs (e.g., limp animations, severed limbs), and bleed into the Eulerian Scent Grid.
 Wounded beasts permanently retain these impairments and become targets for
 scavengers and apex predators.
 ?
 1. The Hull
 Traversing the exterior steel plating of a massive submerged submarine amidst crushing ocean
 currents, thruster jets, and barnacle-encrusted metal.
 The 4 Large Beasts
 ?
 Passive Giant (Hull-Grazer Whale):
 A gargantuan filter-feeder
 that slowly scrapes
 algae off the sub?s keel. Its massive, armored body acts as a moving, solid surface you
 can wall-run across to cross high-current voids and shield yourself from open-water
 predators.
 ?
 ?
 Clever Hunter (Siphon Octopus):
 An insanely intelligent
 cephalopod that anchors itself
 inside flooded exhaust tubes. It tracks the sub?s rhythm and uses dynamic water
 displacement from ballast vents to drag you into its lair, using severed pilot-fish bodies
 as bait.
 ?
 ?
 Unique Predator (Electric Hull-Crusher Eel):
 Senses
 the electrical field of the
 submarine. It wraps its massive body around the hull and violently slams into the metal
 plating, sending a massive shockwave across the hull that disrupts your momentum and
 shatters nearby rust-algae.
 ?
 ?
 Apex Predator (Steel-Biter Megalodon):
 A terrifyingly large shark that bites entire
 chunks out of the submarine's outer superstructure. When it approaches, its massive
 shadow eclipses the local light, and its violent strikes create massive water turbulence
 that tears you off the hull if you aren't anchored in a crevice.
 ?
 The 2 Fodder Beasts
 ?
 Barnacle-Mites:
 Abundant, tiny crustaceans that carpet
 the metal. Dashing through
 them crushes them instantly, coating your suit in slick fluid that momentarily reduces
 drag.
 ?
 ?
 Pilot-Fish Swarms:
 Small fish that ride the hydrodynamic
 boundary layer of the hull.
 Sprinting through them causes the swarm to scatter, providing a visual cue for incoming
 current shifts.
 ?
 The 5 Flora & Organic Types
 ?
 Rust-Algae:
 Brittle metallic growth. Grazing it creates
 a cloud of rust particles that blinds
 predators and corrodes their natural armor if they give chase.
 ?
 ?
 Bio-Electric Hydroids:
 Plant-like organisms attached
 to rivets. Striking them discharges
 a static shockwave that stuns pursuing Siphon Octopuses.
 ?
 ?
 Pressure-Anemone:
 Highly elastic flora. Vaulting off
 it at high speed propels you
 forward while releasing a jet of water that pushes away trailing predators.
 ?
 ?
 Metal-Eater Moss:
 Highly acidic moss growing around
 damaged pipes. Crossing it
 strips a predator's armor if you trick them into trailing your exact line.
 ?
 ?
 Hull-Kelp:
 Long, thick strands attached to the sub's
 hydroplanes. You can grab their flow
 lines with your movement arc to swing around 90-degree metal corners.
 ?
 2. The Pirate Ship
 Navigating the sunken graveyards of 17th-century wooden galleons, rotting rigging, cannon
 ports, and coral-overgrown treasure holds.
 The 4 Large Beasts
 ?
 Passive Giant (Timber-Shell Tortoise):
 A behemoth
 tortoise with a shell encrusted in
 sunken cannonballs and wood planks. It lumbers through ship hulls, crushing rotting
 wood decks and opening up new traversal corridors through the wrecks.
 ?
 ?
 Clever Hunter (Rigging-Mimic Cuttlefish):
 Disguises
 its tentacles as fraying ropes
 hanging from shipwreck masts. It waits patiently for you to vault off a rope, grabbing you
 mid-air unless you inspect the tension of the line before jumping.
 ?
 ?
 Unique Predator (Cannoneer Mantis Shrimp):
 Hides inside open cannon portholes.
 When it senses high-velocity movement across its field of view, it fires a cavitation strike
 at the speed of a bullet, creating a lethal shockwave that shatters surrounding timber.
 ?
 ?
 Apex Predator (The Grand Kraken):
 A colossal deep-sea
 squid whose tentacles wind
 through multiple shipwrecks simultaneously. When it moves, it snaps entire ships in half,
 completely altering the terrain and forcing high-speed escape parkour.
 ?
 The 2 Fodder Beasts
 ?
 Wood-Borer Worms:
 Tiny organisms that riddle the ship
 timber. Dashing over them
 causes weakened wood to collapse beneath you, dropping pursuing predators into lower
 decks.
 ?
 ?
 Copper-Scale Minnows:
 Brightly reflecting fish that
 nest in open chests. Herding them
 creates a flashing light curtain that distracts the Cuttlefish.
 ?
 The 5 Flora Types
 ?
 Cannon-Moss:
 Spongy moss that absorbs kinetic energy.
 Landing on it allows for silent
 drops from high rigging without alerting acoustic predators.
 ?
 ?
 Ship-Rot Fungus:
 Detonates in a spore cloud when struck,
 obscuring vision and
 emitting a rotting scent that draws scavengers into the area.
 ?
 ?
 Mast-Kelp:
 Wraps tightly around broken wood. Dashing
 through it snaps the kelp under
 tension, sending a wooden beam swinging like a battering ram into pursuing beasts.
 ?
 ?
 Barnacle-Cluster:
 Razor-sharp growth. Fatal to you
 if you dash directly into it, but acts
 as a cheese-grater to large predators pursuing you closely.
 ?
 ?
 Siren's Lantern Weed:
 Emits an intense, pulsating
 glow that illuminates dark hold
 interiors, revealing hidden Rigging-Mimics.
 ?
 3. The Island
 Traversing the submerged roots, tidal pools, waterfall lagoons, and ancient flooded tribal
 temples of a tropical island.
 The 4 Large Beasts
 ?
 Passive Giant (Goliath Island Beetle):
 A massive,
 amphibious beetle worshipped by
 the local tribe. Its broad, stone-like shell carries carved tribal idols and serves as a
 mobile platform across wide, open lagoon waters.
 ?
 ?
 Clever Hunter (Mangrove Stalker):
 An amphibious reptilian predator that operates both
 in water and on roots. It utilizes tribal vine traps and natural chokepoints, waiting
 patiently above water drops to snap you as you land.
 ?
 ?
 Unique Predator (Totem-Centipede):
 A massive, segmented
 horror that weaves
 through temple stone and beach sand. The uncontacted tribe alerts you to its presence
 by beating drums when it wakes; it senses ground vibrations and breaches violently from
 underwater walls.
 ?
 ?
 Apex Predator (The Island God / Arch-Serpent):
 A colossal
 sea serpent worshipped
 by the island inhabitants. It circles the island's coastal cliffs, smashing through ancient
 stone bridges and creating massive tidal surges.
 ?
 The 2 Fodder Beasts
 ?
 Tidal Mud-Skippers:
 Small fish that skip across the
 water surface. Sprinting behind
 them gives you a visual marker for safe, shallow stepping-stones.
 ?
 ?
 Glow-Fireflies:
 Swarm around water-lilies. Passing
 through them coats your body in
 bright dust, making you visible to the Totem-Centipede if you don't rinse off in deep
 water.
 ?
 The 5 Flora Types
 ?
 Tribal Drum-Fungus:
 A taut, drum-like mushroom. Hitting
 it with high kinetic energy
 launches you high into the air while emitting a loud acoustic shockwave that triggers the
 Centipede.
 ?
 ?
 Poison-Dart Vine:
 Hanging vines that drip neurotoxin.
 Grazing them is fatal, but tricking
 a Mangrove Stalker into snapping through them paralyzes its jaw.
 ?
 ?
 Razor-Palm Roots:
 Sharp underwater roots. Forces precise
 parkour weaving; pursuing
 predators get tangled and sustain permanent lacerations.
 ?
 ?
 Idol's Bloom:
 A rare flower that, when brushed against,
 releases a mist that instantly
 resets your hydro-dash cooldowns.
 ?
 ?
 Mangrove Root-Sponges:
 Compress under impact and spring
 back, preserving 100%
 of your vault velocity.
 ?

 Apex Predator (Abyssal Arachnid):

 The 2 Fodder Beasts

 Glow-Shrimp:
 Swarm around light plants. Sprinting
 through them splatters glowing fluid
 on your suit, allowing you to paint invisible spider webs by brushing past them.
 ?
 ?
 Blind Cave Loaches:
 Sluggish fish that panic at vibrations,
 signaling where the Tremor
 Worm is about to breach.

 The 5 Flora Types
 ?
 Lantern-Shroom:

 up path junctions.
 ?
 ?
 Acid-Lichen:

 contact, but strips the armor off
 pursueing predators if you juke them into it.

 ?
 Vibration-Spore:
 Fragile drum-like fungi. Hitting
 them generates a massive acoustic
 explosion that deafens the Echo-Stalker and summons the Tremor Worm.

 ?
 Cave-Cabbage:
 Dense underwater leaves that mask your
 scent and thermal profile
 when you slide through them.

 Nerve-Root:
 Paralytic barbs that slow down fodder
 beasts, turning them into stationary
 targets for predators.

 movement vectors.
