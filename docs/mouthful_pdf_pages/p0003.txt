The living web
The arena runs Red Tide's ecosystem engine: about 25 prey and predator species with
diets, blood, sound, and population. Prey schools move with the day (dawn and dusk bring
bait up to the shallows and reef; night sends it down); predators follow blood; grounds
that are overeaten empty out and refill. Players are species in the web: your kills bleed,
your fights are noise, and the NPC sharks come to both. A round's ecology is the same for
everyone and different every round.
