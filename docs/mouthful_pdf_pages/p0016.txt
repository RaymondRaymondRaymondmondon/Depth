The blobfish, honestly
A blobfish wins by being ignored: bots rate it as low-value, players laugh, and a blobfish
that reaches the whale fall at tier 5 and eats the corpses nobody wants can be an
Enormous Blobfish by high tide. The Blobfish King is reached about once in forty rounds
by the people who try, which is the right number for a skin nobody else can get.
Progression and cosmetics
Tokens buy skins that apply to every form on a path (a skin is a palette and a pattern, plus
a hat for the forms that can wear one) and nothing else. Same structure as the other
arcade games: a token shop and a crate.
Tokens
10 per round, 1 per 100 score, 20 for a round won, 30 for a first crown on a path, 500 for the
Blobfish King.
