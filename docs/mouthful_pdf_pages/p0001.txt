Overview
Mouthful is a 3D eat-and-grow arena in the Deep Arcade: every player starts as a fry at
the bottom of the food chain and eats their way up it, choosing a path at each fork, until
they're the biggest mouth in the water or something bigger finds them. Up to 12 mouths
share one reef (players plus bots), the round never stops, death sends you back to a fry,
and the crown goes to whoever holds the top of the chain longest.
What it takes from Fish IO and the .io grow games: one round that runs continuously; eat
what's smaller, flee what's bigger; a mass bar and a leaderboard; growth that unlocks
forms with abilities; a crown for the king. What it adds: a true 3D ocean with depth as a
dimension (the shallows, the reef wall, the open blue, the trench), Depth's living food web
as the prey and the predators, six evolution paths with real trade-offs, and the fact that
choosing wrong is funny (you can choose the blobfish).
Design pillars
1. Everything is food or a mouth. You, the bots, the players, the fish, and the things in the
trench are all one chain. Size decides who eats whom; abilities decide who gets to use
their size.
2. Depth is strategy. Shallow water is safe and poor; the open blue is rich and watched;
the trench is where the largest things live and where the largest things are made.
Where you swim is the first choice.
3. Paths, not stats. The six paths are different games: dash hunters, ink tricksters,
armored sharks, floor-bound snipers, walking hazards, and the blobfish. Each fork is a
commitment you feel immediately.
4. Death is five seconds. YOU respawn as a fry with a small head start and your path
memory; the round is long enough to climb back and short enough that nobody holds
the crown by sitting still.
5. Silly and lethal. Fish with faces, abilities with punchlines, and a leviathan in the trench
that eats kings.
