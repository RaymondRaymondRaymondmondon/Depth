Rule
As designed
Everyone's a blobfish; the first to tier 6 wins;
nobody has abilities and everyone is sad
The lobby picks one path for everyone (Shark
Night, Crab Day)
The round starts at tier 4 in the trench; 8 minutes;
the leviathan is awake
Teams of 3 share a path and a score; killing a
teammate's prey feeds the team
No respawn for the king: lose the crown and you're
out; last crown standing
One player, bots, and a longer round for learning
the paths
The paths are built as a rock-paper-scissors-and-then-some; the arena's bands are where
each one wants to fight. This is the meta the balance pass aims for.
