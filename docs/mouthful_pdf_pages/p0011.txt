Mouthful — Arcade Game 8 Design Document
Blood and the web
Every bite bleeds into the ecosystem engine's scent grid; a fight that lasts 20 s draws the
NPC sharks in the band; a kill is a corpse that the scavengers (and the Giant Isopod
player) want. A player who kills a lot in one place is hunted by things that aren't players,
which keeps kings moving.
Dangers that aren't players
The arena hunts back. The NPC predators are the ecosystem's; the boat, the currents, and
the events are the designer's.
