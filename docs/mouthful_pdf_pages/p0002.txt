Mouthful — Arcade Game 8 Design Document
Session. A round is 15 minutes (lobby: 10 | 15 / 20).2-12 players; empty slots are bots that
play real paths. Score pays arcade tokens; tokens buy skins or spin the crate.
Tone. Depth's inked low-poly in bright water: the same ocean as Red Tide seen from the
fish's side. Deaths are a gulp and a puff of scales. The blobfish is treated with respect by
nobody.
The arena
One map, about 600 m across and 300 m deep, shaped like a reef shelf that drops into a
trench. Depth is the arena's structure: each band has its own prey, its own predators, its
own cover, and its own reason to be there.
