Presentation, technology, and bots
Look
• Depth's inked low-poly underwater, bright in the shallows and reef, dim in the blue,
dark in the trench with bioluminescence. Red Tide's renderer, fog, caustics, and marine
snow.
• Every form isa CreatureBui1der body plan from Red Tide's art workbook (fusiform,
anguilliform, cephalopod, shark, crab, puffer, and a blob) with path palettes; growing a
tier scales the mesh and swaps the form at forks with a 1 s morph (a shimmer and a
new silhouette).
• Faces: every form has eyes that track prey and a mouth state; the blobfish's expression
never changes.
• The camera pulls back with tier; the reticle shows bite cone, ability ring, and the weak-
point marker on a target.
