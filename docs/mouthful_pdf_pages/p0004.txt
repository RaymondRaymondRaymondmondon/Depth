• Hiding. Eel holes (tiers 1-2), coral corridors (up to tier 3), wall caves (up to tier 5), the
kelp curtain (anyone, until it's shredded), silt (crustaceans bury), ink.
• Currents. A surface current runs the shallows east; an upwelling off the wall lifts
anything in it 2 m/s; the trench has a slow downdraft that costs stamina to fight.
• Light. The shallows and reef are lit; the blue dims with depth; the trench is dark except
for bioluminescence and whatever you bring. Dark means short sight for everyone,
which favors ambush paths.
• The pier and the boat. A fishing boat crosses the shallows and blue on a schedule: a
net (drags anything tier 1-3 to the surface, which is death), a hook (bait that's a trap),
and a chum line (free mass and a shark magnet).
• The cleaning station on the reef: fish there don't bite each other (a IO m truce zone;
attacking inside it marks you for the NPC sharks).
• The brine pool in the trench: IO damage per second to anything in it; crustaceans float
on it.
Growing
YOU have mass. Eating adds it; being bitten takes it; crossing a threshold grows you a tier,
and tiers 2, 4, and 6 are forks where you pick a path. Size is what you can eat and what can
eat you.
