Balance targets
• A competent player reaches tier 4 in 3 minutes, tier 6 in 7, and the crown in 11 if nobody
stops them.
Equal-mass duels follow the counters table at 55-65%; mirror matches are 50 ± 3.
• The crown changes hands 3-6 times in a 12-player round.
• Deaths by cause at the end of a round: players 60%, NPC predators 25%, the boat and
hazards 10%, the leviathan 5%.
• The Blobfish King is reached about once in 40 rounds by players who try.
