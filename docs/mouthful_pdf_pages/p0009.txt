Movement and combat
Swimming
• Third person behind the fish; mouse or stick steers in full 3D; W swims, shift boosts
(stamina), space and ctrl pitch up and down for those who want them. Boost is the
chase and the escape; stamina is 4 s at base and refills in 6.
• Base speeds by tier: 4 m/s at tier 1 rising to 7 at tier 5 and falling to 6 at tier 8 (big things
turn slowly). Path multipliers apply. Turning radius grows with tier: a tier 7 can't follow a
tier 3 through a coral corridor.
• Crustaceans walk at their listed speed, jump with space, and fall at 3 m/s; they can grip
walls on the wall's ledges.
