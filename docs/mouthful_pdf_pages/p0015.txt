It's a blobfish
• Tiers 1-2: the shallows and the reef's edges; eat schools; take the first fork by where
you want to live.
• Tier 3-4: the reef; crustaceans hold the floor and the wall ledges; eels hunt the
corridors; cephalopods hunt the eel holes' neighbors; sharks start going to the blue;
pufferfish graze and get left alone.
• Tier 5-6: the blue for sharks and eels (tuna); the wall for everyone else; the whale fall at
minute 11 pulls everyone to the trench.
• Tier 7-8: the trench to finish growing, then out of it, because the leviathan.
The king's problem
A king decays 1% a minute, is visible to everyone, and is worth 300 plus their mass. Kings
that hide shrink; kings that hunt bleed. The intended king play is a circuit: the blue for
tuna, the reef edge for a mid-tier kill, back to the blue, never the trench after the crown.
The intended regicide is three tier-5s with abilities, or one fleeing fish and a patient
leviathan.
