Mouthful — Arcade Game 8 Design Document
Simulation and networking
• The host runs the ecosystem (Red Tide's engine: 25 species, about 400 active agents),
the NPC predators, the boat, events, and the bots.
• Host-authoritative through the shared arcade layer: local prediction for the player's
swimming; bites resolved on the host with 150 ms of lag compensation; 20 Hz
snapshots with interest management by distance and the band's visibility.
12 players is the design cap; join-in-progress is supported (fry spawn with the head
start).
Bots
Bots play the paths with three levels: Minnow (eats prey, flees everything bigger), Hunter
(hunts players a tier below, uses abilities, avoids the trench), Shark (reads tells, baits
abilities, goes for the king). Bots fill empty slots and never outnumber players in the top
three of the leaderboard (a Shark bot that would take the crown throttles itself unless the
mode says otherwise).
Save
The arcade profile stores rounds, best tier per path, crowns, skins, and the Blobfish King
flag.
Sound design
• Bands have beds: surf and gulls in the shallows, the snapping-shrimp crackle of the
reef, a wide quiet in the blue, the vents' hiss and the pressure drone in the trench.
• Growth is heard: your own swim gets heavier with tier; a tier-8's tail is a slow whoosh
and the fry you pass flutter away.
• Bites: a snap for a miss, a wet crunch for a hit, a gulp for a swallow (bigger with the
mass), and the chomp of the king.
• Abilities each have a voice: the dash's whip, the ink's blurt, the frenzy's thrum, the
claw's snap and the slam's thud, the puffer's intake and squeak, the detonation's pop,
the stonefish's silence (the one ability with no sound; its tell is visual only).
• Danger: the apex sharks have a two-note motif that enters when one's within 40 m; the
orca pod's clicks; the leviathan's single deep note when it wakes, which everyone in the
trench hears and acts on; the boat's engine and the net's hiss.
