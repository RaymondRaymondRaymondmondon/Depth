Eating rules
• You can bite anything; you can swallow (kill and gain its mass) anything with less than
60% of your mass. Between 60% and 100% is a fight (bites take 10% of the target's
mass per hit until one flees or is below 60%). Above 100% is a flee.
• Swallowing takes al s animation during which you're slow; a tier-5 swallowing a tuna is
a tier-6's moment.
• Prey mass is as listed; a player's mass is their bar, so eating a tier-4 at 400 mass is 400
mass, the fastest growth in the game, which is why players hunt players.
• Mass decays 1% per minute above tier 3 (big fish are hungry) and not at all below. A
king who stops eating shrinks.
Forks
• Fork 1 (tier 2): pick one of six paths: Eel, Cephalopod, Shark, Crustacean, Pufferfish,
Blobfish. The choice is permanent for this life.
• Fork 2 (tier 4): the path's second form (two options each, except the blobfish).
• Fork 3 (tier 6): the path's final form (two options each, except the blobfish).
Dying resets you to a fry, but the game remembers your path: your next tier-2 fork
offers the same path first (a free re-pick), and a path streak (three lives on one path)
gives that path's forms +5% to their main stat.
