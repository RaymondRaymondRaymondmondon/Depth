Mouthful — Arcade Game 8 Design Document
The crown
The first player to tier 8 is King and wears the crown: +10% to everything, visible to all on
the leaderboard and in the water (a glow), and a target: killing the king is worth their mass
plus 500 and the crown passes. The crown is scored by time held.
The six paths
Each path is three forks deep: the path at tier 2, a second form at tier 4 (two options), a
final form at tier 6 (two options). Stats are multipliers on the tier's base (speed, HP, bite).
Every form has one ability on a cooldown and one passive. The blobfish has none of this
and is proud.
1. The Eel path (reptile and eel): dashes
Long bodies that close distance. The hunt is a chase; the ability is the catch.
