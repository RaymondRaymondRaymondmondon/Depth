Mouthful — Arcade Game 8 Design Document
Match structure
The round
• 15 minutes by default. Everyone spawns as a fry in the shallows at 0:00; the orca pod
passes at a random minute between 5 and IO; dusk at 10:00; the whale fall at 11:00; the
final minute is high tide: mass decay stops, the leviathan sleeps, and the leaderboard is
pinned on screen.
• Players join late as fry with a mass head start equal to the lowest living player's mass
(capped at tier 3).
• Bots fill to 12 and play real paths with three difficulty levels; they're named like fish
("Gary", "Brinefloat", "The Admiral") and have the same kill feed as players.
