Shared enemy rules
Enemies see, hear, and smell like beasts: they can be lured by chum, blinded by ink, and
distracted by beasts attacking them. Their squad Al prefers a fight against players over
beasts but will turn to defend itself.
Enemies scale with tide: +8% HP per tide, better weapons every 5 tides (Forged
weapons at tide 15+).
An enemy killed by a beast counts for no scrip, but the beast that ate it is now fed and
calmer.
