The web in motion
• Two webs, one cave. The wet web (mould -Y cavefish eels and catfish -Y caiman -Y
sleeper shark) and the dry web (moss crickets newts and spiders toad caiman
and salamander) meet at the pools, and the caiman walks both.
• The Drowned don't bleed, so only the Ghost Worm and the caiman (which attacks cold
things by habit) fight them. A team that learns to lead the Drowned past the Ghost
Worm's wall crack wins Hunts cheaply.
• Lanterns and lights: the Lantern Bearer, the glowworms, firefly squid, and drop bottles
all draw CUriOUS species. The anglerfish's lure looks like a drop.
• The sturgeon: 80 blood for one harmless fish. New players shoot it; the sleeper shark
thanks them.
Slipstreams move blood: a wounded fish fleeing into a slipstream carries its trail to the
next hall.

Ghost Worm all OCCUr in a four-player test.
