Slipstreams A-F are one-way currents (8 m/s) between zones; a diver can't fire inside one
and arrives with 2 s of drift. Beasts use them too: what you see enter a slipstream behind
you arrives where you do. The Drowned do not use them.
Level mechanics
• Rockfall. Gunfire in the Chimney and the Cathedral has a 5% chance per loud shot
(noise 6+) to drop a stalactite somewhere nearby: 200 damage to whatever is under it,
beast, enemy, or diver. The Resonator drops them on purpose.
• Air chambers. The Dry Chambers hold the map's insects and amphibians; divers walk
there, slowly, and the toad's tongue reaches from the pool.
• Echo. The cave counts as one region for the alarm; the Drowned come from the Dry
Chambers.
• Bloodvine. A red alga that smells like blood to the web: shooting a patch makes
predators investigate it. The cave's built-in decoy, and its built-in trap when it grows
near the Forge.
