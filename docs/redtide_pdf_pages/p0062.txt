Red Tide — Arcade Game 5 Design Document
• Maps are built in code from layout data. Each map is a text layout of rooms, corridors,
and props with decoration rules (which flora grows on which surface, where habitats
go). Atlantis has a city generator: districts on a hill, streets by slope, building types by
district, decorated by the habitat rules. The Sunken Ship is a lathed hull with decks cut
from it. Caves are marched from noise fields with hand-placed anchors for the galleries
and slipstreams.
• Blood and water. A red tint at the mask edge that grows with local scent; visible blood
plumes; the scent meter and predator pulse on the HUD.

• Host-authoritative through the shared arcade layer (GameNetworkingSockets). The
host runs the ecosystem and hit validation.
• Client prediction for the local diver's movement; lag compensation for hits: the host
rewinds beast positions up to 200 ms to judge a shot, so aiming feels local.
• Snapshots at 20 Hz with delta compression; beasts and enemies interpolated 100 ms
behind; interest management by distance (200 m) and line of sight.
• Scent and alarm live only on the host; clients receive the HUD values for their own
position.
• Disconnect: the diver is downed and held for 2 minutes; reconnecting restores them if
the body is revived.
