The web in motion
• Corpses are the deep's economy. Rattails, isopods, amphipods, sea pigs, and bone
worms finish everything; the sixgill follows them. Killing the scavengers to farm scrip
on the rim turns the rim into a shark lane by tide 8.
Lights: hatchetfish, specimen kelp, void blooms, the anglerfish's lure, the leviathan's
lure, and the divers' own lamps. Turning lamps off lowers curiosity-driven approaches
by half and doubles what a diver can't see.
• The specimens: salamanders and coelacanths breed if ignored and only the caiman
eats them; a lab overrun with specimens is loud and full of blood every time a Remnant
squad passes through.
• The overlooks: whale and squid fight each other; the Relict eats the winner; standing
on an overlook during that is standing in the blood of three apex beasts.
• The Chief's mech is size 5 and the Relict attacks it on sight: the Remnant's final Hunt
can be ended by leading the mech to an overlook.
