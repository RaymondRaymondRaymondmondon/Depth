Red Tide — Arcade Game 5 Design Document
Simulation budget
About 300 active agents at full rate (10 Hz decisions, 60 Hz movement) near the players,
and population counters only for regions beyond 120 m. The host runs the simulation;
clients receive agent positions and states with fog filtering by distance. --eco-sim <map>
runs the ecosystem headless and prints population curves, blood levels, and
predator arrival times for a scripted player pattern.
Weapons
Red Tide's arsenal follows Zombies' shape (a fixed starting pistol, wall racks, a random
box, a Pack-a-Punch, and a wonder weapon per map) with underwater guns: gas-driven
dart and needle guns, spearguns, harpoon rifles, and sealed-cartridge powder guns. Every
weapon has a noise value that feeds the alarm and the sound layer, so the loud guns are
the strong ones and the quiet ones are the safe ones.
Stats and rules
• Two weapon slots (three with Mule Brine). Weapons are picked up from racks, the
Locker, or dead enemies.
• Damage is per dart or bolt; rate is rounds per minute; noise 1-10 feeds the alarm; reload
is seconds.
• Ammo is bought at the rack (half the weapon's price) or refilled by a Resupply drop.
Locker weapons can only be refilled by Resupply or the Forge's re-arm.
• Headshots and weak points do (3x with Deadeye Brine).
• Water drag: dart weapons lose damage past 25 m, harpoons past 60 m, powder guns
past 40 m.
