Level mechanics
• Localized alarm. The ship is one region with two sub-regions (inside, outside); gunfire
inside fills the alarm because the hull carries sound. Splitting two-and-two between
inside and outside is the intended lesson.
• Listing. Every few tides the wreck settles: a cabin door jams, a container slides, the air
pocket shrinks by a third.
• The air pocket is the crocodile's lair and the place Wreckers retreat to.
• Dangerous flora line the corridors; the price of sprinting is fire coral.
