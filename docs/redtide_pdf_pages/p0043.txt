The web in motion
• The corridor pair: moray flushes, grouper catches; kill one and the other becomes
reckless.
Dolphins versus sharks: the pod's protection is worth more than any tonic; the Raiders
know it and shoot dolphins on purpose to turn them against the divers.
• The flats: garden eels retract (revealing you), goatfish clouds mark safe sand, the
bobbit worm punishes the straight line, and the dugong's blood calls the hammerhead.
Keep the eaters of dangerous things alive: green turtles eat box jellies; Napoleon
wrasse eat cone snails and blue-rings.
• The drum: the Shaman's drum raises aggression and can call the Matriarch. Kill the
Shaman first, or take the drum and use it on the Raiders.
