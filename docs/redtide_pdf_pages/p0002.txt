Dossiers and one hidden quest per map
1. The food web is the enemy. Zombies come because it's a round; beasts come because
you made blood, noise, or a corpse. The ecosystem runs without you and reacts to
everything you do.
2. Every kill is a decision. Small kills are safe scrip; big kills bring bigger things; too many
kills bring the things that eat everything.
3. Enemies are prey too. The human and unhuman factions that hunt you are also on the
menu, and the smartest play is often to let the reef eat them.
4. Each map is an ecosystem first and a level second. Layouts are built around where
creatures live, feed, and hide.
5. Zombies pacing. Fast, readable, arcade-loud: 60-second tides early, minutes-long late,
always a way to run, always a reason to stop and buy.
The five maps (smallest to largest): the Sunken Ship, the Underwater Cave, the Coral
Reef, Atlantis the Forgotten City, and Approaching the Void.
Session: 4 players (or 1-3 with scaled quotas), 20-60 minutes, ending when every player is
dead. The score is the tide reached and the scrip earned, and both pay arcade tokens.
Core loop and rules
A match runs in tides. Each tide has a kill quota; meeting it ends the tide, pays a bonus,
and starts the next one harder. Between tides the water calms for 20 seconds, the only
reliable time to buy, build, and revive.
