The Pressure Forge (Pack-a-Punch)
5,000 scrip after power. A pressure chamber that crushes the weapon into a stronger
form: +150% damage, +50% ammo, a new name, a sea-glass finish, and a slot for one
alternate ammunition (rolled free, re-rolled for 2,500):

Effect (Zombies analogue)
Every 6th hit chains lightning to 3 nearby (Dead
Wire)
Hits mark the target so predators prefer it for IO s:
the beast becomes bait (Turned)
Hits explode in light, blinding beasts with eyes for 3
s (Fireworks)
Every 6th hit pushes everything in a line 8 m
(Thunder Wall)
Hits cook: burn damage that leaves no blood (Blast
Furnace)
The Forge can also re-arm a Locker weapon (full ammo) for 4,500.
Melee
Diver's knife (100 damage, silent), Boarding Axe, Tesla Gaff, Trident, and the Forge-only
Sawtooth (a sawfish rostrum, 450, bleed). Melee kills make no alarm and less blood (the
wound is one clean cut), so a knife team is the quiet route through the early tides.
