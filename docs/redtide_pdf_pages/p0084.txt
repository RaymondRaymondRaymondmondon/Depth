Solo and duo
• Quotas scale 0.4/0.6 / 0.8; squads are 2/ 3/3 units; Hunt forces are 5/719; Predator
Hunt kills are 1/2/3.
• Solo: Quick Brine gives a self-revive (3 per match); a downed solo diver has 60 s.
• DUO: a downed diver has 55 s; revives take 3 s.
Hidden quests: step lists
Each map has one hidden quest. Steps are ordered; a step that fails (the item is lost, the
timer runs out) resets only that step. Every quest has a fail-safe: nothing in it can make a
match unwinnable.
The Sunken Ship: the captain's safe
1. Trigger: read the captain's log on the bridge (a chalk prompt appears after tide 3).
2. Collect three keys: one drops from the Goliath (any kill), one from the Wreckers'
Foreman (a Hunt), one is held by the Common Octopus (kill it in its den; if it has stolen
an item, the key is with it).
3. Carry all three to the safe on the bridge; opening takes 20 s and rings the ship's bell (10
noise). Fail: a diver holding a key who dies drops it where they fell.
4. Reward: 3,000 scrip each, a Forged wonder weapon (the Lightning Keel) for the
opener, and the dossier page "The Owners".
