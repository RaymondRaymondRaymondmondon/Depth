Red Tide — Arcade Game 5 Design Document
• How to play: one scrolling page in the ship's log style: the loop, scrip, blood, the alarm,
downs, the Locker, the Forge, tonics, drops. Six short paragraphs and one diagram of
the blood rules. Nothing else teaches.
• Dossier: a tabbed bestiary per map; pages unlock by kill or 30 s of observation; each
page shows the beast's silhouette, class, diet, predators, weak point, and its dossier
note.
• Records: per map, highest tide, most scrip, fastest Forge, longest survival, and team
records.
• Pause (host only, 3 per match): the salon behind the porthole, the team list, options.
• Results: the tide reached, scrip per diver, kills by class, deaths by cause, beasts eaten
by beasts because of you, arcade tokens earned; a slow shanty.
Controls (keyboard and mouse; gamepad mapped the same)
Move, swim up and down (space and Ctrl), sprint (shift, with a stamina bar that only shows
while sprinting), aim, fire, reload, melee (V), tactical (G), swap weapon (1, 2, 3), interact (E,
hold for purchases over 1,000), lamp on and off (L), ping (middle mouse: a marker
teammates see, with a voice line), charm (T), scoreboard (Tab).
The divers and their quips
The four divers are four suits, not four characters: the Diver, the Whaler, the Stowaway,
and the Mechanic, each with a fixed silhouette and a voice, and each replaceable by a
skin. Their lines are about the situation, not their lives, so any skin can say them; the fun is
that they talk to each other.
How quips work
• Each diver has a voice (a formant synth pitch and rhythm) and a line set per situation.
Lines are picked from the set, never repeated within 3 minutes, and only one diver
speaks at a time.
• Callbacks: about a third of lines are two-part: one diver speaks, and a teammate within
15 m answers 1-2 s later with a reply from their own set. The four voices differ in tone:
the Diver is dry, the Whaler is gruff, the Stowaway is cheerful and slightly drunk, the
Mechanic is nervous and precise.
Quips are subtitled and never interrupt a tell or a Hunt announcement.
