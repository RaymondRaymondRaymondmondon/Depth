Red Tide — Arcade Game 5 Design Document
The web in motion
• The sprat chain: algae mat krill sprats trevally and barracuda bull shark tiger
shark. Shooting sprats for cheap scrip is the map's first trap.
• The cleaner rule: the galley and the outside cleaning stations calm the hosts that visit
(groupers, barracuda, turtles). Wiping them out makes the mid tiers hostile
everywhere.
• The crocodile problem: the only air inside is its lair. Feed it a turtle (shoot one near the
pocket) and it eats for 60 s.
• Wreckers feed the reef: a Wrecker squad dying in the salon is 80 blood in a closed
room; the bull shark arrives 30 s later. Let the reef kill them outside instead.
• The Goliath: everything that flees runs to the engine room's shadows and gets eaten
there. The Forge is safe only right after it has fed.
Definition of Done
• All 40 beasts and 12 flora exist as data and models; --eco-sim ship 20 shows the
sprat chain calling a bull shark within 90 s of 15 sprat kills in the salon.
• A four-player LAN match reaches tide 10 with the crocodile, a Wrecker Hunt, and the
Goliath's inhale all seen.
Level 2: The Underwater Cave
A flooded cave system with dry air chambers, a deep sump, and six slipstreams that carry
divers and beasts between its halls in seconds. It is dark, loud (every shot echoes), and
half its cast is insects and amphibians that live where the water meets the air.
