Red Tide — Arcade Game 5 Design Document
Effects
• Every beast has a voice cue (clicks, grunts, croaks, whistles, or silence by design), a
pain cue, a death cue, and a feeding cue (tearing, crunching, slurping). Feeding cues are
loud by design: they tell the players where the corpses are being eaten and how many
things are eating.
• Blood has a sound: a wet bloom on a kill, a spreading hiss as a plume grows.
• Weapons: fire, reload steps, empty click, the Forge's crush and hiss, the Locker's eel
snap and the chest sinking, the Harpoon Cannon's boom.
• Tonics: each machine has a jingle (the Zombies tradition), a bottle clink, and a gulp.
• Drops: a bell for a drop appearing, a distinct sting per drop.
• The alarm: no sound tells the player the alarm value, but the faction's arrival has a tell:
the Wreckers' cutters scraping the hull, the Drowned's bell, the Raiders/ drum, the Lost
Ones' marching, the Remnant's airlock cycling.
• Tells for hazards: the sand worm's rumble, the Reacher coral's creak, the bobbit
worm's hiss, the toad's croak, the Wyrmls grate rattle, the leviathan's lure hum.
HUD and UI: the tide bell, the quota tick, the scrip counter's coin, the down and revive
stings.
Accessibility
Every audio tell has a visual: a directional pulse on the mask, a caption for barks, and a
scent meter for the blood strings.
Balance, simulation, and build order
Red Tide is balanced against Zombies' pacing (a competent team reaches tide 20 in about
40 minutes) and against its own ecosystem, which must punish careless killing without
making careful play boring.
