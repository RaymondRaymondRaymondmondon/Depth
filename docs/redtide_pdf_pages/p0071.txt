Barks
Spoken as gurgling radio chatter (formant synth, subtitled). Each unit has 6-8 lines per
situation; examples:
Spotting divers: "Divers on the wreck. Nobody's collecting our salvage." / "Four of 'em.
Chum 'em."
• Chum thrown: "Dinner's served, fish." / "Let the reef do the work."
• Shark arrives: "That's not ours! Back to the breach!" / "Cut the chum, cut the chum!"
• A Wrecker dies: "Marlow's gone. Keep working." / "The safe's worth it. Push."
• Netting a diver: "Got one wrapped!" / "Pull it in, pull it in."
• Foreman: "The safe's mine, and sols the ship." / "Winch up. Put a bolt through the
helmet."
The same treatment for the other factions
The Drowned (Deckhand, Lantern Bearer, Bosun), the Reef Raiders (Spear Diver, Turtle
Rider, Sling Diver, Shaman), the Lost Ones (Legionnaire, Cultist, Slinger, Armored Lost
One, Priest), and the Remnant (Sentinel, Researcher, Cultist, Station Chief) each get a
units table, five tactics, and bark sets in their map's workbook on a Faction sheet.
Boss fights
Every map boss follows one template: three phases at 100-60, 60-30, and 30-0% HP; an
arena that is a room until tide 12 and the whole map after; one way to blind, stun, or
expose it that rewards knowing the map; and a reward of 1,500 base scrip, a Locker
weapon per diver, and a quest key.
