Level 3: The Coral Reef
The bright map: sunlit coral forests whose corridors hide eels and grabbing coral, a lagoon
edged with mangroves where frogs and toads live, a sand flat with its own cast, and a
drop-off where dolphins, seals, and whales pass. Mammals and amphibians join the fish,
reptiles, and crustaceans, and the reef's first lesson is that some of them are on your side
until you shoot one.
