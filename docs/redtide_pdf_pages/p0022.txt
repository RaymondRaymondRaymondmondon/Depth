What a beast contributes to the web
Killing a beast removes it from its region's population, adds its corpse (a blood source
scavengers reduce), and changes who patrols there. Killing all the cleaners in a zone raises
every host's aggression; killing all the grazers empties the zone of the predators' reason to
come; killing an apex empties a zone of fear for one tide, and then something else moves
in.
Level 1: The Sunken Ship
The Marguerite, a 60-meter steam yacht on her side on a sand shelf at 30 meters, is the
smallest map and the tutorial for the whole game: tight corridors, flora that bites, fish
whose blood calls sharks, and an alarm that fills fast when four divers fire in one room.
