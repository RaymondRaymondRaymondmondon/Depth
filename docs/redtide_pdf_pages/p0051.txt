Level 5: Approaching the Void
The largest map: a black sand plain on the rim of an abyss, and beneath it a cave system,
built as vertical chimneys and galleries rather than the second map's halls, that descends
past a drowned research station to a vault that looks straight down into the void. The rim
is empty and enormous; the guns, tonics, and the Forge are all below, and every gallery
deeper is more dangerous and richer. The Sand Worm patrols the far rim so that no one
wanders off the map.
