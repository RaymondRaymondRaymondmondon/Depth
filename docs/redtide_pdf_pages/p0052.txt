Overlooks: every level has at least one gallery open to the abyss. The void is a visual: a
black column with drifting motes and a faint pale light far below; a diver who swims out
over it sinks and dies (a 5 s pull with a warning). Traps: a pressure door (crushes a
corridor), the reactor vent (a scalding jet), the vault's collapsing ledge over the abyss.
Hidden quest: assemble the station's final log from five terminals to learn what the
researchers were breeding.
Level mechanics
• Down is better. Racks and tonics deepen with the galleries; the Forge is at the reactor;
the Vault's racks and Locker are the best on any map. Blood decays slowest here (the
water barely moves), so every deep fight lingers.
• The Sand Worm. A size-7 behemoth seen tunneling on the rim's horizon. Beyond a
marked line of bone stakes, the sand shakes; 8 s later, the worm surfaces and
consumes the diver (no down, no revive). It never enters the caves and takes part in no
web; it is the edge of the world.
• Overlooks are where the abyss's own beasts arrive: the colossal squid, the sperm
whale, the Relict. Fighting there is fighting with your back to a fall.
