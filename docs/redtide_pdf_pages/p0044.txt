Red Tide — Arcade Game 5 Design Document
Level 4: Atlantis, the Forgotten City
A walled city on a hill, drowned and overgrown: farms and irrigation on the flat ground
inside the wall, workshops and markets in the lower town, bathhouses and barracks on
the slopes, and the government hall and the grand chapel on the summit. Every building
type is now a habitat, and the city's own water systems, the aqueducts and cisterns, move
blood and beasts through it.
