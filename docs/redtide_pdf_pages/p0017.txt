Player stats and progression
• In-match stats: kills, headshots, scrip earned, downs, revives, beasts eaten by other
beasts because of you, enemies eaten, highest tide.
• Between matches: an arcade profile rank from 1 to 50 across all Red Tide maps; ranks
unlock charms, skins, and dossier pages. No stat ever changes in-match power; every
match starts from the same spawn-in.
• Dossiers: every beast, flora, and enemy killed or observed for 30 s adds a dossier page
(behavior, diet, weak point, what it fears). The full dossier is the map's real strategy
guide.
Player skins
Four divers with fixed silhouettes, each a Depth character type: the Diver (brass helmet),
the Whaler (slicker and hood), the Stowaway (a patched suit and a bottle on a cord), and
the Mechanic (a riveted hard suit). Skins bought with tokens change suit colors and
helmets (verdigris, red tide, bone, pearl, Atlantean) but never the silhouette, so
teammates and enemies always read who is who.
Enemy factions
Each map has one faction drawn by the alarm and by Hunts. They are intelligent, they
shoot back, and they are also animals in the food web: they bleed, they are hunted, and
some of them use the web against the players. Enemies drop their weapons and 200-600
scrip.
