--eco-sim <map> <minutes> [pattern] runs the ecosystem with a scripted player
pattern (quiet, loud, camping, spread) and prints population curves, blood levels by
region, and predator arrival times.
--redtide-sim <map> <tides> [careful I careless] plays a bot team and reports
the balance targets above.
--web-check asserts every species has at least one predator or a reason not to
(toxic, apex, boss), every tier-I species has a producer, no diet cycle is unreachable,
and every corpse has a scavenger.
All numbers live in redtide_species.json , redtide_weapons.json , and
redtide_maps/ ; nothing is inline.
