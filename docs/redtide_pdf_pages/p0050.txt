The web in motion
• Downhill: the aqueduct means the summit's fights are the lower town's problem; a
team that sets one diver below to hold the drains will lose them to the Wyrm.
• Ichor: dead Lost Ones repel sharks and the Wyrm for 60 s and draw crabs, cucumbers,
and the monitor; a street of their dead is a safe corridor for a minute.
• The library: the squid can't leave the stacks; the Locker there is a trade of pulls for
reach.
• The plaza lane: tuna and swordfish cross at speed; standing in the lane is a ram;
standing beside it and shooting a tuna brings the great white over the wall.
• The farms: harmless grazers everywhere, and the crocodile in every canal; the sluice
trap floods a terrace and drowns a Lost One phalanx.
