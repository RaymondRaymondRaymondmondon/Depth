Overview
Red Tide is a four-player 3D survival shooter in the Deep Arcade, built on the bones of
Black Ops 2 and 3 Zombies but with no zombies: the players are divers dropped into living
ecosystems, earning money by killing beasts while the food web, the blood in the water,
and the enemies drawn by their gunfire close in around them. Every map is an ecosystem
of about 40 beasts and 12 flora that eat, hide, breed, and hunt each other whether or not
the players are watching.
