Squad tactics
1. A squad enters at the hull breach and moves Stern -5 Engine Cabins Salon,
checking each room's corners; it will not enter a room with a size-4 beast in it.
2. On contact: the Netman targets the closest diver, the Speargunner takes a firing
position 15 m back, the Cutters flank through the second doorway, the Chummer
throws chum behind the divers so the reef arrives from the direction they retreat to.
