The city has 60+ buildings on a hill 120 m tall and 500 m across, connected by streets,
stairs, and the aqueduct. Traps: a canal sluice (floods a farm terrace), the bathhouse
hypocaust (boils a room), the chapel's god-pool (drains and refills). Hidden quest: rekindle
the lighthouse on the wall to see the whole city on the sonar.
Level mechanics
• The aqueduct carries blood downhill. Blood made on the summit flows down the
aqueduct into the baths and the lower town within a minute. Fighting uphill sends
predators to the players who stayed below.
• Cisterns and drains. A network under every district: conger eels and the Wyrm come
out of any drain grate; grates can be netted (a build) or watched.
• Buildings are homes. Every building type hosts its species by data: amphorae mean
octopus, baths mean grouper, cellars mean cardinalfish, granaries mean bream. The
dossier teaches which door has what behind it.
• The Lost Ones live here. Squads patrol streets, hold plazas, and know the drains. Their
ichor scent draws the city's scavengers and repels its apex beasts, so a street full of
dead Lost Ones is briefly safe from sharks and full of crabs.
• The open plaza on the summit is the only long sightline: the tuna and swordfish lane,
and the Priest's chosen ground.
