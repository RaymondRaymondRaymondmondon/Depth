• Wounded players bleed; downed players bleed heavily. Reviving fast is also ecology.
• Moving out of bloody water is the only real reset; blood follows currents, so the map's
flow tells you where it goes.
• The HUD shows blood as a red tint at the edge of the mask and a small scent meter, and
predators' approach as a pulse from their direction.
The enemy alarm
• Each map region has an alarm value. Gunfire adds the weapon's noise; explosions add
triple; melee adds nothing. It decays 5% per second.
When alarm exceeds x (1 / (1 + 0.1 x tide)) , a roll every 5 s spawns an
enemy patrol at the region's faction entry with chance + 3% x tide (capped at
70%). Higher tides make quieter play matter more.
• Four divers firing in one region fill one alarm four times faster than four divers spread
across four regions. Spreading out lowers enemy pressure but raises blood-in-one-
place risk when someone goes down alone: the coordination trade-off the Sunken Ship
teaches first.
• Enemies that arrive follow the same sound and blood senses as beasts, so they find
you the way the beasts do.
Behavior state machine
Every beast runs: Graze or Rest Investigate Hunt Feed Flee Defend Return
Dead, with transitions driven by hunger, blood, sound, threat size, and home distance.
Schools use boids with a shared leader; packs assign flank and drive roles; swarms are a
single agent with many bodies. Fleeing beasts drop the blood they were about to make: a
wounded fish that escapes leaves a thin trail, which is itself a lure for the next predator.
Population
• Each region has spawn pools per species with a carrying capacity tied to flora density
and tide number.
• Beasts that leave a region to follow blood are replaced slowly at home; beasts killed in a
region are replaced at the region's respawn rate (small fast, apex slow, boss never).
• Predators that overfeed leave; grazers then rebound; predators return. The cycle is
tuned to about 3 minutes so a match feels alive without stalling quotas.
• The boss beast of each map is a single persistent agent with a very high blood
threshold and a home; it wanders more each tide.
