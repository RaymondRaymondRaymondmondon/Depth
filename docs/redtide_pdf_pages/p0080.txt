A season is a data drop: species rows, diet matrix columns, spawn entries, and dossier
pages, with models from the existing body plans.
Weapon handling and the Forged list
Every weapon gets handling numbers (how it feels) and a Forged identity (what it
becomes). The Forge still gives +150% damage and +50% ammo, but each weapon's
Forged form has a name and one changed behavior, the way Pack-a-Punch weapons do.
Handling
Spread is in degrees at rest; ADS is aim-down-sight time in seconds; swim multiplier is
speed while aiming; headshot is the weak-point multiplier for the class (Deadeye raises
every class by 1.0).
