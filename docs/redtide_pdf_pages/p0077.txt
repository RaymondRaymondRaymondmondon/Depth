The divers work for the Owners, a salvage concern that pays scrip for cleared water and
asks no questions. Each map's dossier terminals, chalk marks, and Wrecker chatter drop a
line or two: the Marguerite went down with the captain's safe; the cave swallowed an
expedition whose lanterns still burn; the reef's tribe hunts because the Owners pay them
too; Atlantis's citizens drowned and kept walking; the station bred what it found in the
abyss and the abyss bred back. That's all the story the game needs: the players supply the
rest over voice chat.
