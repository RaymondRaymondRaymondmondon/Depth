Red Tide — Arcade Game 5 Design Document
Level mechanics
• Coral corridors limit fire lanes to 8-12 m; loud guns are wasted, spearguns and knives
shine.
• Eel holes line the corridors: passing within 1 m risks a moray or ribbon eel strike;
Vulture's Eye shows occupied holes.
• Reacher coral grabs a diver who brushes it and holds until a teammate cuts them loose
(or the coral is shot to O); it also grabs beasts and enemies, which feed it.
• The sand flats have no cover and full sightlines: the loud-gun zone, and the place
where a downed diver's blood spreads fastest.
• Dolphins. The pod follows the divers and drives sharks off while nobody has shot a
dolphin. One shot turns the pod hostile for the match.
• Crown-of-thorns eat coral: leaving them alive erodes corridors (opening lanes for
sharks); killing them is 40 blood each.
