Red Tide — Arcade Game 5 Design Document
Animations
• Reloads are underwater and slow-fast-slow: gas pistols pop the cylinder and thumb in
darts one at a time until full or interrupted; needlers swap a glass cartridge with a hiss
of bubbles; powder guns eject a sealed brass clip that sinks; spearguns cock with both
arms and a visible strain; harpoon rifles winch a bolt from a side quiver; the scatter gun
is loaded shell by shell and can be interrupted. Every reload has an empty and a partial
version and a Speed Brine version 30% faster.
• Firing: bubble bursts from gas guns, a brass flash from powder guns, a line snapping
taut from spearguns.
• Inspect animations show the sea-glass Forge finish.
• Weapon skins: arcade tokens buy finishes (barnacle, verdigris, bone inlay, pearl, red
tide) that apply to all weapons.
The Harpoon Cannon (Harpoon Hour)
A shoulder-mounted salvage cannon with infinite bolts for 30 s, 500 damage, noise 10: the
map's loudest sound, which is the joke and the risk.
Tonics, charms, items, builds, and progression
Tonics (perks)
Brass vending machines that dispense a bottle; four can be held (five with a charm). Each
map has 6-8 of the 12 placed by its designer; the first two are always Juggernaut and
Quick Brine.
