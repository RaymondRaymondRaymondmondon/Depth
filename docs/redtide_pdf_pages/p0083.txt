Enemy spawning and Hunts
Alarm squads
1. Each region tracks its alarm (constants in the engine workbook). Above threshold, a
roll every 5 s spawns a squad with the tide's chance; the alarm halves when it
succeeds.
2. The squad spawns at the region's nearest faction entry (each map lists them) that is at
least 35 m from every diver and out of every diver's sight; if none qualifies, at the map's
default entry.
3. Squad size is 3 at tides 4-9, 4 at 10-19, 5 at 20+, drawn from the faction's composition
(never two of the specialist unit in one squad).
4. Squads path toward the region where the alarm fired, then toward the nearest diver's
last known position; they lose the trail after 20 s without sight, scent, or sound, and
then patrol the faction's route.
5. At most 2 alarm squads are alive at once (3 at tide 20+); further rolls are skipped.
6. A squad wiped by beasts counts as resolved: the alarm is not raised by their deaths.
Hunts
• Tide 5, 10, 15, and every 5th tide after: a Faction Hunt unless a Predator Hunt is rolled
(50% from tide 10, from tide 20).
• Faction Hunt. The quota is replaced by "eliminate the Hunt force": 10 units at tide 5, 12
at 10, 14 at 15, 16 at 20+, plus the faction's Hunt leader. The force spawns in three waves
30 s apart from the map's Hunt staging point; the leader spawns with the third wave.
