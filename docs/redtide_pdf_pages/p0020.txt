Shared beast rules
Every beast in every map is built from the same size classes, tiers, and behavior
archetypes, so the 200 species are data on top of a small set of systems. Each map's
tables give a beast's class, size, tier, diet, predators, and a behavior note; everything else
follows from these rules.

Tiers
• Tier 1 grazers, filter feeders, and detritivores: eat flora and corpses; the base of the
web and the safest scrip.
• Tier 2 small predators: eat tier 1; territorial or venomous species here are the early
hazards.
• Tier 3 mid predators: eat tiers 1-2 and each other; the tide 4-9 pressure.
• Tier 4 apex: eat everything below, including enemies and divers; arrive by blood from
tide 4, wander from tide 10.
• Tier 5 boss: one per map, size 6, a home and a habit; a Predator Hunt can call it.
