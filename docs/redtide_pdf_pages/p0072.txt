The Goliath (the Sunken Ship)
• Arena. The Engine Room until tide 12; after that it wanders and the ship is the arena.
15,000 HP; front plates halve damage; the gills behind the plates are the weak point,
exposed for 2 s after an Inhale and after a Tail Slam.
• Phase 1 (100-60%). Inhale (a 1.5 s windup as the gills close and water rushes; pulls one
diver from 4 m into its mouth; the team has 5 s to deal 150 damage to the gills or the
diver is swallowed and downed), Lunge (140, bleed), Boom (a 10-noise pulse that stuns
1 s in 10 m). It stays within 12 m of its home.
• Phase 2 (60-30%). Adds Tail Slam (90, knocks back 6 m, drops loose cargo on three
marked squares), Boom every 3 s, and calls two Groupers. It roams the whole room; the
Forge can't be used while it is within 8 m.
• Phase 3 (30-0%). Enraged: speed Inhale every 8 s, a swallow is an instant down. It
breaks the aft bulkhead and chases into the Stern.
• Arena rules. Shooting the luminous algae mat off the wall (300 damage) blinds it for 6 s
because it hunts by the mat's light; cycling the power switch stuns it 2 s (once per
fight); loose cargo can be shot to drop on it for 200.
• Team plan. One diver baits the Inhale from 4 m; two shoot the gills in the 2 s window;
one handles the Groupers and the cargo. Reward: 1,500 base scrip x the tide multiplier,
a Locker weapon each, and the captain's safe key. It reforms in its home after 6 minutes
