Red Tide — Arcade Game 5 Design Document
The Underwater Cave: the expedition's key
1. Trigger: the Lobster's kill drops the expedition's key.
2. Find the three lanterns the Drowned left burning (one in each dry chamber) and put
them out with an ink cap or an ink bomb; each doused lantern silences the swiftlets for
5 minutes and drops a page of the expedition's log.
3. With the key and three pages, open the Lantern Cache's locked crate in the Cricket
Cavern during a tide (not the calm). The crate takes 15 s and the Drowned come for it (a
mini-Hunt of 6).
4. Reward: a free wonder weapon (the Resonator), 2,000 scrip each, and the Drowned no
longer spawn from the Lantern Cache for the rest of the match (they use the Chimney
instead).
The Coral Reef: the drum
1. Trigger: kill a Shaman (Hunt) and pick up the drum.
2. Carry the drum to Turtle Beach's altar without beating it. A drum-carrier swims at 80%
speed; beating it (a temptation the HUD offers) calls sharks and resets the step.
3. At the altar, survive one tide on the beach while the turtles nest (the altar counts
nests; 5 nests complete it). Raiders target the beach.
4. Reward: the Anemone Gun (Forged), the dolphin pod becomes allied again if it was
hostile, and hammerheads avoid Turtle Beach for the match. This is separate from the
wonder-weapon quest for the Anemone Gun, which stays as written in Modes.
Atlantis: the lighthouse
1. Trigger: read quest terminal 1 (customs house).
2. Read terminals 2-5 (wall fort, scroll store, treasury, crypts) in any order; each reveals
one of the four wall-fires' positions on the rampart.
3. Light the four wall-fires with the reactor-crystal's spark, carried from the treasury in a
jar (the jar breaks if the carrier is downed). The rampart walk is 1,500 m of fire coral and
barracuda.
4. Climb the lighthouse and light it: the whole city appears on the sonar for the rest of the
match (every grate, every Lost One, every apex).
5. Reward: the Wyrm's key (opens the treasury vault: 5,000 scrip split) and the Tide Staff
(Forged) for the lighter.
