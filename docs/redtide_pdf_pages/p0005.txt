Every 5th tide from tide 5 is a Hunt: instead of a quota, the map's enemy faction arrives in
force (see Enemy factions) and the tide ends when they're dead or eaten. Starting at tide
10, a Hunt can also be a Predator Hunt: the map's apex beasts come to the players'
position because the water is bloody enough, and the quota is a fixed number of apex
kills. The reward for either is a Resupply and a guaranteed Locker weapon.
