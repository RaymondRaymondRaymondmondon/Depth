Movement, in one paragraph
Holds
252 diver lines across 21 situations (3 per diver
per situation) plus the voice rules for expanding
each set to 6-10
Divers swim at 2.0 m/s, sprint at 3.4 for 6 s on a stamina bar that only appears while
sprinting and refills in 8 s, rise and dive at 1.4, and reach full speed in 0.4 s. Aiming slows
swimming to 60%; harpoons and spearguns to 40%. In air chambers they walk at 1.6 with
no jump and a 1 m step-up. A hold can be broken early by mashing (six taps) or by a
teammate dealing 15% of the holder's HP. Knockbacks unsettle aim for 0.6 s. Purchases
over 1,000 need a 1 s hold; reviving requires holding still; downed divers drift-crawl at 0.6.
Past the abyss edge the pull lasts 5 s with a warning at the first second.
Progression, in one paragraph
A match pays 10 tokens, a win (tide 10+) 25, and 2 per tide past 10; firsts pay more (tide 20
on a map 50, a boss 30, a quest 100, a full dossier 75). Ranks cost 50 x rankA1.6 tokens
each, so a good match earns 40-70 and rank 50 is about 150 matches. Ranks unlock
charms, skins, pouch slots, and three bonus dossier pages; two charms (Lucky Locker, Fair
Shares) come from tide milestones instead. Cosmetics cost 100-300. Nothing bought or
earned changes in-match power.
The how-to-play page (final text)
One scrolling page in the arcade menu, styled as a page torn from the ship's log, chalk
headings, and one diagram. This is the game's only teaching; nothing in a match explains
itself.
THE OWNERS ' STANDING ORDERS FOR DIVERS
1. The job. You and up to three others go down with a pistol, a knife, two charges, and five
hundred scrip. You kill what lives there and the Owners pay by the head. Every tide sets a
quota; meet it and the next tide is worth more and wants more of you. There is no end.
There is only how deep you get.
