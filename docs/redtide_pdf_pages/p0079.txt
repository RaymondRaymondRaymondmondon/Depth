Wonder weapon quests
The Locker deals every wonder weapon, always. On the Coral Reef, Atlantis, and
Approaching the Void a quest also awards it, guaranteed, to whoever finishes:
• Reef: the Anemone Gun. Feed the Bommiels cleaning station three parasites (brushed
off teammates into a jar), survive one Raider drum without shooting a dolphin, then
carry a live blue-ringed octopus (netted) to the Blue Hole's ledge without being bitten.
• Atlantis: the Tide Staff. Light three chapel braziers with the reactor-crystal's spark
carried from the treasury, hold the plaza through one tuna crossing, and offer a Lost
One's ichor (a Chummer jar filled from a corpse) at the god-pool while the Wyrm is
submerged.
• Void: the Abyssal Lure. Assemble the station's final log from five terminals, re-aim
three lure beacons toward the abyss, and lure the Relict to the reactor vent, where the
scald exposes its belly for one bolt from the Cannon Harpoon.
