Health, downs, revives
• 100 HP, regenerating 4s after the last hit at 20 HP/s (Zombies-style). Juggernaut Tonic
raises HP to 250.
• At O HP a diver is downed: they float with the Cormorant only, for 45 s, bleeding into
the water (a downed diver is a strong blood source, which is the danger and the
drama).
• Any teammate can revive in 4 s (2 s with Quick Brine). A revived diver loses all tonics
unless they hold a Salt Charm that keeps them.
• If every diver is down, the match ends. Solo has one self-revive per Quick Brine bought
(three per match).
• Air. There is no air timer; divers breathe indefinitely. The pressure against camping in a
corner comes from blood, scent, and the enemy alarm instead.
Doors, power, and the map opening up
• Doors and debris cost 750-2,500 scrip and open permanently for everyone. Each map
opens in stages from the start pocket outward.
• Power (the generator, the tide mill, the temple's crystal) turns on the Forge, the
Locker's second location, tonic machines beyond the first two, and the map's traps.
