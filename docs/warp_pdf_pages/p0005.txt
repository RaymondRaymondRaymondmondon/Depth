Warp Dodgeball — Game Design Spec
• Alive enemy ball is predicted to hit you within 0.6 s, and
• it is coming from within 1000 of the direction you're facing, and
• you are standing, crouching, or jumping (not diving, recovering, squatting, or on a
ladder), and
• your hands are empty. Holding a ball, you can block instead: the ball is deflected and
goes dead. If the hit knocks your ball out of your hands, you are out.
Only the most urgent threat gets a prompt. The prompt is a shrinking ring around the
incoming ball with the catch button icon.

The window is centered on the predicted moment of contact.
Outcomes
• Pressed inside the window: catch. The thrower is out, your first eliminated teammate
returns, and you now hold the ball.
• Pressed outside the window (early or late): fumble. The ball pops off you and you are
out.
• Not pressed: an ordinary hit, so you are out unless you dodged.
Because pressing early is as bad as getting hit, players must decide between catching and
dodging, which is the core tension of dodgeball.
Portal system
Each player owns one linked pair of portals (A and B). A ball that enters one exits the
other at the same speed, turned to match the exit portal's facing. Only balls travel through
portals; players cannot, which keeps the center-line rule meaningful.
