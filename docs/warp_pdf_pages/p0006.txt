Warp Dodgeball — Game Design Spec
• Pressing portal A or B fires an instant ray from the camera. The portal is placed if the
hit surface is portal-able and a 1.0 m x 1.6 m oval fits flat on it.
• Portal-able surfaces are light gray panels; everything else is dark and rejects portals.
This lets level design control which angles exist.
• Portals can go anywhere valid, including the opponent's side of the arena.
• Placing A again moves your existing A. Cooldown: 1.0 s per portal.
• Your portals are tinted in your team color, light for A and dark for B, with your player
number on the rim. Opponents see them too.
When you are eliminated, your portals close. A ball in mid-transit finishes its trip.
• Portals are only active when both ends of the pair exist.
Transit (the math Claude Code needs)
Trigger when the ball's center crosses the portal plane inside the oval.
• New position = exit transform x 1800 turn about the portal's up axis x inverse of entry
transform x old position.
• Velocity and spin are rotated by the same rotation. Speed is unchanged.
• For 0.1 s after exiting, the ball ignores collisions with the wall behind the exit portal.
Anyone's ball can go through anyone's portals. That includes the enemy's, so a portal
placed carelessly can be used against you.
• Portal views are rendered so players can see through them (a second camera per
portal rendering to a texture). Recursive views are capped at 1 level.
Rebound out (the risk rule)
A live ball that hits its own thrower after passing through any portal, or after bouncing off
a wall, gets the thrower out. In practice this happens when a player throws too hard
through a portal that faces a nearby wall: the ball keeps enough speed to bounce back
through the portal pair toward them. Lighter throws lose more energy and die on the floor
first, so power is a choice, not a default.
To stop instant self-hits on release, the thrower is immune to their own ball for 0.3 s after
release unless it has already been through a portal.
Ball physics and elimination rules
A ball is live from release until it touches the ground; walls, ceilings, and obstacles do not
kill it, which is what makes bank shots and portal rebounds possible.
