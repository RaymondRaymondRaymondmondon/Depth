Overview
Warp Dodgeball is team dodgeball where every player carries a portal device: throw a ball
into one of your portals and it flies out the other, aimed at an opponent from an angle they
never saw coming. The catch is that the physics are honest, so a throw that is too hard can
rebound off a wall, come back through your portal, and knock you out.
Core loop: grab a ball place your two portals charge and release a throw (straight,
curved, or through a portal) -Y dodge, duck, dive, or catch incoming balls -5 last team
standing wins the round.
Design pillars
• Readable physics. Balls arc, bounce, and curve the same way every time, so players
can learn bank shots and portal angles.
• Skill in the release. Power (how long you hold), spin (curve), and portal placement are
the three things a good player masters.
• Risk and reward. Every powerful option has a cost: overcharged throws lose accuracy,
portal shots can come back at you, and a fumbled catch gets you out.
How to use this spec with Claude Code: build the milestones in order. Each one ends in a
playable state with acceptance tests. Numbers in this doc (speeds, timings, sizes) are
starting values meant to be tuned, and should live in one config file rather than scattered
through code.
