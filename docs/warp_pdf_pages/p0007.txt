2. Live: thrown, and tagged with the thrower and team. Can get players out.
3. Dead: harmless. A ball becomes dead when it touches the ground, hits a player, is
caught, or is blocked. "Ground" means any walkable surface: the floor and the tops of
platforms and cover blocks (any surface tilted less than 450). Entering a floor portal
does not count as touching the ground.
How a player gets out
1. Hit by a live enemy ball anywhere on the body.
2. Hit by their own live ball after it went through a portal or bounced off a wall (rebound
out).
3. Their throw is caught by an opponent.
4. They fumble a catch.
5. A hit knocks the ball out of their hands while blocking.
6. Their feet cross the center line.
A ball only gets one player out per flight; after the first hit it goes dead. Friendly fire is off
by default (teammates' balls pass harmlessly), with a lobby setting to turn it on. A lobby
setting can also make headshots not count.
