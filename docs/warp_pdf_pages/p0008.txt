Warp Dodgeball — Game Design Spec
Arenas
Both arenas are mirror-symmetric so neither team has an advantage, and both are
enclosed so balls stay in play.
Classic court
• Floor: 18 m x 9 m, with a center line and an attack line 3 m back on each side. A2 m run-
off strip surrounds the court.
• Walls 6m high on all sides; flat ceiling at 8 m.
• Portal-able panels: the full end wall behind each team, the upper half (3 to 6 m) of both
side walls, and the whole ceiling. The floor is not portal-able in Classic.
• Nothing else. The mode is about pure aim, timing, and portal angles.
