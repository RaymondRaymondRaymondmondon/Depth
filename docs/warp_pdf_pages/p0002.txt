Match structure (both modes)
• Teams of 1 to 6 per side (default 4v4). Each team stays on its own half; feet crossing the
center line is an out.
Opening rush: balls start lined up on the center line. On the whistle, both teams sprint
for them. A ball grabbed in the rush must be carried back behind your attack line (3 m
from center) before it can be thrown.
• Around ends when one team has no players left, or after 3 minutes. On timeout, the
team with more players standing wins; a tie goes to sudden death (one ball, no portals).
• A match is best of 5 rounds.
• Eliminated players wait on their team's sideline in catch order: when a teammate
makes a catch, the first player in line returns.
Player movement and controls
Every evasive move trades safety for something: lower hitbox for less speed, a long dive
for a vulnerable recovery. The player is a capsule 1.8 m tall and 0.5 m wide; the hitbox
shrinks with posture.
