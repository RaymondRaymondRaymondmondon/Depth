Level 6: Lights Out
• Look: Total darkness. Concrete corridors that can only be seen by what you bring. The
air is still; there's no hum.
• Generation: A long, winding, mostly linear set of corridors with occasional rooms; few
landmarks.
• Mood rule — The Dark: no light sources work for long (batteries drain sounds are
louder; sanity drains Teammates' voices are the only comfort, and the voices are
sometimes not teammates. A glow stick lasts 30 s.
• Hazards: Everything, unseen; pits; the voices.
• Entities: Smilers everywhere (eyes and teeth in the dark), Hounds by SOUnd, Skin-
Stealers imitating voices.
• Loot: Few items, all valuable: a glowing lantern (worth a lot and works here), an old
researcher's camera with film, a box of batteries.
• Labs: Lab Eta (dormant), a Lab that has never been lit; restarting it is the hardest task
in week three.
• Exits: A door at the end that opens onto water (Level 7); a pit (Level 8); walk long
enough and you're in Level O (the only way back without a Lab).
Level 7: Thalassophobia
• Look: A small room or two of an old house, and beyond them, an endless dark ocean.
The house's lower floors are flooded.
• Generation: A few rooms above the water, a flooded basement below, and the ocean (a
fixed-size open water area with a floor far below).
• Mood rule — The Water: most of the level is underwater; swimming drains stamina;
diving needs a breathing tank (shop); something very large lives in it and anything in
the water for more than 60 s draws it. Loot is in the flooded house and on the sea floor.
• Hazards: Drowning; the cold (stamina drain); the thing in the water.
• Entities: The Leviathan of Level 7 (one enormous presence that pulls swimmers down;
a player taken reappears in Level 8, see Death), Deathmoths around the house's lamps.
• Loot: High and heavy: a ship's bell, waterlogged chests, a diving helmet, the house's
family silver.
