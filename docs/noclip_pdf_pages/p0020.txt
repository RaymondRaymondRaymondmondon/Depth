Level 18: Nostalgic Memories
•
•
•
Look: A place made of childhood: a school, a playground, a living room from the
nineties, a birthday party, a summer camp. Bright, warm, slightly wrong; music from a
toy.
Generation: Rooms and outdoor scenes from a kit of "memories", stitched together
without logic; a schoolhouse is the hub.
Mood rule — Comfort: Level 18 restores sanity (+1 per 30 s just by being here) and
that's the trap: it's hard to leave. A player who stays more than 10 minutes gets the Stay
prompt every minute, and if they accept it once by accident they're gone for the day
(they appear back on the Surface in the morning, with nothing, saying they had a lovely
time).
Hazards: The Stay prompt; rooms that loop; toys that are noisy loot.
Entities: The Friends (child-sized figures who want to play; harmless; they take your
loot to their "clubhouse" if you put it down), Partygoers (the birthday party is theirs).
Loot: High, sentimental, and strange: a toy collection, a lunchbox, a yearbook (with
your name in it), a game console, a birthday cake (it's fine).
Labs: Lab Sigma, in the school's gym.
Exits: The schoolhouse front door (Level 4); a slide in the playground (Level 9); a closet
(Level 19).
Level 19: Abyss Inn
• Look: An old inn on the edge of a chasm with no bottom: a lobby with a fire, a bar,
rooms upstairs, a balcony over the abyss, and below it, nothing.
• Generation: An inn (rooms from Level 51s kit, rotted) built along a cliff edge, with rooms
that hang over the abyss on stilts and corridors that end in air.
• Mood rule — The Edge: the abyss pulls: standing within 3 m of the edge for more than 5
s drains sanity fast and, at low sanity, walks you toward it. Falling in is death (you
respawn on the Surface; your carry is gone). The inn is the deepest Lab and the end of
the map: the most valuable loot in the game is in its rooms and on its ledges.
• Hazards: The edge; rotten floors over the abyss; the innkeeper's bell.
