NOCLIP — Arcade Game 10 Design Document
Injuries
Hits do health damage and sometimes an injury: a sprain (no sprint until a bandage), a
bleed (-1 health per 3 s until a bandage; leaves a trail that Hounds follow), a break (half
speed until a splint), electrocution (a 2 s stun, drops what you're holding).
Death
• At O health a player is downed: they crawl, they can talk (proximity), and they bleed
out over 40 s unless a teammate revives them (6 s with a medkit; 15 s without, and the
reviver can't do anything else).
A dead player drops everything they carried and becomes a Wanderer: a ghost who
can drift through walls at walking speed, see entities through walls, and talk to the
living only in a whisper they can barely hear within 3 m. Wanderers can't touch
anything but can stand somewhere and be an arrow. They respawn at the Surface next
morning.
• Some entities don't kill; they take. A player taken by a Skin-Stealer is replaced by one
(see Entities). A player swallowed by Level 7's water reappears on Level 8 a minute
later with no gear and IO sanity. A player who noclips while Lost is alive and alone
somewhere.
Threshold Labs
The Labs are the crew's ship, split into outposts across the map. Each is an abandoned
research station from whoever studied the Backrooms before the Bureau: concrete, steel
doors, banks of CRT monitors, chalkboards of equations, a generator, and in the center a
ring of machinery around a portal that opens to the Surface. Every level has one or two;
most start dormant and have to be found and restarted.
