• Each level has a population budget per hour that rises after 18:00 and triples in
Overtime. The forecast says which levels are active today.
• Entities move between levels only through the same exits players use (a Hound pack
can follow a crew down a stairwell).
• Entities that see each other mostly ignore each other; Smilers avoid Skin-Stealers;
Hounds chase Facelings; that's enough to be exploitable.
• Photographs: a Bureau camera (shop) can photograph an entity for the dossier: a first
photo of each entity pays a bounty and unlocks its counter tip in the Archive. The
photo has to be in frame, lit, and within 15 m, which is the problem.
Loot
Loot is objects that were lost into the Backrooms from the real world, plus a few things
that only exist there. Every item has a size, a weight, a value range (rolled when found),
and sometimes a property. Values rise with level number; the deepest loot is worth ten
times the shallowest.
