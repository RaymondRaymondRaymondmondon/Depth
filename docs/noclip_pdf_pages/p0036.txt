NOCLIP — Arcade Game 10 Design Document
Progression
Between campaigns a profile keeps: the dossier (entities photographed), Labs ever
restarted, the best week reached, exits learned (as kinds), and the Bureau rank (Intern -Y
Salvager -Y Senior Salvager -Y Field Lead -Y Liminal Specialist), which unlocks starting gear
(a flashlight at Salvager; a radio at Senior; a scanner at Field Lead) and cosmetic titles.
Cosmetics
Arcade tokens (10 per day survived, 50 per week's quota met, 25 per new entity
photographed) buy suit colors, hard-hat stickers, vest patches, and headlamp colors. The
crate (one token) holds 40 items in the arcade's USUal four tiers: commons (sticker sets,
colored vests), rares (a Faceling mask that's just a mask, a party hat, a wet-carpet texture
suit), super rares (a Smiler grin on the hard hat that glows, a hi-vis Hound costume), and
specials (the Yellow Wallpaper suit, the Bureau Director's coat, the Exit Sign hat, the
Almond Water Bottle costume, a working lava lamp on the hard hat). Cosmetics never
change the numbers, though a glowing hard hat is a light source and the Smilers know it.
Presentation, generation, sound, and build order
Look
NOCLIP has its own style, specific to this game (other Slop games choose their own):
found-footage liminal. Low-poly first-person 3D with flat, overlit textures (the
Backrooms' wallpaper, carpet, and concrete as procedurally generated tiling patterns), a
VHS post-process (scanlines, chroma bleed, a timestamp in the corner that's the in-game
clock), and fluorescent light that flickers. It deliberately doesn't use Depth's ink: these
games are from somewhere else. The Labs are the one place that looks designed (brass
and steel, the Depth palette), and the Surface is ordinary sodium-lit night.
