Overview
NOCLIP is a 1-6 player first-person co-op horror scavenging game on the Deep Arcade's
new Slop shelf: the games that are knowingly a little cursed, a little internet, and very fun
with friends. The crew are salvagers hired by a shady firm to go into the Backrooms (the
endless liminal spaces behind reality), find things worth money, and get them out. There's
no ship. Instead, scattered through the levels are Threshold Labs: abandoned industrial
research stations, each with a portal back to the real world. Labs are where you drop loot,
buy gear, rest, and signal the portal to charge so the crew can extract.
What it takes from Lethal Company
• Co-op scavenging under a quota: bring back enough value in a set number of days or
you're fired (and in this case, left behind).
• Scrap with weight and value that has to be carried by hand, so greed is a choice.
• Proximity voice: you hear teammates only when they're near, and the things in the dark
hear you.
• Death that is funny for everyone else, and a ghost to watch from.
A shop between runs; cheap gear that matters; a crew that gets better at the same
awful places.
What it takes from the Backrooms
• The first ten levels (0-9) of the Backrooms wiki's numbered list: the yellow Lobby, the
warehouse Habitable Zone, Pipe Dreams, the Electrical Station, the Abandoned Office,
the Terror Hotel, Lights Out, Thalassophobia, the Cave System, and the Darkened
Suburbs, each with its mood, its hazards, and its entities.
• Noclipping: moving between levels by falling through reality at the wrong spot, plus
the doors, stairwells, and strange exits each level has.
• Almond water, the hum, the smell of wet carpet, and entities that are a meme until one
is behind you.
