Anomalous objects
Rare items (one in fifty) with a function that's also loot:
• Almond Water (Pure): heals all meters fully; worth more sold than drunk.
• The Lucky Die: roll it; a random effect for the day (good or bad).
• A Bureau Compass That Works: points to the nearest Lab on any level.
• The Smiler's Tooth: Smilers ignore the carrier; it's worth a lot and it whispers.
• The Exit Sign: placed on any door, it makes that door an exit to the level of the crew's
choice, once.
• A Faceling's Mask: the wearer is ignored by Facelings and Dullers; Skin-Stealers want
it.
• The Empty Room Key: opens a room that wasn't there (a pocket room: safe storage for
the day).
• The Hotel Penthouse Key: opens the penthouse on Level 5 (a vault of jewelry and the
Terror Hotel's worst guest).
