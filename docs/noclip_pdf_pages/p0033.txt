NOCLIP — Arcade Game 10 Design Document
Selling
• Extracted loot comes through the portal in the Bureau's crate onto the loading bay.
• The Bureau buys for quota credit at a fixed rate (the item's base value). Only Bureau
sales count toward the quota. The Bureau pays 20% of credit as cash too.
• The Fence (a man in a van called Dez) buys for cash at a rate that changes daily
("crystals are hot, office chairs aren't"); he pays more than the Bureau for anomalous
and cursed items and asks no questions. Fence sales don't count toward the quota.
• The split is the decision: quota keeps the campaign alive; cash buys the gear that
makes the next quota possible.
The quota
Week 1: 600 credit. Each week +40% (week 2: 840; week 3: 1,180; week 4: 1,650; week 5:
2,300). A crew that hits the quota by day 2 can spend day 3 on cash or on contracts. The
whiteboard shows the quota, credit so far, days left, and a running list of everything sold.
Contracts
Each morning the Bureau offers one contract from a list; it pays a bonus in credit and cash
if completed that day.
