The shape: Levels 0-4 are a tangle near the start; 5-8 are a descent; 9 is the deepest,
reached only through the caves (or rarely from the office). The way home is always either
a Lab or a path up.
The map screen
Each player carries a Bureau field map (the M key): a hand-drawn chart that fills in as the
crew explores. It shows the current level's rooms the crew has seen (shared, if they're
within proximity range when they see them, or synced at a Lab), every exit found with its
destination (once used or once its Archive fragment is read), Labs (dormant or online),
and the crew's positions (only if a teammate has a radio). Sanity below 30 distorts the
map by one room. A level's map resets each day (new seed), but exits learned stay learned
as kinds ("the manila room is always the noclip to 1").
