Pacing of a session
A session is 2-4 days (45-90 minutes). Nothing is gated: all twenty levels are reachable
from day one through their exits, and a crew can go as deep as it dares whenever it wants.
The quota is what pushes. A typical crew makes the first quota working Levels 0-3, the
second by reaching 5-7, and may hold at 7-8 for the third; after that the quotas can only
be met with deep loot, so Levels 10-19 become the job. Level 11 is the deep hub, and Lab
Omega at Level 191s Abyss Inn is the campaign's long goal. Quotas grow 40% a week to
week four and 35% after, and the only way to keep up is to go deeper.
The survivor
Every player is a Bureau salvager in a hard hat and a hi-vis vest with a headlamp, a
backpack, and three meters: health, sanity, and stamina. There is no hunger or thirst; the
Backrooms wear you down through your mind, not your stomach. The meters are simple;
together they're the run's clock.
