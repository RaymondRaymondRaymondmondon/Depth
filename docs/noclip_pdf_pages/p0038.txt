Tests and targets
--noclip-gen <level> <seed> prints a level's layout and asserts reachability from
every Lab to every exit.
--noclip-sim <crew> <days> runs a bot crew and reports: credit per day, deaths by
cause, levels reached, quota met.
• A new crew meets week one's quota about 80% of the time and week three's about
40%; a veteran crew reaches week five.
• Deaths: entities 50%, hazards 25%, getting lost or Overtime 15%, teammates 10% (it
happens).
