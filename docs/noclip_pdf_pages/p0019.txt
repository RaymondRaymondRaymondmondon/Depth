NOCLIP — Arcade Game 10 Design Document
Level 16: J. Barry Mental Asylum
• Look: A decaying 1950s asylum: tiled corridors, padded cells, a day room with a TV, an
administration wing, restraint beds, peeling paint.
• Generation: Wings of cells around day rooms; an administration block; a basement
with the boiler and the records.
• Mood rule — Lockdown: at intervals the asylum's bell rings and every door locks for a
minute; anyone in a corridor is out in the open, anyone in a cell is safe and trapped.
Sanity drains at low sanity, the PA speaks to you by name.
• Hazards: Lockdowns; cells that lock from outside; the boiler.
• Entities: The Patients (figures in gowns who follow at a distance and stop when seen,
like the Neighbors; in groups they block corridors), Wretches in the basement, The
Warden (a single entity in the administration wing who walks the halls during
lockdowns with keys; if it finds a player in a corridor, it puts them in a cell).
• Loot: High: patient files (the Bureau pays for records), restraints, an old radio, the
administrator's safe.
• Labs: Lab Pi, in the basement's records room.
• Exits: The boiler (Level 13); the front gate (Level 14); the chapel's altar (Level 19).
Level 17: Abandoned Carrier
• Look: An enormous aircraft carrier adrift on a grey sea: a flight deck with derelict
aircraft, a hangar deck, endless steel corridors below, bunk rooms, the bridge.
• Generation: A fixed carrier hull with generated interior decks and corridors; the flight
deck and bridge are set pieces.
• Mood rule — The Ship: the carrier rolls with the sea, and the angle changes what's
loose and where water goes; bulkhead doors seal compartments; the lower decks flood
as the day goes on. The flight deck is open and windswept: the best loot (aircraft parts)
and nowhere to hide.
• Hazards: Flooding compartments, falling cargo when the ship rolls, the flight deck's
edge, the elevators.
• Entities: The Crew (Skin-Stealer sailors who wear uniforms and run drills), Hounds in
the hangar, something under the hull (the sea is Level 7's sea).
• Loot: Very high and heavy: aircraft instruments, a jet's black box, the ship's bell, the
armory's crates, the captain's log.
• Labs: Lab Rho, in the ship's sick bay.
