Deep loot values and the quota
Deep loot runs 150-2,500 per item (the inn's ledger is the most valuable thing in the
game), which is what makes week five's quota (2,300) and week eight's (6,400) reachable.
Quotas past week four grow 35% a week instead of 40%. Lab restarts in the deep levels
need two fuel canisters and a part found in the level (a fuse from 15, a ward key from 14, a
ship's breaker from 17).
