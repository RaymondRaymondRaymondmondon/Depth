NOCLIP — Arcade Game 10 Design Document
• Jumping. At any online Lab's portal, a player can jump to any other online Lab: a IO s
charge (quieter than an extraction, about a third of the hum), then they step out of the
destination Lab's ring. One jump per player per in-game hour. A player can jump
carrying what they hold; the crate doesn't travel.
• Rejoining the crew. The network's main use is the one the crew will use constantly: a
player who has pushed ahead and restarted a deep Lab can jump back to the Lab
where teammates are, and a teammate can jump forward to them. Splitting up and
regrouping is cheap, so a crew can SCOUt in pairs and meet at a Lab.
• Shared state. Every online Lab shows the same crate total (each Lab's crate is still its
own physical store, but the Portal Hall's board shows all of them), the same map
(everything any teammate has seen syncs at any Lab), the same Observation feeds
(any Lab's monitors can switch to any other Lab's cameras), and the same intercom (a
player at one Lab can talk to a player at another).
• Extracting from anywhere. An extraction at any Lab sends every crate in the network
to the Surface, not just its own, as long as the other Labs' generators are running. A
crew can drop loot all over the map and cash it out from the Lab nearest its last stand.
• Calling the crew. A player at a Lab can summon: everyone else gets a prompt to jump
there (each still on their own hourly jump), which is how a scattered crew regroups for
a deep Lab's extraction.
• When the network fails. A Lab whose generator dies drops off the network (its crate is
stuck until it's refuelled; its jumps are offline). In Overtime the network goes down
entirely until 06:00. Level 121s Lab is the exception noted in that level.
The ten levels
Each level is procedurally generated from its own rules every day (a seed per day per
level), with fixed landmarks (its Labs, its exits, a handful of set pieces) so a crew can learn
it without memorizing it. Difficulty rises with the number. Every level has a mood rule that
makes it play unlike the others.
Level O: The Lobby
• Look: Endless rooms of mono-yellow wallpaper, damp beige carpet, buzzing
fluorescent panels; no windows, no furniture, no end.
• Generation: A maze of rooms and corridors on a grid, with random wall removals so it's
never quite a maze; pillars; occasional rooms of a different size.
