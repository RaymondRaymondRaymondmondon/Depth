The Surface
The real world is the Bureau's warehouse on an industrial estate at night: a loading bay, a
portal ring identical to the Labs', a sales counter, the Fence's van in the car park, a vending
machine, and a whiteboard. It's the crew's safe room and its spreadsheet.
