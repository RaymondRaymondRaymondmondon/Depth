NOCLIP — Arcade Game 10 Design Document
• Mood rule — Triage: the hospital heals: beds restore health, the pharmacy has every
medical item in the game, and a player who lies on a gurney for 10 s is fully healed. It
also admits: a player who's low on health is paged by name over the PA, and something
comes to take them to surgery.
• Hazards: Locked wards; the morgue's drawers; the PA (it says where you are).
• Entities: The Orderlies (tall figures in scrubs who come for paged players and wheel
them away on a gurney: a player taken is found on an operating table in the theater
with half health and a missing item; or a kidney, and a note), Dullers in the waiting
rooms.
• Loot: High: medical equipment, a pharmacy's stock, surgical instruments, the morgue's
tagged personal effects (valuable, cursed).
• Labs: Lab Xi, in the hospital's records room.
• Exits: The ambulance bay (Level 11); a morgue drawer (Level 16); an elevator (Level 15).
Level 15: Futuristic Halls
• Look: Clean white corridors with glowing panels, sliding doors, terminals, and
holographic signs; someone's idea of the future, empty and humming.
• Generation: A grid of corridors and rooms with automated doors; terminals; a central
control room.
• Mood rule — The System: the level is run by a computer. Terminals show maps, open
doors, and lock them; they also log the crew's position, and the system's security
responds. A crew that hacks a terminal (a 15 s mini-game: match the pattern) controls a
section; a crew that fails is locked in.
• Hazards: Sliding doors that close on you; security drones that tase (electrocution);
decontamination rooms (sealed, gassed briefly).
• Entities: Sentries (security robots that patrol on routes and shoot taser darts;
disabled by EMP or hacking), Facelings in lab coats.
• Loot: Very high, light: data drives, prototype devices (anomalous), a robot's core, a
holographic projector.
• Labs: Lab Omicron, which is already powered (it's part of the system) but locked; a
hack opens it.
• Exits: An elevator (Level 14 or 17); a maintenance hatch (Level 13); the control room's
hidden door (Level 18).
