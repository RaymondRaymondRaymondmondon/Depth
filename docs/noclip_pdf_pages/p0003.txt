NOCLIP — Arcade Game 10 Design Document
A day
1. Briefing (the Surface). The crew is in the Bureau's warehouse in the real world. They
see the quota, the days left, today's contract (an optional job with a bonus), and the
forecast (which levels are "active" today: more loot, more entities). They buy gear with
money from previous sales.
2. Insertion. The Bureau's portal drops the crew into a Threshold Lab chosen by the crew
from those they've unlocked. Every crew starts its first day at Lab Alpha, on Level O.
3. The shift. About 12-18 real minutes of in-game time (a day is 06:00 to 24:00 Backrooms
time; the clock is on every watch). The crew explores, scavenges, carries loot back to a
Lab, moves between levels, and comes back. The Backrooms get worse as the day goes
on: more entities after 18:00; the lights flicker more; after midnight anyone still out is in
the Overtime (below).
4. Extraction. At any Lab the crew can signal the portal: a 30 s charge-up the crew has to
defend (the portal's hum is the loudest thing in the level and draws entities). When it
opens, everyone in the Lab is pulled to the Surface with everything stored in the Lab's
drop-off.
5. The Surface (end of day). Loot is sold: to the Bureau for quota credit, or to the Fence
for cash (see The Surface). The crew restocks. The next day starts from the Lab they
extracted from, or any other unlocked Lab.
Overtime
If the clock passes 24:00 with crew still in the Backrooms, the portal network shuts until
06:00. Anyone outside a Lab is stuck for the night: the lights go out level-wide, entity
activity triples, and the only safe places are Labs with their generators running. Surviving
the night in a Lab with loot is a legitimate (insane) strategy: the morning's extraction
counts for the next day. Surviving it outside a Lab is a story.
How a run ends
• The quota is met at week's end: the crew is paid, a bonus is offered to the player who
brought in the most (a cosmetic token bonus), and a new week begins with a higher
quota and a new forecast.
• The quota is missed: campaign over; a score screen shows weeks survived, total loot,
entities documented, levels reached, and how everyone died.
