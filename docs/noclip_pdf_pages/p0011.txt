• Mood rule — Heat: the heat drains stamina (sprints are short and recovery is slow)

copper pipe section (heavy, valuable).
• Labs: Lab Gamma (dormant), behind a valve-sealed door.
• Exits: A ladder up through a manhole (Level 3); a pipe that's big enough to crawl (Level
4); a steam vent noclip (Level 1).
Level 3: The Electrical Station
• Look: Brick and concrete halls full of humming machinery, transformers, conveyor
belts, cages of wiring; the air smells of ozone; very hot; loud.
• Generation: A grid of machine halls with catwalks above and crawlspaces below;
breakers; locked cages.
• Mood rule — Power: the whole level runs on breakers. Throwing breakers changes
which doors open, which machines run (noise covers footsteps), and which floors are
electrified. A crew with a map of breakers controls the level.
• Hazards: Live floors (the conveyor halls), arc flashes, crushing machinery, heat
(stamina drains and the shimmer blurs vision past 20 m), noise that hides entities'
tells•
• Entities: Hounds, Smilers in powered-down halls, Skin-Stealers (begin appearing here;
see Entities), Deathmoths (eggs in warm corners).
• Loot: High: fuses, breakers, a working radio, copper coils (heavy), machine parts, a
transformer core (huge, two players, very valuable).
• Labs: Lab Delta (dormant), in a control room above the main hall.
• Exits: An elevator that works when powered (Level 4); a ladder down to Level 2; a
freight door to Level 1; a transformer room noclip (Level 6, unpowered = very dark).
