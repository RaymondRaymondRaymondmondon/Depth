Sound
The hum (Level O's fluorescent buzz) is the game's identity and changes per level: the
hum, warehouse drips, pipe hiss and clank, the station's transformer roar, office HVAC
and a distant phone, hotel jazz and a vacuum somewhere, Lights Out's total silence and
your own breathing, the house's creak and the ocean, the caves' drips and echoes, the
suburbs' crickets and a far-off dog and the ice cream truck. Entities each have a tell (the
Hounds' panting, the Smiler's faint giggle, the Partygoers' music, the Duller drone, the
Clump's clank, Deathmoth wings, spiders' clicks, the Leviathan's groan). Proximity voice is
reverb-processed by the room. Hallucinated sounds use the same assets, which is the
trick.
