What it does
A row of lockers and a conveyor that takes loot into
the Bureau's sealed crate. Anything in the crate
goes to the Surface on extraction and counts
toward the day's sale. Loot in the crate is safe from
everything except a Lab breach
A vending terminal: the shop (see Equipment).
Prices are Lab-specific (a Lab deep in Level 6
charges more for batteries and less for almond
water)
Sleep for 30 s to restore 20 sanity once per day; the
only rest in the Backrooms
Powers the Lab's lights, doors, and portal.
Generators need fuel (a canister found on most
levels) to run past the day; a Lab with a dead
generator is dark, its doors don't lock, and its portal
can't be signalled
CRT monitors on cameras the Lab placed in its
level: the crew can watch hallways, see entities on
their routes, and talk over an intercom to anyone
near a camera. A player at the monitors is the
crew's guide, Lethal Company's terminal role
Filing cabinets of the old researchers' notes: lore,
and at each Lab one map fragment of how its level
connects to others
Restarting a dormant Lab
1. Find it (the Bureau's map shows roughly where; the level's exits and the old
researchers' arrows point to it).
2. Get inside: the blast door needs power from a breaker found somewhere in the level,
or a crowbar and two players (loud).
3. Fuel the generator (one canister) and flip the breakers.
4. The Lab is now online for the rest of the campaign: insertion point, drop-off, shop, rest,
cameras, and an extraction point.
