The deep levels (10-19)
The next ten levels in the Backrooms wiki's numbered list, part of the same map and
reachable at any time; the rising quota is what drives a crew down into them. They are
bigger, stranger, and worth far more; each has a Lab that is harder to restart, and two of
them break the rules the crew has learned. Names follow the wiki's list; where the wiki's
take on a level is thin or varied, the game builds from the name and premise.
Level 10: The Bumper Crop
• Look: An endless wheat field under an overcast sky; a wind that never stops; a barn, a
silo, a scarecrow on a pole every few hundred meters; a dirt road.
• Generation: Open terrain with wheat at shoulder height (sight 3 m inside it, 200 m
above it), roads, farm buildings as landmarks.
• Mood rule — The Wheat: inside the wheat nobody can see anything, including entities;
the wheat moves in the wind and also when something walks through it, so the only tell
is a wave that goes against the wind. Climbing a silo or a barn roof gives the view;
standing there makes you the only thing visible for a mile.
• Hazards: Getting lost (the compass works here, the only deep level where it does); a
combine harvester that drives the road and the field on a route; storms that flatten the
wheat for a minute.
• Entities: The Scarecrows (they're on different poles each time you look; one per field
comes down at dusk), Hounds in the wheat.
• Loot: Medium-high, heavy: farm tools, a tractor battery, sacks of grain (the Bureau pays
for Backrooms grain), a barn's tack, the farmhouse's quilt.
