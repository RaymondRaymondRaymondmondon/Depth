NOCLIP — Arcade Game 10 Design Document
Routes
A day's plan is a route: Lab levels Lab. A good route has a Lab at each end, passes
through one high-loot level, avoids today's active-entity levels in the forecast, and comes
home before 18:00. The Observation Room's cameras and the Archive's map fragments
make better routes possible, which is the progression a crew feels.
Entities
Entities are rare, readable, and different from each other: every one has a sense it hunts
by, a tell, and a counter. They're the Backrooms' shared cast reinterpreted as game
behavior, and the Bureau's dossier (a field guide in the Archive) fills in as the crew
photographs each one.
