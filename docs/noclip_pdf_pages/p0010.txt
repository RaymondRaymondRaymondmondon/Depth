Mood rule — The Hum: the lights' buzz drains sanity slowly (-1 per 90 s extra); silence
(a room with a dead light) restores it, and dead lights are where Smilers are. Direction
is meaningless: the compass spins.
Hazards: Getting lost; dead-light rooms; carpet stains that are damp and squelch
(noise); the occasional manila room (a different wallpaper: noclip spot).
Entities: Rare. A Faceling or two wandering; a Smiler in dark rooms after 18:00.
Loot: Low value, light, everywhere: abandoned backpacks, VHS tapes, a working
flashlight, an office chair, almond water, someone's wallet.
Labs: Lab Alpha (the start).
Exits: Noclip through a manila-room wall (to Level 1); a stairwell door that's sometimes
there (to Level 2); fall through the floor in a carpet-stain room (to Level 1, painfully).
Level 1: The Habitable Zone
Look: A vast concrete warehouse: grey floors and walls, exposed rebar, puddles, steel
crates on racks, fluorescent lights that occasionally flicker off for minutes. Fog in low
rooms.
Generation: Large halls connected by corridors and ramps; supply crates in clusters;
stairwells.
Mood rule — Supply Crates: crates spawn and despawn when nobody's looking: a
room checked is a room that might have changed. Crates hold the level's loot and
supplies (almond water, batteries, food), so Level 1 is where a crew restocks.
Hazards: Lights-out periods (2-5 minutes; entities move); fog rooms (sight 5 m).
Entities: Hounds (in packs of 2-3 during lights-out), Dullers (slow, grey, everywhere
when the lights are off), Facelings (wanderers; some trade).
Loot: Medium value: crates of tools, a generator part, a crate of almond water (sells
well), old Bureau equipment, a Faceling's mask (very valuable, see Entities).
Labs: Lab Beta (dormant), near a large hall with a loading door.
Exits: Stairwells down (Level 2); a loading-bay door that opens onto Level 3 (rare);
noclip through a flickering wall (Level O or 4).
Level 2: Pipe Dreams
• Look: Long, narrow maintenance tunnels of grey concrete with pipes on every wall and
ceiling; some pipes leak hot water or steam; dim red bulbs; sweltering.
