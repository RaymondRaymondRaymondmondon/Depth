NOCLIP — Arcade Game 10 Design Document
Value
Sold value depends on the buyer (the Bureau pays quota credit at a fixed rate; the Fence
pays cash at market rates that change daily) and on condition (damaged items are worth
half). Every item is logged on the Surface with its level and a one-line description:
collectors' notes from the Bureau, dry and funny.
Equipment
Bought on the Surface between days (delivered with the crew at insertion) or at a Lab's
Commissary (more expensive, but available mid-day). Equipment is cheap enough that a
crew always has some and expensive enough that every purchase is a trade against quota.
