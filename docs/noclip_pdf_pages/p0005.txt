What the player experiences
Normal
The hum gets louder; walls breathe slightly at the
edge of vision; a fluorescent light flickers that the
others can't see
False sounds: footsteps behind, a teammate's voice
from the wrong direction (synthesized from that
teammate's recent voice lines), a door that wasn't
there; Smilers' eyes in dark corners that aren't real
(and sometimes are)
Fake entities: hallucinated Hounds and Facelings
that move toward you and vanish on contact; loot
shows the wrong value; the minimap lies by a room
Your teammates sometimes look like Facelings;
your own voice on proximity is distorted for others
Lost: the player wanders off on autopilot toward
the nearest noclip point for IO s unless a teammate
grabs them; if they reach it, they noclip to a random
level alone
Hallucinations are per-player and client-side: the others don't see them, so a crew learns
to ask "do you see that?" over voice, which is the point.
Carrying
• A backpack holds 4 small items (tools, consumables) and 2 small loot items. Loot
comes in sizes: small (in a pocket slot), medium (in the hands, one at a time, -15%
speed), large (two hands, -30% speed, no tools usable), and huge (two players carry it
together, -40%).
• Weight slows sprinting and drains stamina. Dropping loot is instant (a key) and makes a
noise; throwing it is a 3 m toss that makes more.
• A cart (shop item) carries 6 medium items or 2 large; it's loud on hard floors and silent
on carpet.
