NOCLIP — Arcade Game 10 Design Document
Losing teammates
A crew that splits up loses sanity (the alone drain) and gains speed. A crew that stays
together is safe and slow. The game's best moments come from someone wandering off.
Sharing
Loot in a player's hands is theirs; loot in the crate is the crew's; money is the crew's; a
player who sells their own carry to the Fence on the Surface does it for the crew.
Cosmetics and tokens are personal.
