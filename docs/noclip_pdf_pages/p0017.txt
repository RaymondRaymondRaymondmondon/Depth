Level 13: Vitrum Madness
• Look: A maze of glass: walls, floors, and ceilings of clear and frosted glass and mirrors;
everything is visible and nothing is reachable; light bends.
• Generation: A 3D lattice of glass corridors with mirror panels; some glass is clear (see
through, can't pass), some frosted, some mirror.
• Mood rule — Reflections: you can see the loot, the exit, and the entities, and none of
them are where they look. Mirrors show reflections that are delayed by a second (a
teammate's reflection can be wrong). Shattering glass (a crowbar, the shotgun) opens
paths and is the loudest thing in the level.
• Hazards: Shattered glass (bleeds), dead ends that look open, falls through glass floors.
• Entities: Mirror Things (a player's reflection that steps out of a mirror when they've
looked at it too long; it's a Skin-Stealer variant made of glass and it shatters on a hit),
Smilers reflected (one Smiler, many reflections; only one is real).
• Loot: High and fragile: glass sculptures, crystal glassware, a mirror that shows
somewhere else (anomalous), lenses.
• Labs: Lab No, behind frosted glass that has to be broken.
• Exits: A mirror that's a door (Level 14); the subway's glass station (Level 11); a shattered
floor (Level 16).
Level 14: Inhospitality
• Look: An endless hospital: wards, corridors with gurneys, operating theaters, a
reception, a morgue; flickering lights; the smell of antiseptic; announcements over the
PA paging doctors who aren't there.
• Generation: Wings of wards around nurses' stations; floors connected by stairs and
elevators; a morgue in the basement.
