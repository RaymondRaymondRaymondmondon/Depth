Level 8: The Cave System
• Look: Rock caves, tunnels, chasms, underground rivers, crystal formations, spider
webs. Cold. Dripping.
• Generation: A 3D cave network with vertical shafts, chasms with bridges, and large
chambers.
• Mood rule — Verticality: climbing (ropes, ladders found and placed) and falling are the
level; the crew needs ropes and lights; the deeper the cave, the better the loot and the
bigger the spiders.
• Hazards: Falls; cave-ins (noise triggers them); rivers that sweep you; webs that hold
you.
• Entities: Spiders (the Backrooms' cave arachnids, large and many-eyed), Crawlers,
Hounds, Deathmoths in the warm chambers.
• Loot: High: crystals (the most valuable small loot in the game), old mining equipment,
gold ore, a lost researcher's journal.
• Labs: Lab Theta (dormant), in a chamber reached by a long rope descent.
• Exits: A tunnel that opens into a suburban street (Level 9); an underground river noclip
(Level 7); climb all the way up (Level 1); a ladder up into a farm cellar (Level 10).
Level 9: The Darkened Suburbs
• Look: An endless American SUbUrb at night: identical houses, streetlights (some
working), lawns, a sky with no stars. Houses are dark inside.
• Generation: A street grid of houses with yards and fences; house interiors from a kit; a
park, a school, a convenience store as set pieces.
• Mood rule — The Houses: houses are the loot and the danger; each is a small dungeon;
their front doors lock behind you sometimes. Streetlights are safe; the dark between
them isn't. The level is too big to search, so contracts send crews to specific
addresses.
• Hazards: The dark between streetlights; houses that change when you leave a room;
the ice cream truck's song.
