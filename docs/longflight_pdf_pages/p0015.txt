The Flight — The Long Flight (tv.'o-hour expansion)
Titles
Titles are earned, shown on the leaderboard, and worth score: the Fisher King (most fish
in a season), the Stormrider (crossed the Storm Wall), the Kraken's Bane, the Peacemaker
(proposed a Peace that passed), the Oathbreaker (red, -50), the Shepherd (no chick
starved all match), the Matchmaker (five dynastic marriages), the Wonder-builder, the
Dodo's Keeper, the Last Founder (the only Founder never to die). Each title is a line in the
Chronicle and 25-100 score.
Songs
A colony with a Drummer and fervour over 50 writes a song each season: a short
synthesized melody built from the colony's founder species' call, its species (if it has
speciated), and the season's best moment. Songs play when the colony's flocks fly in
formation and when its island is approached; a rival's song is a warning you hear before
you see them. Songs are cosmetic and the first thing players remember.
Tools (Clever III)
A colony with Clever Ill birds learns tools, one per season: the Hook (a thorn held in the
beak: fishers catch size +1), the Shell Hammer (builders break coral: new sites on reef
islands), the Fire Carry (an ember carried from the volcano: hedges burn without bombs),
the Mirror (a shard that signals a flock across the map instantly), the Rope (seaweed line:
two birds carry a size-6 fish or a chick-bearing nest). Tools are the Clever line's reason to
exist and the only research that isn't on the Roost.
Taming
A Priest with Faith tier 3 and a season of offerings can tame one of the map's beasts for
the colony: a dolphin pod (drives fish to your grounds; fights sharks), a sea turtle (a living
raft that carries chicks between islands), the Grey Wings' eagle (a Striker that doesn't eat
your fish), a crocodile (guards a lagoon's shore), the ape (sleeps on your island and throws
rocks at your enemies; eats a tuna a day). A tamed beast is a named unit, can be killed, and
is in the Chronicle. The kraken can't be tamed; it can only be chained.
Colony identity
By year two a colony is: a dynasty name, a species name if it speciated, a livery and a song,
a Chronicle with ten chapters, titles, elders, a tamed beast, and a wonder. Two colonies of
the same founder species can be nothing alike. That's what the extra hour is for.
