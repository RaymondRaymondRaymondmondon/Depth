Lobby options (added)
Match length (24 or 32 days); Far Sea on or off (off: a two-hour match on the old map,
denser); Great War allowed or Council-only; the Roc on or off; save-and-resume at season
breaks; the Reckoning's events on or off (off: the final season is a normal Winter, for
groups who want the peaceful version).
Data files (added to The Flight's)
flight_long_arc . json (seasons, events, the Reckoning), flight _ generations . json
(aging, heirs, succession choices, elders), flight _ evolution . json (traits, ranks,
speciation, species powers, mutations), flight _ projects . json (the nine wonders and
second tiers), flight_farsea. json (places, wild colonies, the Roc, the navy),
flight _ council. json (leagues, motions, the war deck), flight_trade_long.json
(goods, routes, the Exchange), flight _ chronicle. json (templates, titles, songs),
flight _ tools _ taming. json .
Tests (added)
--flight-long <days> rt-Jns a full bot Long Flight and reports:
successions, speciations, wonders built, the Council's votes, whether a Great War
happened, the Reckoning's events, and the score spread.
--flight-breed <trait> <generations> asserts rank-up rules and speciation at 20
birds.
--flight-chronicle <seed> generates a Chronicle from a scripted match and checks
every template has its slots filled.
Balance targets (added)
A bot colony speciates by day 22 if it breeds for one trait from day 5; never if it doesn't.
