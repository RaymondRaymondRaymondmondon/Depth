The clock's mercy
Two hours is long for friends. Three things keep it moving:
• Catch-up winds. A colony in the bottom third of the score gets a tailwind decree every
morning (free) and its wild mates arrive faster. Nobody is out of it by hour one.
• Save and resume (the Long Night mode from A Night Off's lobby): the host saves
between seasons; a group can play year one tonight and year two next week, with the
same colonies, the same chronicle, and the same grudges.
• The Reckoning is a timer everyone can see. From day 25, the end is on the screen,
which makes the last hour a sprint rather than a wait.
Generations and succession
In the Long Flight the Founder is mortal. It ages through year one, dies by Winter, and the
colony passes to an heir. Succession is the models signature: the player's bird changes,
the colony keeps going, and the dynasty is what the player has built.
Aging
• The Founder is young on days 1-8 (as designed), prime on 9-12 (+10% to everything:
the best days of its life), old on 13-16 (-10% speed and stamina per day; it can no longer
fish size-4; its flock bonus doubles because everyone's used to it).
• It dies of age at dawn on day 17 at the latest, or earlier by violence. A violent death
before day 13 is a crisis: the heir is a chick.
• The second Founder (the heir) ages the same way through year two and dies at the
Reckoning's end, which is why the match ends when it does.
Heirs
On day 5 the colony's first clutch produces a marked chick: the heir, shown with a
crest. The player can re-mark a different chick any day (a different mate's chick, a
different trait) until the succession.
