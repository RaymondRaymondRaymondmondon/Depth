Routes and convoys
• A route is chartered between two markets (towns or players' Market Halls); it shows
on the map as a line. A route needs Traders assigned; each round trip pays by distance
and the goods' spread.
Three or more Traders on a route fly as a convoy with an escort slot (a Tank or a
Harrier); convoys pay 20% more and are what pirates and frigatebirds hunt.
• A route through another player's territory needs their passage (a diplomacy click) or
risks their Harriers; a route through the Far Sea needs an islet to rest on.
