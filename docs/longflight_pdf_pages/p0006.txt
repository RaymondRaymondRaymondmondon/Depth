Becoming a species
When a colony has 20 birds with the same rank-III trait, it speciates: the player names the
species, every bird's model takes the rank-III look, the colony's livery shows it, and the
species gets one species power chosen from three offered for that trait (a Quick species:
the Swift's Skim; a Fierce species: the Beaked's Tear; a Deep species: the Penguin's
underwater fishing; a Silent species: the Shadow's Dusk Raid; and so on, so a long-bred
colony borrows another founder's unique). A second species power at 40 birds of a
second rank-III trait is the deepest a colony can go.
Hybrids
A dynastic marriage (see Generations) or a converted wild bird of another founder
species in a nest produces hybrids: a chick with two founder species' boosts at half
strength each. A hybrid line is slower to breed (traits don't rank up for a generation) and is
the only way to have, say, a colony that is half Pelican logistics and half Gannet plunging.
The Cuckoo's stolen chicks are hybrids by definition, which is why the Cuckoo's colony in
year two is a zoo.
Drift
Traits also drift: every generation has a 5% chance of a mutation, a random trait at rank I
the parents didn't have, and a 1% chance of a rare one (Albino: visible from anywhere,
mates arrive Giant: a size-4 bird that carries 6 and fights like a Striker but eats for
three; Crested: fervour +5 per Crested bird). The family tree shows them; a colony can
breed toward a mutation it likes.
