What's older
• Grounds remember. Every old ground's yield in year two is its year-one average: an
overfished lagoon stays poor, a rested one stays rich. The Fish Gate resets one.
• Islands remember. Hedges, towers, monuments, and scars (burned forests, a cratered
summit) persist; a tropical island logged for twigs all year one is bare in year two unless
a Gardener replanted it.
• Towns remember. Reputation carries; a town that liked you in year one escorts your
convoys in year two; one that hated you refuses your heir at the dock until a gift.
• Beasts remember. A kraken fed all year one sleeps through year two's Spring; one that
was bombed hunts the bomber's colony's grounds.
• The wild colonies grew. Bird Island is twice the size; the Grey Wings have a second
crag; the pirates have a captain's heir.
