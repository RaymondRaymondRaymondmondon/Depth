The Flight — The Long Flight (tv.'o-hour expansion)
New in year two
• The Roc hunts the whole map by Summer: flocks over ten birds in the open at noon are
its prey; the counter is to fly in two flocks of nine, or at dawn, or to build the Lighthouse
and blind it.
• The navy (the Far Sea towns' frigate) hunts pirates and anyone the Council
embargoed; it can be hired by a league for a season for the price of a Grand Project's
first tier.
• Plagues are more likely in colonies over 80 birds; Nurses and the chemistry tree are the
cure, and a colony that lets one run loses a generation.
• The second succession for colonies whose heir dies in the Great War: the heir's heir is
a chick, and the colony's year two ends under a regent, which the Chronicle writes as a
tragedy.
• Winter year two is the coldest: stamina costs double in the north, the Ice Shelf covers
the Atoll and the Kelp Raft, penguin colonies thrive, and the Long Night lasts two days.
A match in year two without war
It's a real option: the Council's Peace of the Sea twice over, a Long Peace in Autumn, every
player building, breeding, and trading, and the Reckoning scored on wonders, species,
chronicles, and holdings. The Reckoning's events (below) make sure a peaceful year two is
still dangerous; the sea doesn't sign treaties.
The Reckoning
The final season (days 29-32). Everyone can see the end; holdings count triple; the
Chronicles are read; and the sea has the last word.
