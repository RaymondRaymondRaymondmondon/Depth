The Flight — The Long Flight (tv.'o-hour expansion)
Breeding and evolution
In the one-hour game a mate's trait gives its chicks a small bonus. In the Long Flight traits
compound: a chick with a Quick mother and a Quick father is Quick II, its chicks can be
Quick Ill, and by year two a colony that has bred for one thing is a species that nobody
started with. Evolution is the Long Flight's deepest system and it is still one screen: the
nest panel's family tree.
How traits compound
• Every bird has up to three traits with ranks I-Ill. A chick gets each parent's traits; where
both parents share a trait, the chick's rank is one higher (max Ill). Where they differ,
the chick gets one from each (random), up to three.
• Rank II is double the bonus; rank Ill is triple and adds a visible change: Quick Ill birds
are slimmer and swept-winged; Hardy Ill are broad; Fierce Ill have a hooked beak; Keen
Ill have the owl's eyes; Big Eggs Ill lay visibly larger eggs; Fertile Ill clutches are five.
• The player steers breeding by which mate gets which nest and which fish fills the
courtship bowl (a trait's favorite fish calls that trait).
