The Flight — The Long Flight (tv.'o-hour expansion)
• One to three wonders are finished by day 16 in a six-player match; six to nine by day 32.
• The Great War is declared in about 60% of six-player matches and lasts 3-4 days.
• The Reckoning's day-31 event costs the average colony 10-20% of its nests; a prepared
one, under 5%.
The score spread between first and last in a six-player match is under with the
catch-up winds.
Build order
The Long Flight is built after The Flight and its one-hour expansion are playable, in this
order: generations and succession; evolution; the Far Sea (the map generator's second
ring, the Roc, the wild colonies); Grand Projects; leagues and the Council; trade empires
and the Exchange; the Chronicle, titles, songs; tools and taming; the Reckoning; save-and-
resume. Each stage ends with
--flight-long running one more layer.
