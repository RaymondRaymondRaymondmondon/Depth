Grand Projects
Wonders. Each takes a season of a colony's builders and a mountain of twigs, shells, and
pearls; each changes the map for everyone, not just the builder; each can be built by one
colony per map (the first to finish gets it; the others' progress converts to shells). They
are the Long Flight's long goals and the reason to hold an island for a year.
