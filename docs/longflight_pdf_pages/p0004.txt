Succession
When the Founder dies, the heir becomes the player's bird immediately, adult if it has
fledged, chick-leader if not. The colony gets:
• A succession day: fervour -20 (grief), warriors home, and the Chronicle writes the
Founder's obituary (what it did, who killed it or didn't).
• A succession choice, one of three: The Old Way (keep every colony bonus as is), The
New Broom (swap the founder species' boost for the heir's mother's species if she was
wild-born of another species, which is how a Taloned colony becomes half-Swift), or
The Pilgrimage (the heir flies to the shrine and claims a relic's effect permanently for
the colony, if the colony holds one).
• Enemies know. A colony on its succession day is the day to raid it, and everyone's map
says so.
Dynasties
A colony's succession line is its dynasty: Founder, heir, and (if the heir dies in year two)
the heir's heir. Dynasties show on the leaderboard as a name the player picks at the first
succession ("the House of Longbeak") and in the Chronicle. Two dynasties can marry: an
heir sent to another colony's nest (by Ferrier, with a truce) produces a chick that both
colonies treat as their own, and the colonies can't war for a season. It's the Long Flight's
alliance mechanic that doesn't need a screen.
Elders
Veterans that survive a whole year become elders: they stop fighting and start teaching.
An elder in a colony gives one permanent bonus from its trait (a Fearless elder: the
colony's warriors rout 10% less; a Keen elder: scouts report exact), and its death (of age,
at the Reckoning) is in the Chronicle. A colony with five elders by year two is a colony with
a culture.
