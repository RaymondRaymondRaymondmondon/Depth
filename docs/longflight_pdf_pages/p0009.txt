Second tiers (year two)
In year two each finished project can be raised for half its cost again: the Rookery hatches
every egg on the island in a day, the Lighthouse's beam blinds flocks, the Gate's reef
becomes a wall ships can't cross, the Temple's curse routs a flock, the Works makes the
Blockbuster daily, the Market Hall sets prices, the Chain makes the kraken fight at the
builder's side in a battle (once), the Ark carries a Grand Project, and the Monument
doubles.
The Far Sea
At dusk on day 16 the fog beyond the map's edge lifts and the map doubles. The Far Sea is
a ring of new water around the old one: richer, stranger, and already inhabited. Year two is
the race for it.
