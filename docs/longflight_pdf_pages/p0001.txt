Overview
The Long Flight is the two-hour mode of The Flight: a match of two full years, 32 game
days, for 2-6 players who want a colony to become a civilization. It sits on top of the base
game and the one-hour expansion; nothing there changes. What the extra hour buys is
time for things to compound: Founders die and are succeeded, colonies breed into
species of their own, wonders get built, the map doubles when the Far Sea opens,
alliances turn into leagues and leagues into a Great War, trade becomes an empire, and
the colony writes its own history.
The rule for depth. Every new system here is a layer that only matters once the layer
below it is working, and each one is still one screen and one choice at a time. A six-player
group that knows the base game can play the Long Flight without reading anything,
because nothing new happens before day 8, and everything new announces itself.
The six new layers
1. Generations. The Founder ages and dies on purpose; an heir takes the colony with a
mixed inheritance. Dynasties are the long game's identity.
2. Evolution. Mates' traits compound across generations until a colony is its own species
with its own name.
3. Grand Projects. Wonders that take a season to build and change the map for everyone.
4. The Far Sea. At the end of year one the fog beyond the map lifts: new islands, new
dangers, new neighbors, and the kraken is not the biggest thing anymore.
5. Leagues and the Great War. Alliances that bind, a council that votes, and a war that
the whole map fights at once.
6. The Chronicle. The colony's history is written as it happens: titles, songs, legends, and
a book the players keep after the match.
Session. Two hours; 32 game days at about 3.75 real minutes per day; four seasons twice
over; the Reckoning in the final season. The lobby can also set 24 days (90 minutes) for a
shorter long match.
