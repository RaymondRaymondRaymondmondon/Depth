The Great War
The Great War is Autumn of year two's set piece: four days in which the whole map fights
as two sides, with rules that make it different from a raid:
• Fronts. Every island held by either side is a front; the map shows them. Taking a front's
island flips its holdings and its grounds to the taker for the war.
• War decrees. The decree deck is replaced by a war deck (Muster: every adult fights
today; Scorched Grounds: your abandoned islands' grounds are overfished on
purpose; Night March: flocks move at night without rest; Last Stand: a besieged
island's defenders get +50% for a day and starve twice as fast after).
• War weariness. Every day of war costs every colony 5 fervour and one fish per bird
extra (armies eat); by day four most colonies want it over, which is when the Council's
Peace of the Sea is proposed again.
• The beasts take sides. The Kraken's Chain, the Roc (if a league holds its peak), and the
Grey Wings (if paid) fight in the Great War; the Drowned Fleet drifts to the loudest
front.
• Ending it. The war ends when one side holds two-thirds of the fronts, when the
Council votes Peace, or when four days pass. The winning side's members get a war
title in their Chronicle and 200 score each; the losers keep what they held at the end.
A match without a Great War is possible (the Council never passes it, or no league
proposes it); the Reckoning rewards the quiet colonies differently (below).
