The Flight — The Long Flight (tv.'o-hour expansion)
Colonizing it
The Far Sea is where second and third colonies go and where dynastic marriages happen
(two colonies on one archipelago island). A colony founded there by a Pathfinder on day 17
is a real colony by day 24, which is just in time for the Great War.
Leagues and the Great War
The one-hour game's diplomacy is truces and bounties. The Long Flight adds leagues
(alliances that bind), a Council (a vote the whole map takes), and the Great War (one war
everyone is in). Each is one screen.
Leagues
• Two or more players form a league by exchanging Heralds; it costs each a pearl and a
day. A league shares vision, allows feed lines, lets flocks fly together under one leader,
and splits the Reckoning's score (the league's total, divided evenly, so a league of three
strong colonies beats three strong rivals but a league carrying a weak member pays for
• Leaving a league costs 30 fervour and a season of being oathbroken: every town's
rates -20%, no new leagues, and your name in red on everyone's leaderboard.
• A league can hold a Grand Project together (costs split, effect shared).
• Six players can be two leagues of three, three of two, or a league against everyone,
which is where the Great War comes from.
