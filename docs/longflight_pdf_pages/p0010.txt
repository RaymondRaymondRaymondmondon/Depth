Rules of the Far Sea
• It's far: a day's flight from the old map's edge for most birds; the Albatross, the Swift,
and formation flight make it half. Feed lines across it need the Pelican or a chain of
islets.
• Its wild birds are their own colonies with warriors (the thorn-birds, the penguins, the
Roc) and can be converted, allied, or conquered like a player.
• Its grounds are untouched: every Far Sea ground is at full yield on day 17 and crowded
by day 20.
• The old map doesn't empty: the kraken, the ape, and the pirates stay; the Far Sea's
dangers come inward over year two (the Roc hunts the old map by Summer; the Fleet
drifts; the Ice Shelf advances).
