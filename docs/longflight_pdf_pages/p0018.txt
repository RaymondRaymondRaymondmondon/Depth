What it does
Two days of the worst weather: every drifting
island moves, the Fleet runs aground somewhere,
the Storm Wall closes in by a region; wonders on
exposed sites take damage unless hedged
The kraken (chained or not) rises and attacks the
colony that bled its cove most in two years; the Roc
attacks the colony that killed most birds; the Grey
Wings attack the colony that paid least; the
Drowned board whoever is loudest. Every colony
faces its own debt at once, and leagues decide
whose to answer
The second Founder dies at dusk; the heir (or
regent) takes the colony into the final hour; at
midnight the score is final
Victory
A Long Flight is won on the Reckoning score, with no early end except the base game's
two-thirds-of-sites rule, which in a two-hour match is nearly impossible and worth trying.
