Scratching is done with the cursor (3 s a ticket) and can be pocketed to scratch on the
porch between waves.
Timing
The bell at 10 s. At O everyone is pulled back to their stall wherever they are (a slot pull
mid-spin finishes and pays). A player who bought nothing gets a small interest on cash
(+5%) for patience.
The Slop Shop
A neon booth run by a raccoon in a vest. It sells two kinds of thing: cosmetics that last the
match, and sabotage that ruins someone else's next hunt. Every sabotage item names a
target player when bought (or "the leader" for a premium), shows on the board so
everyone knows who did it, and has a counter that the target can buy or do.
Cosmetics (match-only)
A hat (cowboy, duck bill, bucket, crown) $30; a gun paint (chrome, gold, camo, bubblegum
pink) $40; a victory dance $25; a stall flag $20; a dog outfit (the dog wears it while
retrieving your birds) $35; a kill sound (quack, kazoo, airhorn, sad trombone) $30; tracer
colors $20. Permanent cosmetics are bought with arcade tokens outside the match.
