Guns and attachments are kept for the match; nothing carries to the next match.
The intermission
Forty-five seconds, one room, six people, and everything costs money. The room is laid
out so a player can do two or three things in that time, not all of them, which is the
decision.
The gun counter
Walk up to the glass case: guns and attachments show on the case with price and stats;
buying is one click and the gun appears in your hands. Mr. Zappa gives each player one
daily deal per intermission (a random gun or attachment at 30% off, shown only to that
player).
The mystery-gun machine
A big red crank machine. One crank costs the price of the cheapest gun in the game
(the Flare Pistol, $100).
• It dispenses a plastic capsule with a random gun from all 30 (the Zapper excluded),
equal odds. A crank can hand you the Mega Zapper for $100, or the Bread Gun.
• The gun goes in your hands; your current second gun drops to the stall hook (or the
floor if the hook is full, where anyone can take it).
• A crank at under 10 s left plays a drumroll; the capsule opens on the porch as the hunt
starts.
Slot machines
• Six machines; $10, $25, or $50 a pull. Three reels of ducks, dogs, guns, bells, and the
Zappa logo.
• Payouts: two of a kind returns the bet; three ducks three dogs three guns pays
a random gun; three bells three Zappa logos and a fanfare everyone hears.
