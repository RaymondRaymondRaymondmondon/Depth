What it does
Fires a live crab that clamps onto a bird and drags it
down (a kill after 1 s); crabs that miss land in the
marsh and walk back to your stall
A hand-cranked toy gatling: spins up for 1 s, then a
firehose of plastic pellets; turning it is slow
A toy ray gun that fires a slow green beam;
anything in the beam is turned into a roast chicken
(a kill)
A weighted net that catches up to three birds in an
area; they fall together
Fires bread; birds that eat it fly slowly toward your
stall for 3 s (easy shots, and other players can steal
them)
A boomerang that arcs out and back, hitting
everything on its path both ways; catch it or lose it
Bubbles trap a bird and float it upward slowly for 4
s; pop it for the kill (anyone can pop it)
Pulls metal: armored ducks, the UFO, and other
players/ dropped guns; armored ducks are yanked
to the rail
A rocket that bursts into a sky-wide spray of
sparkles: a few random kills and a show
Sticks a plunger to a bird; the bird flies erratically
and slowly; a second plunger kills
Blows birds back toward the porch (they stay in
the sky longer) and blows other players' hats off
Fires a banana bunch that splits into five bananas
in the air; each is a kill on a small bird
