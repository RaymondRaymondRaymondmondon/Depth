Fowl play — Arcade Game 11 Design Document
Balance targets
• A round-I Zapper player shoots 6-9 birds; by round 10 a well-bought player shoots 20-
30.
• The fun guns are fun, not best: each wins on one bird type and loses on another; none
takes more than 15% of birds across a sample.
• Gambling loses money on average (8% edge) and wins the match about one time in ten
for the player who leans on it.
• Sabotage costs about a third of a round's earnings and costs the target about a fifth of
a round's birds.
Open questions for the designer
Should shooting another player's gun knock it out of their hands (a stronger PvP edge),
or stay cosmetic as written?
Should the mystery-gun machine be able to dispense the Zapper (a duplicate, and a
laugh)?
Is the UFO in every match, or a rare event?
Should money carry between matches as a profile bank, or reset each match as
written?
