Presentation, technology, sound, and build order
Look
Fowl Play is bright and plastic: a low-poly marsh at golden hour, a warm timber clubhouse,
and toy guns that look moulded and slightly glossy. Birds are rounded and expressive (a
hit bird's eyes go wide; a falling bird spins). The HUD is a chunky 8-bit-style strip along the
bottom (round number, bullets as little icons, birds as little silhouettes that fill as they're
hit), a love letter to the old screen, rendered crisp. This look is Fowl Play's own.
Technology
• First-person 3D on the arcade's shared renderer; birds are simple rigged meshes on
spline flight paths with noise, plus behaviors (scatter, dive, flee, charge).
• Host-authoritative hits with lag compensation (rewind birds up to 150 ms) so shooting
feels local; bird paths are deterministic from a seed so clients agree.
• Six players, about 40 birds in the air at most, small bandwidth.
