Fowl play — Arcade Game 11 Design Document
Birds
Birds flush from the reeds in waves, fly a pattern across and away over the marsh, and
escape off the far edge after 6-10 seconds if nobody hits them. Every bird has HP (most
die to one pistol shot), a pattern, a bird count toward the score, and a payout.
