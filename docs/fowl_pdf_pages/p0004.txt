The club dog (a scruffy hunting hound) flushes birds from the reeds at the start of each
wave, runs out to retrieve kills, and holds them up at the tally. When a whole wave
escapes, it pops up and laughs, as tradition demands. It can be shot. It can't be hurt (shots
bounce off with a squeak), but shooting the dog costs the shooter one bird from their
score and the dog remembers: next round it retrieves that player's birds last.
