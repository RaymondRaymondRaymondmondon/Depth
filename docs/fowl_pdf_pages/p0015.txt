Fowl play — Arcade Game 11 Design Document
• Guns are data: a mesh built from toy-gun parts (body, barrel, grip, slide, magazine,
accessories) with the shared grey-and-red palette, so new guns are cheap.
Sound
Plastic: every gun has a toy click under its real sound (the pistol's zap is a beep and a
crack). Ducks quack, geese honk, swans hiss, the phoenix shrieks, the UFO hums. The dog
barks, sniffs, and laughs. The clubhouse is a jaunty piano loop during the intermission; the
hunt is a chiptune march that speeds up each round. Slot machines ding; scratchers
scratch; the Slop Shop has its own tacky jingle. A jackpot plays a fanfare everyone hears
from the porch.
