Session. 15 rounds, about 25 minutes. 1-6 players (bots fill). Score pays arcade tokens.
The match
A match is 15 rounds. Each round is a hunt (60 seconds of birds) followed by an
intermission (45 seconds in the room). The leader after round 15 by birds shot wins;
money is how you get there, not the score.
