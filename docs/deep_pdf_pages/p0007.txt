Light is its own channel, measured in lux. Dive lamps put out about 1,000 lux at 1 m, flares
about 10,000 lux, and the Flash-Stalker's jaw strobes about 50,000 lux. Glowing flora range
from about 1 to 200 lux. Light attracts light-drawn species and blinds light-sensitive ones,
separately from the Wake.
