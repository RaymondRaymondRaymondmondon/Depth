• Dormant I grazing (default): filters plankton, grazes, or rests in a burrow; uses energy
at the baseline rate
• Alert/ investigating: noise rises 15 dB above ambient, or scent passes 0.1 ppm. The
creature turns toward the stimulus and approaches carefully.
• Active hunting: hunger over 60% and prey detected. Runs species-specific tactics
such as pack flanking, camouflage ambush, or high-speed ramming.
• Blood frenzy: scent over 1.5 ppm. Ignores light hazards and self-preservation and
strikes any moving thing.
• Territorial defense: a player or vehicle comes within 50 m of a nest or nursery. Warning
displays (flashing bioluminescence, growls) come first, then lethal attacks.
