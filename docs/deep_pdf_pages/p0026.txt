The Deep
• Food web: Iron-Kelp, the other macro-algae, and phytoplankton feed the plankton, the
sluggish grazers (Kelp-Cow, Kelp-Grazer Manatee, Holdfast Hog), and armored snails.
Those feed stalkers like the Stalker-Hound and Lancer Pike, plus suction hunters like
the Suction-Maw Eel. Scavengers led by the Root-Marrow Worm recycle fallen kelp. At
the top, the Tangle-Serpent Leviathan and Shroud Serpent ambush anything big that
displaces water, so noisy, bleeding, or propeller-driven crews attract the whole chain.
