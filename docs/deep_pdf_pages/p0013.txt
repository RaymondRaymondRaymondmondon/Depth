The Deep
• Survival Knife: harvests, cuts tangles, and fights off small predators.
Spear Gun: tethered or free-firing; for hunting medium-sized food and scaring off
predators. Each shot makes a distinct thwack (Mid band).
• Stasis Rifle (phase 3): freezes a fish in a bubble so it can be collected alive. Bloodless,
but its discharge is a very loud 115 dB High-band crack and it uses a lot of battery.
• Silent Stasis Rifle (phase 8): the same freeze at under 15 dB, for stealth hunting in the
Graveyard and Void. Uses even more battery.
