6. The Crystal Reef (700 to 900 m)
• Layout: fields of razor-sharp quartz and silicate crystal pillars growing in geometric
lattices. They slash dive suits, crack viewports, and tear small SUbS.
• Look: prismatic light, dazzling reflections in sub lights, transparent and translucent
fauna.
• Hazards: suit punctures, cracked viewports (needs Pressure-Glass Viewports),
disorientation from scattered floodlight beams, glass-hard stealth predators that are
fragile but highly aggressive.
• Food web: piezo-electric silica algae feed the diatom and plankton swarms, while 15
grazers crop the crystal flora; only the Pane-Back Dugong can grind the tier 3 spires
and clusters. Transparent mesopredators such as the Shard-Shark, Mirror-Squid, and
Prismjaw Gar hunt inside the refractions, and scavengers recycle detritus. Four apex
hunters top the web, led by the silent, optically camouflaged Glass-daw Leviathan.
