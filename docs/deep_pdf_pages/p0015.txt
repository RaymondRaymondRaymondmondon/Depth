1. The Sunlit Shallows (O to 50 m)
• Layout: turquoise water over vast coral reefs, sandbars, and shallow lagoons; sunlight
reaches the seabed. The safe starting zone. Sandbanks can ground heavy subs.
• Look: golden sunbeams through clear water, rainbow coral, huge swirling schools of
shimmering fish, gentle currents.
• Hazards: sandbar strandings, coral scraping, territorial reef defenders during midday
sun spikes.
• Food web: sunlight powers phytoplankton and carpet reef algae. Plankton swarms
feed the filter feeders; algae, moss, and sea-grass feed the grazers. Those feed
