The Deep
Endgame. The final goal is the same as before: harvest Void-Crystals to power the
Nautilus's ascent mechanism. Phase 3 is where the first "Night Shift" deep dive happens
(bioluminescent predators hunting in the dark), and phase 8 is where crews must rely on
passive sonar and battery power to avoid the blind, sound-hunting Echo Leviathan.
The Nautilus
The Nautilus is a sprawling vessel and the crew's expanding mobile base. Players
physically walk between rooms to run its systems, like the stations on the Gannet in The
Trawl.
