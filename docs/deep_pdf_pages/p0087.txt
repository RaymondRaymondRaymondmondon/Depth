8. The Abyssal Graveyard (1,200 to 1,800 m)
• Layout: grey silt plains strewn with colossal fossilized ribcages of extinct leviathans,
rising 50 m from the mud. The crew can drive the Nautilus inside the ribcages to hide
from active sonar. The Crashed Bathysphere Facility lies here.
• Look: eerie white bone, drifting marine snow, total blackness beyond the floodlights.
• Hazards: extreme pressure (needs Ceramic-Composite Plates), blind sound-hunting
predators that track the Nautilus by engine vibration, silt storms that blind passive
sensors.
• Food web: marine snow, bone, and graveyard flora feed plankton, filter feeders, and
grazers, including the Osedax Bone-Borer and the marrow-boring shipworms and
pholads that turn fossil bone into Abyssal Marrow. Those feed heavy scavengers such
as the Abyssal Scavenger Crab and Bonefall King Crab, and twenty mesopredators led
by blind Sonar-Hound packs. At the top, the Echo Leviathan hunts engine hum from 2.5
km and comes when the Hounds howl, alongside the Shroud Kraken, Grave-Warden
Sleeper Shark, and Sepulcher Wyrm.
