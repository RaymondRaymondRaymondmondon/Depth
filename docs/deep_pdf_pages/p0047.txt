The Deep
• Food web: vent bacteria and vent flora feed the plankton, the armored grazers (Basalt
Plate Crab, Iron-Foot Snail), and heat-resistant filter feeders (Crimson Plume
Tubeworm, Smoker Mussel). Those feed plated mesopredators like the Vent-Shark,
Magma-Snapper, and Scald Octopus, while scavengers clean up carcasses and bone
falls. The Pyroclast Megalodon, Caldera Kraken, and Sulfurmaw Leviathan hunt the
large mesopredators, and the vibration-tracking Boiler Worm swallows Ashmaw
Baskers, whole schools, and noisy subs.
