3. The Bioluminescent Caverns (150 to 300 m)
Layout: limestone tunnel networks and huge cathedral caverns. Echoes amplify every
sound, so stealth is vital. Tight squeezes need the Kite-Sub or swimming. Upper
chambers hold air pockets with bat roosts. The Abandoned Underwater City lies here.
Look: pitch-black tunnels lit only by neon-blue, magenta, and amber flora (about 1 to
200 lux) and pulsing creature organs; strobe flashes light the wet walls.
Hazards: extreme echo (amplifies Wake), cave-ins, blinding strobes from predators (up
to about 50,000 lux), getting lost in narrow tunnels.
Food web: chemosynthetic glowing fungi, lichens, and bacterial mats feed plankton,
blind crustaceans, and grazers, while bat guano from the air pockets feeds beetles,
prawns, and molds. Glass-shrimp, cavefish, and other grazers feed echolocating rays
