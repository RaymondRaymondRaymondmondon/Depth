• Danger: the humming of the surviving emergency beacons mimics engine vibration, so
a confused Echo Leviathan circles the facility constantly. Players use the station's
layout to break line of sight and drop Acoustic Decoy Buoys into its vents to lure the
leviathan away from key salvage.
Crafting, resources, and upgrades
Crafting draws directly on the food web: players harvest compounds from specific
creatures and plants, and must do it without triggering an ecological collapse. Every
named resource is its own item. Each hull upgrade mixes a new material from the current
biome with materials from shallower biomes.
