The Coral Reef. The showcase map: dense, layered reef built from many coral species
(staghorn, brain, table, fan, soft corals), with fish everywhere. The Lagoon's mangrove
roots and bright shallows, the Staghorn Forest's corridors, the Bommie with its cleaning
station, the open Sand Flats with sea grass and garden eels, the Brain Coral Maze, the Blue
Hole's dark circular drop, and the Drop-off Wall falling into deep blue where mantas, orcas,
and whales pass. Sunlight caustics everywhere; colour falls off to blue with depth.
Atlantis. A real city plan on a 120 m hill, 500 m across, with 60+ buildings: the great wall
and gatehouse, terraced farms with irrigation canals and a mill, the lower town's houses,
market, and toolsmithsl street, bathhouses with hypocausts, barracks and the armory, the
library with its observatory, the forum and government hall, and the grand chapel with the
god-pool on the summit. Marble and sandstone with mosaics, bronze statues, and
columns, all overgrown: algae, sponges, sea fans, coral growing out of windows, silt drifts
in streets. The aqueduct as a landmark running down the hill. The lighthouse on the wall.
The crystal engine glowing in the treasury.
Approaching the Void. The Rim: a vast black sand plain with sea pens and sand eels, the
worm's tunnels on the horizon, and the abyss as an enormous black edge. Below: vertical
chimneys and galleries with glass sponge reefs, bamboo coral, black coral, tube worms,
and hydrothermal vents in the Warrens. The research station: a rusted early-20th-century
structure of riveted steel modules, portholes, specimen tanks (some broken), terminals,
the reactor, and the mech bay. The Lowest Vault: a cathedral cave open to the abyss. The
void itself: a black column with drifting motes and a faint pale light far below, as the design
doc says, which must look vast and wrong.
Traps and dynamic set pieces
Every trap in the design doc gets a full animated set piece: the cargo container dropping
across the salon door, the Chimney's stalactites falling, the Reacher coral gate closing and
eating, the surge channel flushing the Bommie, the canal sluice flooding a terrace, the
hypocaust boiling a room (steam and bubbles), the god-pool draining and refilling, the
pressure door crushing a corridor, the reactor vent's scalding jet, and the vault ledge
collapsing into the abyss.
