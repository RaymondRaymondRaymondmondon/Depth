Red Tide — Visual Overhaul Spec
Animation
• Swimming. Underwater locomotion, not land walking: slow heavy strokes, legs kicking,
the body pitching with direction. Heavy-boot walking on the bottom where the design
doc has floors. Drifting out of a slipstream.
• Air pockets. Head and shoulders above the surface, wet suit and dripping in the air, a
different breathing rhythm.
• Combat. Each weapon class's hold, aim, fire, and reload in third person (matching first
person), melee swings that fight water resistance (slow windup, fast strike).
• Downs and revives. Downed divers drift and sink slowly, a hand pressed to the wound,
firing the Last Stand pistol. Revives show a teammate grabbing the harness and
working a valve.
• States. Netted, grabbed by tentacles, swallowed (the Goliath's Inhale), parasite
attached, stunned, poisoned (a green tint to the breath bubbles), and death.
• Quips and pings. Head turns to the teammate who is talking; a gesture accompanies
callbacks.
• Bubbles leave every helmet exhaust on each breath. Breathing speeds up when the
predator pulse rises.
First person
• The view is from inside the helmet: the brass HUD from the design doc frames the
screen, with faint glass reflections, droplets after an air pocket, and condensation
when breathing fast.
• First-person arms wear that diver's gloves and sleeves (rubber gauntlets, tarred
gloves, mismatched mitts, or the Mechanic's armoured sleeves).
• Every held item is gripped correctly with both hands where it should be: fingers
wrapped around grips and fore-ends, thumbs on hammers, a hand on the cocking lever
of spearguns.
• Looking down shows the diver's own chest gear and boots.
• Tonic drinking, charm use, buying, building, and reviving all have first-person hand
animations. Drinking a tonic underwater: the bottle feeds through a valve in the helmet.
