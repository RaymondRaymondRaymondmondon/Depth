Enemies and bosses
Each faction is a fully designed group of characters with its own materials, silhouettes per
unit type, and a look that tells the player what it is and how it fits the food web. Each boss
is the hero creature of its map and gets the most modelling time of anything in the game.
Faction design
Faction units use the shared humanoid rig (or their own rigs where they are not human).
Every unit type in a squad must be identifiable by silhouette at 30 m, because tactics
depend on spotting the Netman, the Lantern Bearer, or the Shaman first.
