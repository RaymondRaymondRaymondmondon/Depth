Red Tide — Visual Overhaul Spec
Player characters: the four divers
Each diver is a fully modelled person inside heavy, believable period diving gear, with a
silhouette that no skin can change, as the design doc requires. Gear is the realistic layer,
so helmets, suits, and fittings get gun-level care.
