Red Tide — Visual Overhaul Spec
Weapons
Red Tide's guns are invented underwater weapons, so their realism comes from
engineering logic: every gun must look like a machine someone could have built in 1910 to
fire under 30 m of water, with every part that would need to exist (gas reservoirs, seals,
valves, cartridges, cocking mechanisms) modelled and animated.
Construction standard
• Model each gun as an assembly of real parts: barrel with a bore and muzzle, receiver,
trigger and guard, sights, grips and stocks, screws and pins, and the underwater
systems: gas bottles with valves and gauges, rubber seals and O-rings at every joint,
drain ports, sealed breech covers, and lanyard rings.
• Moving parts are separate, pivoted objects: cylinders turn, hammers cock, levers and
bolts work, spears slide out, gauges' needles drop as gas is used, glass cartridges show
their remaining needles.
• True scale in centimetres, then framed by the viewmodel FOV.
• Bevels on every edge (0.5-2 mm); weighted normals; baked normal, AO, and curvature
maps from a high-poly version.
