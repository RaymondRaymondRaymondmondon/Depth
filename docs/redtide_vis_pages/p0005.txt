Red Tide — Visual Overhaul Spec
• Texture with Blender shader nodes (noise, Voronoi, musgrave, curvature-driven wear
and grime), then bake to images. No downloaded textures, purchased assets, or Al-
generated images.
• Clean UVs with consistent texel density, packed with trim sheets for architecture.
Rig with Rigify-style skeletons; animate with keyframes plus procedural shape keys.
Bake every action to the glTF before export.
• Three LODs per creature, character, and gun (the design doc's "3 per creature;
billboards past 80 m" stands), generated with decimate and checked by eye.
Raylib integration. Raylib loads glTF meshes, materials, and skeletal animations
( LoadMode1 , LoadMode1Animations ). Write custom PBR and water shaders, add GPU
skinning and instancing (one draw per species per LOD, as the budget requires), and
convert .glb to a faster engine format at build time if load times demand it.
