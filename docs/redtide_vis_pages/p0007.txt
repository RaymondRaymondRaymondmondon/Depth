Bodies and faces
• One shared humanoid rig and body (about 7 heads tall), shared with The Trawl. The
body exists under every suit so suits fold and move like clothing over a person.
• Faces are visible through helmet ports, so model them: brow, eyes with wet highlights
and blinks, nose, mouth, stubble or beards. Expressions as shape keys (neutral, strain,
fear, grin, shout). The mouth moves with voice chat and quips.
• Fogged or wet glass and reflections on the helmet port glass, so the face reads through
it without looking like an empty hole.
Suit materials and wear
• Brass and copper with verdigris collecting in seams and around rivets, polished bright
where hands touch.
• Canvas that darkens and stiffens with water, rubber with cracks at folds, leather straps
with stitching, steel with rust bloom at joints.
• Grime and blood build up through a match: blood clouds briefly from wounds and fouls
the suit, then thins.
Skins
The design doc's token skins (verdigris, red tide, bone, pearl, Atlantean) change materials
and colours only, never the silhouette. Build each as a material set on the same mesh:
verdigris (green patina, aged), red tide (deep red dyed canvas, rust-red brass), bone
(bleached ivory plates, scrimshaw), pearl (nacre plating, iridescent), and Atlantean
(bronze with gold inlay and Atlantis motifs).
