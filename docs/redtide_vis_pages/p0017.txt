Shared enemy needs. Squad formations, flanking, suppressing fire, taking cover, and
retreating as the design doc describes, with readable body language: pointing, signalling,
reloading behind cover, panicking when a beast attacks. Each faction gets bark
animations to match its barks. Enemies dropped weapons use the same gun models as
the players' guns. Faction entries (the hull breach, the cave mouth, the drop-off, the city
gate, the airlocks) get visual set-dressing so players learn to watch them.
