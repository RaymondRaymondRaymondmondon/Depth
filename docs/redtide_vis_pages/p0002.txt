Art direction
The target is "a living underwater world you could screenshot as a postcard, then get
eaten in": rich stylized-realistic creatures and places, heavy period diving gear, and guns
that look machined and real, all under water that has depth, colour, and light.
Tone by map. Red Tide runs brighter than The Trawl, but each map has its own mood, and
the mood darkens as the maps get deeper.
