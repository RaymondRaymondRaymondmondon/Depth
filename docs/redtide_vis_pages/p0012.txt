Melee weapons get the same care: the diver's knife (a stainless blade with a rubber grip
and a sheath), the Boarding Axe (a rusted steel head on a tarred haft), the Tesla Gaff (a
hook with copper coils and a battery), the Trident (a bronze trident with barbs), and the
Sawtooth (a real sawfish rostrum with teeth, bound to a handle).
Wonder weapons
Each map's wonder weapon is the most spectacular object on that map, and its look
should explain what it does.
• The Galvanic Rod (Ship). A ship's dynamo on a pole: brass housing, copper windings, a
hand crank, arcs crawling over the coils. Forged as the Lightning Keel with a ship's keel
plate and a constant crackle.
• The Resonator (Cave). A sonic cannon of tuned crystal coral and brass horns; it visibly
vibrates and rings. Forged as the Deep Bell, with a bronze bell at the muzzle.
• The Anemone Gun (Reef). A living weapon: coral and shell grown around a brass core,
with polyps pulsing in the barrel. Forged as the Garden, which blooms with colour.
• The Tide Staff (Atlantis). A bronze staff with an Atlantean crystal held in fins of gold;
water visibly swirls around it. Forged as the Sovereign's Staff with a crowned head.
• The Abyssal Lure (Void). A lantern made from an anglerfish lure and station brass, with
a cold living light inside. Forged as the Deep Call, its light shifting and pulsing like a
heartbeat.
Forge finish and skins
• The Pressure Forge finish is sea-glass: translucent frosted green-blue glass panels and
inlays replacing parts of the metal, with light glowing through. Each Forged gun gets its
new name engraved and a slightly altered shape (crushed, compressed, stronger-
looking), not only a material swap.
• Alternate ammunition shows on the gun: Galvanic (copper coils on the barrel, sparks),
Chum Rounds (red cartridges), Flare Burst (orange-tipped darts), Riptide (fins and
vents at the muzzle), Boiling (a glowing heat jacket).
