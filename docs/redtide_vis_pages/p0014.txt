Red Tide — Visual Overhaul Spec
• Surfaces: scales as a normal-map pattern sized to the species, iridescence on silver
fish, slime sheen on eels and amphibians, shark skin as fine denticles, shells with
growth rings and encrusting life, cephalopod skin with chromatophores that animate
colour and pattern.
• Each species record's 3-band colour (base, belly, accent) from the design doc drives
the material, plus a species pattern (stripes, spots, bars, mottling) authored once per
species.
• Size class sets detail: size 1-2 are clean and readable; size 3-4 add scars, parasites, and
individual markings; size 5-6 get hero-level sculpting.
• Weak points from the design doc's damage model are modelled visibly: gills that flare
open, soft pale undersides on shelled beasts, the cephalopod vent, reptile throats.
Hero species to model individually
At minimum, give individual sculpting and animation to every apex (tier 4) and every
unique map resident: the sharks (reef, hammerhead, great white, Sleeper Shark),
barracuda, morays, the grouper family, the salon's crocodile, the octopus, the orca pod
and the Matriarch's pod, mantas, whales, dolphins and seals, sea turtles, the bobbit worm,
the giant squid, the water monitor, temple kraits, the Pale Caiman, coelacanths, yeti crabs,
the Ghost Worm, the Sand Worm, the Relict, and the Alien Horror. The rest come from the
kit with species tuning.
Animation by body plan
Keep the design doc's animation rules (sine along the spine, flapping rays, undulating eels,
1K crab legs, Chains for cephalopod arms, four-limb gaits), but author them properly in
Blender as actions and blend them in-engine:
• Fish. Idle hover with fin flutter, cruise, burst swim, sharp turn, feed (strike and
swallow), flee, a hurt flinch, and death (rolling belly-up and sinking or floating by
species).
• Sharks. A slow, heavy cruise; the pectoral-fin drop and arched back as a threat display;
a jaw-protrusion bite with the eyes rolling back.
• Eels and morays. Undulation, a strike from a hole and a pull back, the jaws working
constantly.
• Rays and mantas. Wing flaps, gliding, the burrow-and-burst from sand.
• Crustaceans. 1K walking, claw threats and snaps, defensive retreat into a hole, shells
that crack.
