The Trawl — Arcade Game 2 Design Document (Draft)
• Overboard: the nearest bot throws the life ring (with its aim error), the Medic bot runs
to the rail, and the helm bot stops the screw.
• The bell: every bot moves to the nearest defensive post (cannon, gaff at the rail, rifle if
carried) and looks for the threat.
Boarders: bots with gaffs fight; others back away from the rail.
• Leak or fire: the Bosun bot, or the nearest bot if there is no Bosun, goes to it.
• Charmed or grabbed crewman: the nearest bot rings the bell, then helps.
Orders (ping wheel, aimed at a bot or at a station): take this station; follow me; hold here;
haul the net; cut the line; fire on my mark; dive (Diver-role bots only); go home (all bots
stop fishing and secure the deck).
What bots never do unless ordered: dive, use depth charges or explosive harpoons,
board derelicts or the Ghost Ship, raise the lantern, or ring the bell for no reason. Bots do
not use voice; they show short text barks ("Fish on, port!" "Water in the hold!") above their
heads and in the ping feed. A dead bot's ghost rings the bell when a threat comes within
10 m.
Kills and cooking. Bots finish catches with the priest or gaff aimed at the head (headshot
chance: Green 40%, Able 60%, Old Hand 80%) and never attempt trick shots, so a human
crew will always out-earn a bot crew on Killscore. Idle bots sluice deck blood into the offal
barrel. A bot left at an island fire tends it and pulls each fish at 1.5x, and idle bots put loose
fish in the catch crates.
