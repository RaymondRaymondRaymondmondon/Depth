--trawl-sim <nights> [crew] runs a bot crew of 1-6 (with a role per bot)
and reports quota-met rate, money per night by source (hook, net, gun, dive), deaths
by cause, and which threats arrived when.
--trawl-eco <ground> <minutes> [pattern] runs the ecosystem alone with a
scripted crew pattern (quiet, chumming, trawling, depth-charging) and prints
population curves, blood, Wake, and threat arrival times.
--trawl-fight <species> <tackle> N runs the fishing fight with a bot angler and
reports land rate, mean fight time, and line breaks.
--trawl-shots renders every species, threat, deck station, and weather to PNG.
--net-loop trawl runs a host and five bots over loopback through a scripted night.
--trawl-wreck <seed> prints a generated wreck and asserts its layout
rules.
