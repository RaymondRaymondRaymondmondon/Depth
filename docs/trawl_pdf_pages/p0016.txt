Moon. Full moon doubles plankton at the surface (more bait fish, more predators up high);

three nights of a deadline differ.
Below decks
Under the Gannet's deck is a small, dark interior, and going down there is a short facility
run. There is no lantern below, only swinging oil lamps and hand lamps. Voices from the
deck are muffled to nothing except through the engine room's speaking tube, and every
sound outside the hull is louder: a shark brushing the planks, the Drowned knocking.
