The Trawl — Arcade Game 2 Design Document (Draft)
Guiding the skiff
Out of the Gannet's light, the skiff crew sees only its own lantern radius; the sea is black.
The sonar operator sees the skiff as a bright blip with every contact around it, and
becomes the skiff's eyes, the way a Lethal Company terminal operator watches the radar.
• Walkies are the lifeline: proximity voice dies past 12 m, so a skiff crew without walkies
has only flares and the ship's bell.
• Marks the sonar operator places show as bearing arrows on the skiff crew's screens.
• The searchlight can hold the skiff in its cone for safety, but light draws the CUriOUS.
• Left behind. Anyone not aboard the Gannet when it crosses the harbour line is lost for
the night (dead, body lost, the Owners' fine).
Fishing from the skiff
The kill happens at the waterline. Anything under 30 kg can be landed in the skiff; bigger
fish are killed in the water alongside (the Long shot bonus is easy here) or tow-roped and
dragged back to the Gannet. There are no scuppers and no offal barrel: every drop of
blood goes straight into the sea, and a landed thrasher can capsize the boat.
Skiff destinations
Each ground has skiff-only fishing marks (about twice the bite rate, the ground's rarer
species, and its boss water) and landings (islands and ledges explored on foot, with
salvage caches, consignment items, harbour-request finds, and shore bait).
