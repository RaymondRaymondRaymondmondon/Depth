A watertight door joins the hold and the engine room. Shutting it contains flooding, and
can leave someone on the wrong side.
Hatches and light
• Hatches are open (fast, but they let water and things in), shut (4 s to open), or
battened (secure, IO s, and cannot be opened from below).
• Oil lamps go out past 200 of roll or when splashed; relighting takes 3 s with matches.
Below, a hand lamp is the only reliable light.
• The crew board shows who is below. Ghosts can go below and see what is there.
