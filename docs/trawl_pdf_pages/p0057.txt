The skiff
The skiff is how the crew reaches the richest water and every island worth landing on. The
Gannet is too deep, too wide, and too loud for the reef passes, the kelp lanes, the cave
galleries, and the temple shallows, so the best fishing marks, most boss water, and all
shore loot are skiff-only. Sending the skiff out is the Trawl's facility run: one or two
crewmen leave the lit deck, and the boat becomes the ship they have to get back to.

Rowing is a rhythm on the two mouse buttons; losing the rhythm "catches a crab" and
stops the boat for a second. Each stroke writes a little noise into the water.
Launch and recovery. A hand at the skiff davit lowers it in 8 s. Recovering it takes 10 s
alongside the stern with the Gannet stopped, so a skiff running home from something is a
problem for both boats.
