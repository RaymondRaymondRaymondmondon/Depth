• The boat: engine chuff by telegraph setting, boiler hiss and the relief valve's shriek, hull

• Guns: each weapon's report, muffled impacts under the surface, the harpoon's tether
whine, the depth charge's thud felt through the hull.
• Diving: the pump's rhythm heard in the helmet, the diver's breathing (faster at low air),
bubbles, hose scrape, silt, distant knocks inside the wreck.
