Junk never fights on deck, but it does take up room in the skiff and the hold, and the maps
and keys only pay off for a crew willing to row to the islands.
Birds and the catch crates
A dead fish is only safe in the sell spot: the six lidded catch crates on the Gannet's aft
deck, or the hold. Anything dead anywhere else (on the deck, on the gutting table left
unattended for 10 s, in the skiff, on a beach by a cooking fire) is fair game for birds. Birds
are sellable too, so a thief is also a second catch.
How birds work. Birds are agents in the food web's air layer. Dead fish, cooking smoke,
chum, net hauls, and gutting draw them. A bird grabs the heaviest fish it can lift and flies
off with it; shooting it down drops both the bird and the fish, wherever they fall: on deck,
on an island, or in the sea, where they bleed and have to be gaffed or netted before
something else takes them. Every bird kill is airborne, so the Airborne bonus is automatic,
which makes birds shotgun and rifle practice that pays.
