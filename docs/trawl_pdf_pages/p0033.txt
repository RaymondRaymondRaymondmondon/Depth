Carrying and ammunition. The Gunsmith sells tools for the kill, not safety. Guns make
money on deck; against most threats they only buy time (see Threats: Resistances).
• Slots. Most weapons take one of a crewman's four slots; long guns, long melee
weapons, and specials take two (the catalogue lists each), and no one carries more
than one two-slot weapon.
• Ammunition on the body is the loaded magazine plus one spare reload. Everything
else (rounds, shells, spears, flares, dynamite) lives in the magazine locker in the fo'c'sle,
so restocking is a trip below decks.
