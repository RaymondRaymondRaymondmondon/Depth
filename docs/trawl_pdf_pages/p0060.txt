Why the skiff is dangerous
The food web treats a lone skiff as the easiest meal on the water: predators weight their
target choice toward the smallest vessel with the most blood around it. A Great White
capsizes the skiff with one ram, a Siren can charm a lone rower into rowing toward her, a
grabber can take a man straight off the thwart, and the Kraken takes the whole boat.
Whoever goes out in the skiff is betting on the sonar operator, the walkie batteries, and
the crew's willingness to come and get them.
Islands: fires, traders, and treasure
The landings are why the skiff goes out. Every cooking fire is on one, seven of them have a
trader selling weapons, gear, and boat upgrades the dock never stocks, and all of them
hold treasure. Treasure and trade feed the ship's purse, never the quota.
