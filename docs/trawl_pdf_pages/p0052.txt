Most threats are worth something dead: a shark sells at 2 sh/kg, a Ghost Worm hatchling's
gill lanterns fetch 60 each, an adult's 400. The Siren, Wraith, Drowned, and Kraken leave
nothing sellable except relics on the Ghost Ship.
Resistances
Guns kill fish; they rarely solve threats. Each threat takes damage from each kind of
attack at a multiplier, and every shot still writes noise into the water, so shooting at the
wrong thing makes a bad night worse. The big three can be driven off but are rarely killed.
