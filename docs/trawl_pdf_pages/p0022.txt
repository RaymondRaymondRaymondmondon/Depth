Landing
• Fish under 5 kg: lifted over the rail.
• 5-40 kg: a second hand with the gaff or the landing net. Gaffing has a timing window as
the fish comes alongside; a miss spooks it into one more run.
40 kg and over: tail rope, then the net winch or the cannon's winch hauls it aboard, and
the whole boat lists while it comes up.
The kill
Landing a fish starts the second fight. Anything of 1 kg or more comes over the rail alive
and angry: a physics body with hit points that slides with the roll, fights by its deck
behaviour, and can flop back into the sea. The crew kills it with whatever is in hand, the
Killscore pops up over the body, and then it is gutted, cooked, or iced. Forage fish under 1
kg die on landing and skip the kill (they are bait and net filler).
Hit points = 8 + 6 x weightAO.75: a 4 kg snapper has 25, a 40 kg yellowfin 103, a 250 kg
marlin 385. A net haul lands as one pile: small fish die in it, and anything big in the pile
fights.
