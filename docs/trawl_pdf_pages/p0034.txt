The Trawl — Arcade Game 2 Design Document (Draft)
• Wet powder. Cartridge guns misfire 10% of the time in rain, 25% in a squall, and 40% in
a storm or after their carrier has been in the water, unless the ammunition rides in an
oilskin case (Chandler, 20). Dynamite fuses fail 30% of the time in heavy rain.
• Guns overboard. A dropped gun is gone. A crewman who goes overboard carrying a
long gun has 5 s less before drowning.
• Cost. Ammunition is priced so that a careless night on the chatter gun can cost more
than its kills earn; the quiet priest and the knife stay part of every good crew's kit.
The tethered harpoon. A harpoon hit makes the target a hooked fish (see Fishing) with
the cannon's winch as the reel and 120 kg steel as the line. This is how the crew lands a
Great White or holds a Ghost Worm's head up while the rifles work. A tethered giant tows
the boat; the winch can be released in an emergency.
Kills and value. How a creature dies no longer lowers its value; it raises it, through the
Killscore (see Fishing: The kill). The one exception is Overkill, where an explosive hit blows
a fish to chum. A wounded fish that escapes bleeds into the scent grid as it goes.
Noise and blood. Each shot writes its noise value into the sound layer at the muzzle and
again at the impact point. Fearful species scatter; CUriOUS species come; aggressive ones
come fast. Every wound writes blood (size x wound fraction x 2 per second, size x 20 on
death). A depth charge is a dinner bell for a whole ground: it is the fastest money in the
game and the fastest way to raise Wake (see The food web).
Friendly fire is on. A rifle round or a harpoon can hit a crewman, and a harpoon tether can
drag one over the rail.
The food web
Every ground runs one ecosystem simulation on the engine code Red Tide already has
(agents, the scent and sound grids, the species file loader). The Trawl reuses that code to
save time, not its design: the rules, numbers, species, and tuning here are written for a
boat on the surface at night. The Trawl's web has seven layers (diet, blood and scent,
sound, territory, symbiosis, flora, and the crew) plus three that exist only in this game:
light, vibration, and Wake. The crew is a species in the web, so everything they catch, spill,
and shine changes what comes next.
