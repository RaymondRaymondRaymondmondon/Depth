Flora: giant kelp (canopy shelter; blocks sonar; fouls lines, nets, and the screw), eelgrass
beds (nursery for perch and crabs), urchin barrens (bare rock where kelp has been grazed
away), drift kelp mats (float with the current; yellowtail shelter under them).
Web notes. The Weeds are the game's clearest cascade: otters eat urchins, urchins eat
kelp, kelp shelters everything else. Killing or scaring off an otter raft (gunfire near them
counts) lets urchins spread, turning a kelp patch into barrens over the three nights of a
deadline, and the bass, perch, and sheephead that lived there leave. An otter killed costs
an 80-shilling fine. Bluefin only run along the seaward edge, where the Great White
patrols.
