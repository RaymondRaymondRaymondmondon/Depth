Role upgrades. A role ranks up three times in a run: after the first, second, and fourth met
deadlines. At each rank the player picks one of two upgrades on the dock chalkboard.
Upgrades last until the run ends, survive death, and belong to the role: switching roles at
the dock starts the new role at rank 1. Bots pick from a fixed preference list per role.
