The Trawl — Arcade Game 2 Design Document (Draft)
Build order
1. The boat alone: hull physics, engine, stations, top-down deck, movement, one player.
Gate: the boat rolls, lists under weight, and sinks when flooded.
2. Lines and fishing: Verlet lines, cast, bite, fight, landing against a dummy fish. Gate: -
trawl-fight numbers in range.
3. Ecosystem port: the water column, light layer, vibration, Wake, the Lagoon's species.
: --trawl-eco lagoon curves stable with no crew.
Gate
4. The session loop: dock, Chandler, sail, clock, sell, quota. Gate: a solo night on the
Lagoon is playable end to end.
5. Networking: six players, voice, prediction for reeling. Gate: --net-loop trawl and a
six-player LAN night.
6. Shooting, the net, set gear, death and ghosts.
7. The Weeds and the Grotto with their threats.
8. Diving and wrecks.
9. Atlantis Waters, the Kraken, the Ghost Ship.
10. Tuning pass and sound.
Definition of Done
• Six players over the internet complete a deadline including a dive, a Kraken encounter,
an overboard rescue, and a death-and-ghost.
• Every threat has a distinct sprite, a sound tell, a visual tell, a sonar tell, and a counter
proven by a scripted --trawl-sim case.
• The balance targets are met or deviations are logged in the balance changelog.
• Two players plus four bots complete a deadline on the Weeds, with bots working
ordered stations and one bot taking over a dropped player's seat.
• Each role's perk is covered by a scripted --trawl-sim case (the Medic's CPR revive,
the Angler's hook-set window, the Diver's air, the Bosun's patch time).
