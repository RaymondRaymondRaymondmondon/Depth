Blood through the scuppers. Every hit on deck spills blood on the planking: 1 unit per hit, 3
for a messy hit (shotgun, SMG, explosive), halved for a headshot kill. Deck blood drains
through the scuppers into the sea at 20% a second, straight into the food web, unless a
hand sluices it into the offal barrel with the deck bucket (the barrel then becomes chum).
Gunfire on deck writes its noise into the water too. Melee kills are quiet and cheap; gun
kills score higher and cost more.
Don't shoot the deck. A missed round on deck can hit crew, and a round through the
planking below the waterline starts a small leak (-3 to that section until patched).
Protected species (turtles, otters) are never killed for money. Returning them alive within
60 s avoids the Owners' fine.
Cooking
Cooking happens ashore. The Owners forbid open flame aboard a wooden trawler full of
coal dust, so every cooking fire is on a landing, reached by skiff. A properly cooked catch
sells for up to its price, and the multiplier stacks with the Killscore, so the best money
in the game is a trick-shot kill rowed to an island and cooked to perfection. Each ground's
fires are listed under Islands.
• Getting fish ashore. The skiff carries 150 kg a trip. Fish waiting on the beach or in the
skiff are fair game for birds (see Birds and the catch crates).
• Cooking. A fish on the fire climbs from to over 10 s plus 1 s per kg. It holds at
for 5 s, then burns, falling to over the next 5 s. The sizzle rises in pitch and the
smoke turns from white to grey to black.
• Fires differ. An open fire pit is standard; a hut stove is sheltered from rain; volcanic
vents steam fish slowly but can't burn them; a brazier cooks twice as fast at a cost.
Rain puts out open fires, and relighting takes 10 s with dry kindling.
• Cooked fish keep. Cooking stops freshness loss, so cooked fish can ride back to the
Gannet's hold, or traded on the spot to the island's trader.
• Eating. A crewman who eats a cooked fish of 2 kg or more mends one minor injury (a
pinch, a bruise, a burn). Serious injuries still need the Medic.
• The smell. A cooking fish writes scent into the water and air around the island (5 a
second per fish, doubled for a burning one). Birds come first, then whatever hunts the
shallows, and a moored skiff is right in the middle of it.
