The big three
Each of the three Wake-called threats runs a short script on top of its food-web brain.
Every one of them can be escaped, survived, or killed, and each takes a different kind of
crew work. For reference, the Gannet makes 1.5 m/s at slow, 3 at half, and 4.5 at full (5.9
with the compound engine).
