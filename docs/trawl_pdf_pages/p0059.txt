On foot. Landings are small top-down pixel maps (the deck's art and movement) with
caches to search, doors to force, and carryable salvage that has to be hauled to the skiff
within its 150 kg. Each landing holds treasure, often a cooking fire and a trader with stock
sold nowhere else (see Islands), and usually the ground's consignment candidates.
