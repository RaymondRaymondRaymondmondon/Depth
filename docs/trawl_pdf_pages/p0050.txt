Wake and the big three. The Great White, the Ghost Ship, and the Kraken are not rolled
on a timer. They come when a ground's Wake (see The food web) crosses their threshold:
40 for the White, 65 for the Ghost Ship, 90 for the Kraken (Atlantis only). A careful crew
can fish a whole night in Atlantis Waters without seeing the Kraken; a crew that depth-
charges twice will see it before midnight.
Scaling by deadline. From the second deadline on, threats gain 10% hunger rate and 5%
size per deadline, and each ground adds one threat from the next ground down the list, so
the Lagoon eventually has Anglers.
Threat stats
Crew have no hit points (see Death), so a threat's effect on a crewman is an injury, a grab,
or a trip over the rail. Hull damage is to one section's integrity (0-100). HP is the threat's; a
weak point takes double damage. Values rise with the per-deadline scaling above.
