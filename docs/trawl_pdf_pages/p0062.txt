Cult Landing: obsidian dagger, cult incense, Kraken bait, cult banner, idol eye.
Treasure. Each landing holds one to three caches: some in plain sight, some locked (brass
keys from junk, lockpicks, or a crowbar), and some buried (a message in a bottle or three
chart pieces marks the spot; a shovel digs it up; a dowsing coil finds it without a map).
Caches hold coins, jewellery, gold, rare charms, and now and then a weapon. Chests are
physical and heavy, so hauling a 40 kg chest to the skiff through the dark, with the
cooking smoke still drawing birds, is its own small facility run.
Island dangers. The shallows around a landing are where things hunt a moored skiff, the
smoke from any fire draws birds and predators, and each landing has its own residents:
crabs and a moray in the Atoll's lagoon, gulls on Seal Rock, Wraiths in the cannery pilings,
the sleeping Drowned on the Smugglers' Shelf, isopods on Bone Beach, and the cult itself
at the Cult Landing.
Economy and progression
Money is shillings, kept in the ship's purse, and the quota is paid only in fish. Fish pay now
and spoil unless iced or cooked; salvage keeps and pays best on deadline day. How a fish
dies and how it is cooked decide most of what it earns.
Fish value = species base price x weight x Killscore x cooking x freshness x glut.
