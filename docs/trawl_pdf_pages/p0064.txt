The Trawl — Arcade Game 2 Design Document (Draft)
The larder. Cooked and canned fish don't spoil, so the crew can hold them in the hold
across nights: a quota reserve for a bad night, or trade goods for the next island.
The decision every night. Deliver enough to stay on pace for the quota; bank a good catch
at market when the purse needs guns or upgrades; and row spare fish, especially the
species a trader wants, to an island where they are worth more as trade than as cash. A
crew that cashes everything is always short of quota, and a crew that delivers everything
never affords the skiff's steam launch.
Salvage is sold to the Owners at a rate that rises toward the deadline, the Lethal Company
buy-back rule: 40% after night one, 70% after night two, 100% at the deadline count.
Holding salvage is a bet that the crew will survive to sell it. Salvage never counts toward
the quota; it pays shillings into the ship's purse.
The Chandler. Consumables and hand gear, bought at the dock. Rods are priced in the
Tackle table, guns and attachments at the Gunsmith (see Weapons), and boss lures with
the mini-bosses. The Gannet starts every run with two handlines, one light rod, two gaffs,
a fish priest, two knives, a deck bucket, one life ring, a patch kit, and 20 kg of ice.
