Flora: sunken gardens on the terraces (shelter the grouper and coelacanth), deep coral
(the crabs' habitat), the Eye's bioluminescent veils (drifting lights that rise through the
night and draw lantern fish, and so everything above them).
Web notes. Atlantis has the longest chain in the game: lantern fish rise to the veils, squid
rise to the lantern fish, tuna and swordfish rise to the squid, marlin hunt the tuna, and the
Kraken takes whatever is largest. The sperm whale is a tell, not a catch: its clicks on the
sonar mean a giant squid is below and the whale is hunting it, which briefly makes the
deep safer for divers. Jumbo squid packs strip hooked fish and bite divers.
Mini-bosses, boss lures, and harbour requests
Every ground has two mini-bosses that only a boss lure calls up, as in How to Fish. Boss
lures are separate items from ordinary lures and cost much more. A boss lure only works
over boss water, which lies at the ground's skiff-only fishing marks (see The skiff) and
shows on the sonar as slow rings. Casting one adds 10 to the ground's Wake, so a boss
fight is also an invitation to the ground's apex.
