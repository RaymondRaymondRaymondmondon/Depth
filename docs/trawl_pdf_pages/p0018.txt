The rule for all of it: every trip below takes a crewman out of the light and out of earshot.
Someone has to go for ammunition when the White comes, for ice when the hold is full,
and for coal when the boiler drops, and the crew above has to remember they are there.
Fishing
Fishing is a physics fight between a player, a rod, a line, and an animal that is also an agent
in the food web. The Ul shows only what an angler would feel: rod bend, line angle, reel
sound, and a tension gauge on the reel. There is no minigame bar.
