Suggested six-hand watch. Helm and sonar (one person, swapping), boiler and pumps,
two rods, net and gutting, lantern and cannon. When a dive goes down, one rod hand
moves to the air pump. When something big comes, the rod hands drop everything for the
cannon and the gaffs.
Moving on the deck. Top-down, 8-way movement at 3 m/s, slower carrying (a 40 kg tuna
halves speed; two hands carrying one halves it less). Ladders to the wheelhouse roof, the
mast, and the engine room. The deck is wet: at more than 120 of roll, unbraced players
slide; at 250 they fall. Holding a rail or a station braces.
