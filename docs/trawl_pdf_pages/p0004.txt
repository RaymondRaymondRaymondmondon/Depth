5. Run for harbor. The crew decides when to leave. The return takes 45-90 s depending
on distance and steam. The Gannet must cross the harbor line before 05:00; after that,
the Owners' customs cutter seizes the hold and fines 10% of money. There is always a
reason to stay ten more minutes.
6. Sell. Fish are delivered to the Owners/ quota scales, sold at the Fish Market for the
ship's purse, or kept aboard for trading and the larder (see Economy: Three ways to use
a fish). Salvage goes to the Owners (paid at a rate that rises toward the deadline, see
Economy). Only fish delivered to the Owners' scales count toward the quota;
everything sold goes into the ship's purse to spend on gear.
7. Repeat for nights two and three. After the third night's sale, the Owners count the
quota.
Quota. Starts at 400 shillings for six players (scaled solo, for two, for four;
bots count as crew). After each met deadline it rises by 40% plus 60 shillings, so by the
fourth deadline the crew must work Atlantis Waters or dive for relics to keep up. Fish
delivered past the quota carry over as credit toward the next deadline at half their value;
shillings in the ship's purse never pay the quota.
Owners' consignments. Each deadline the telegraph also names one salvage item the
Owners want delivered to their office window at the deadline count. The consignment is a
separate demand from the quota, and it is what forces the crew to dive and to take the
skiff to the landings.
• Where it is. The telegraph names the item and the ground, with a one-line clue
("STRONGBOX LAST SEEN ABOARD SLOOP MARY ANNE STOP"). The item is in one of
that ground's wrecks or landings. Deadline 1 names the Lagoon or the Weeds, deadline
2 the Weeds or the Grotto, deadline 3 the Grotto or Atlantis, and deadline 4 onward
Atlantis.
• What it pays. No shillings. The crew picks one: a free Slipway upgrade worth up to 600,
or 10% off the next quota.
• Missing it. The run continues, but the Owners raise the next quota by 25% and
repossess the Gannet's most expensive upgrade. Missing two consignments in a row
ends the run.
• Keeping it. Once aboard, it sits in the hold. The Drowned and the Ghost Ship's
boarders go for it first.
