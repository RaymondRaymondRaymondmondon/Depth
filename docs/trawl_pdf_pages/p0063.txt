• Cooking is up to at an island fire, if burnt (see Fishing: Cooking).
• Freshness starts at 100% and falls 1% per real minute on deck, 0.2% per minute gutted
and iced. Iced fish kept overnight lose 25%; un-iced raw fish rot and sell for nothing.
Cooked fish do not lose freshness.
• Glut. Each species' price at the Fish Market drops 3% for every 10 kg of it sold that
deadline, so a hold full of one species is not a strategy at market. The Owners/ quota
scales ignore glut.
• Rare variants. About 1 catch in 60 is a Glimmer variant (a pale, shining colouring). It
sells at the normal price but fills its own Catch Log slot, and it can be fed to the Pier
Wheel on the dock, a penny machine that pays out cosmetics (lantern colours, boat
liveries, weapon finishes).
Three ways to use a fish
Every fish the crew keeps ends up in one of three places, and choosing well is a core skill.
Only fish delivered to the Owners count toward the quota; nothing else does.
