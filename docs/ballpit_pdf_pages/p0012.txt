Capture the flag details
• Each flag sits on the roof deck of its team's tower, so attackers must climb all the way
up.
• The carrier can use the knife and starter blaster only (no ball throwing, no bought gun),
and moves at 90% speed.
• Carriers can ride slides. Sliding down with the flag is the fastest escape, but the slide
exit is a known spot to wait at.
A dropped flag returns home after 20 s, or immediately when a defender touches it.
Disarm the bomb details
• Each round, the attackers carry one foam "bomb" (a big, obvious prop). There are two
plant sites inside the defenders' tower: one on Level 2, one on Level 3.
• Planting takes 4 s; the bomb then goes off after 40 s. Disarming takes 6 s, or 3 s with a
disarm kit from the store (200 score).
• Rounds last 2 min before the plant. Attackers win by detonation or by knocking out
every defender; defenders win by disarming, running out the clock, or knocking out
every attacker before a plant.
• Score carries between rounds, so buying is part of the strategy.
