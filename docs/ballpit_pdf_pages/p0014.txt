Open questions
Each has a working default in the spec; confirm or change it before the matching
milestone.
Cannon balance: cannons are the fastest-firing weapons and every ball is a one-hit
knockout. The spec limits them with a fixed firing arc, an exposed operator,
overheating, and the shared ball supply. Is that enough, or should cannon balls do
damage instead of instant knockouts?
Ball speed: the spec makes balls faster than darts, as described, including thrown
balls. Should thrown balls be slower than cannon balls but still faster than darts
(current default)?
Dodgeball bounce rule: balls stop being deadly after one bounce. Keep it, or let
bounced balls still knock players out?
Players: bots first, then online? Default: bots first.
Knife role: the spec makes the knife a melee weapon with backstab knockouts. Did
you mean something more specific by knives being for attacking the enemy base?
Streak thresholds: should streaks count only knockouts instead of all score? Counting
objective score (default) rewards flag runners and bomb planters too.
