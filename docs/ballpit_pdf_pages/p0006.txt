Ball Pit Brawl — Game Design Spec
• A ball is only deadly until its first bounce, like dodgeball. After that it's just a ball on the
floor (and heads for the conveyor).
• Friendly fire is off in team modes.
Foam knife (always carried)
• 50 damage per swing, 0.6 s between swings, 2 m reach. Two hits knock out a full-health
player.
A hit from behind is an instant knockout.
• The only attack that works inside crawl tunnels.
Thrown balls
• Pick up a ball by walking over it or reaching into a ball pit (0.4 s). You can carry one ball
at a time.
• Holding a ball, your gun is lowered. Throwing has a 0.3 s windup, then the ball flies at 25
m/s with light drop.
Any player can grab a ball anywhere: ball pits are full of them, which is what makes the
ground floor dangerous.
