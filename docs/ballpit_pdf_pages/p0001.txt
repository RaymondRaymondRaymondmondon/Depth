Overview
— Game Design Spec
Ball Pit Brawl is a foam-weapon shooter set inside a giant multistory indoor play center:
players climb nets, ride tube slides, and wade through ball pits while fighting with foam
knives, foam guns, and plastic balls that knock a player out in one hit.
Core loop: spawn with a foam knife and a single-shot foam gun climb toward the enemy
earn score from kills and objectives spend score on better foam guns and darts
fight over the ball cannons, the most powerful weapons in the arena.
Design pillars
• The playground is the battlefield. Every piece of play equipment is also a route or a
piece of cover: nets you can see through but not shoot through, slides that are fast but
trap you, ball pits that hide you but slow you down.
• Balls are deadly, darts are reliable. Any live ball is a one-hit knockout, so balls are the
high-risk, high-reward weapon. Darts do steady damage and never run out if you keep
vacuuming.
• One shared ball supply. Every ball in the arena cycles through one conveyor system
that feeds every cannon. Grabbing balls to throw, or draining the conveyor by firing a
cannon, takes them away from everyone else.
• Earn your arsenal. Everyone starts equal; score buys better guns mid-match.
Camera: first person. Players see their foam gun or knife in hand, and climbing, sliding, and
wading through ball pits are all shown from the player's eyes.
Setting and arena
The whole match takes place inside one huge indoor play structure, a steel frame of
padded decks, nets, tube slides, and ball pits four levels high, inside a warehouse-sized
building.
