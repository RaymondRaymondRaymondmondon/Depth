Weapons
Every player starts with a foam knife and a single-shot foam gun; better guns are bought
with score, and any live ball knocks a player out in one hit.
Damage model
• Players have 100 health. Health regenerates after 4 s without taking damage.
• Foam darts are slow (15 to 30 m/s) and drop over distance, so long shots need leading.
• Balls are faster and flatter than darts (25 m/s thrown, 40 m/s from a cannon) and are an
instant knockout.
