Rules that keep them harmless: no joke item changes hitbox size, speed, damage, or what
enemies can see through. Sounds from joke items are heard only by teammates. Each
item has a IO s cooldown so nobody spams it.
Streak rewards
Staying alive pays: score earned without being knocked out builds a streak, and hitting a
streak threshold unlocks a reward. There are six rewards, and each player picks three
before the match.
How streaks work
• Streak points are the score you've earned since your last knockout (kills, assists,
objectives). Being knocked out resets them to zero.
Spending score in the store does not lower your streak.
• Each of your three rewards unlocks once per life when your streak reaches its
threshold. Press the reward key to use it whenever you like; an unused reward is lost if
you're knocked out.
