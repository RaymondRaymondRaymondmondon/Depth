Ball Pit Brawl — Game Design Spec
Balls that land on decks or floors roll into gutters and ride the conveyor back to the
cannons; balls that land in a ball pit stay there until a player grabs one to throw.
Cannons
• Six cannons: two in each team tower on Level 3 aimed across the atrium, and two
neutral cannons on the central roof deck. Any player can use any cannon.
• Fire rate 6 balls per second at 40 m/s, nearly flat. Every ball is a one-hit knockout until
its first bounce.
• Turning limits: 1200 left to right and 400 up and down, so each cannon has blind spots
players can use.
• Getting on takes 0.5 s; getting off is instant. The operator sits exposed from behind and
above.
• Overheats after 3 s of continuous fire and needs 2 s to cool down.
The conveyor
• Every deck and floor slopes slightly toward gutters at its edges. Gutters drop onto
conveyor belts running along the ground floor.
• The belts carry balls to a hub, where air pipes (like the overhead pipes in the reference
image) lift them up to the cannon hoppers. The lift always refills the hopper with the
fewest balls first.
• A ball takes about 8 s to get from a gutter to a hopper. Each hopper holds 40 balls and
starts the match full.
• Balls dropped by a knocked-out player, and missed throws that land on decks, also go
back into the conveyor.
• One shared supply means a team firing a cannon nonstop drains the balls everyone
else's cannons depend on, and a team that keeps balls in its hands is starving the
cannons.
Score, store, and ammo
Score is both the leaderboard and the currency: you earn it by knocking out enemies and
playing the objective, and spend it at your team's store on guns and darts. Spending
doesn't lower your leaderboard total.
