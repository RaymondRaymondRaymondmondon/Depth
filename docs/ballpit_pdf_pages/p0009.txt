One store in each team's tower (both open to everyone in free for all). It's a counter
you walk up to; buying is instant from a quick menu.
• Sells the foam guns in the weapons table, plus darts: 12 darts for 50 score.
• The store area is a safe zone for 3 s after you arrive, so players aren't knocked out mid-
menu.
VaCUUm reloading (free)
• Every player has a handheld vacuum. Hold the vacuum button to suck up foam darts
lying within a 4 m cone in front of you, at 10 darts per second.
• You can keep moving at 70% speed while vacuuming, but can't fire. Release to fire
immediately.
• Fired darts stay on the floor for 60 s. Enemy darts work too, so fights leave free ammo
behind for the winner.
• The vacuum only collects darts. Balls are picked up by hand or carried by the conveyor.
• Dart reserve: start with 12, carry up to 60.
Joke shop
Every store has a joke shelf of very cheap items that change nothing about the fight. They
exist to make teammates laugh.
