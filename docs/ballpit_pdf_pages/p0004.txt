Netted walls are the main way players cross the big open spaces safely: routes along the
atrium edge are netted, while the fastest routes (bridges, open decks) are exposed.
Movement and traversal
Getting to the enemy means climbing up and sliding down: the fast routes are exposed or
one-way, and the safe routes are slow.
