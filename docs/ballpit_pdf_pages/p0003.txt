Ball Pit Brawl — Game Design Spec
The first image sets the look of each structure (colored pipe frame, padded floors, spiral
and tube slides, a ball pit at the base). The second sets the scale and the open atrium:
wide drops between netted decks, ball pits below, and the overhead pipes that inspired
the ball conveyor.
Layout (default map, 60 m x 30 m hall)
• Two team towers, one at each end of the hall, each holding that team's base, spawn,
and store. In free for all, both stores are open to everyone and spawns are spread
across the map.
• A central atrium between the towers, open from floor to ceiling, crossed by rope
bridges, net tunnels, and tube slides. This is the long-range danger zone.
• Mirror-symmetric, so neither team has a better side.
