Flora: giant kelp (canopy shelter; blocks sonar; fouls lines, nets and the screw), eelgrass beds (nursery for perch
and crabs), urchin barrens (bare rock where kelp was grazed away), drift kelp mats (float with the current;
yellowtail shelter under them).

Web notes: the clearest cascade: otters eat urchins, urchins eat kelp, kelp shelters everything else. Killing or
scaring off an otter raft (gunfire near them counts) lets urchins spread: a kelp patch turns to barrens over the three
nights of a deadline and the bass, perch and sheephead leave. An otter killed costs an 80-shilling fine. Bluefin only
run along the seaward edge, where the Great White patrols.

Ground (page 38): kelp forest, golden canopy mats, clear lanes; everything big at the edges. Signature: running the
engine through the canopy wraps the screw (speed halves until a hand cuts it free from the stern ladder, in the
water); nets fill with kelp (weighs like fish, worth nothing); hooked fish run into the canopy to chafe the line.
Threats: Sirens (sing from the lanes, pull the helm toward the rocks), Kelp Wraiths (drift unseen in the canopy,
entangle a hand at the rail), a Great White on the seaward edge, Feral Mermen who cut nets.
Skiff marks: the Inner Lanes (bass, sheephead; Kelp King boss water), the Otter Raft (abalone dives), the Seaward
Rocks (yellowtail; Gold Tail boss water). Landings: Seal Rock (sealers' hut stove; Old Hoskins pays double for
birds; caches 100-350), the Cannery Pier (cannery boiler; the last foreman takes cooked and canned fish at 150%;
safe 150-350; Wraiths in the pilings).
Mini-bosses: the Kelp King (giant sea bass, 250 kg, Thrasher; dives into the canopy; 600; a kelp crown; lure 120),
Gold Tail (yellowtail school leader, 30 kg, Flopper; jumps every 4 s; 400; a golden scale; lure 120).
